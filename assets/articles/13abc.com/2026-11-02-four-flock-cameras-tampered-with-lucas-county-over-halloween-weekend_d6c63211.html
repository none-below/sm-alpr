Four Flock cameras tampered with in Lucas County over Halloween weekend

By WTVG Staff
Published: Nov. 2, 2026 at 10:26 PM UTC
Add Us On GoogleAdd as a preferred source on Google

TOLEDO, Ohio (WTVG) - The Lucas County Sheriff’s Office is investigating a series of crimes over the weekend in which people tampered with Flock cameras.

Four cameras in total were damaged or moved.

RELATED: Multiple Flock cameras vandalized in Toledo area, police say

Two cameras were turned away from the road. One was at Hill and Derbyshire, and the other was at Old State Line Road and Angola.

One camera was cut down from its pole at Angola and Kieswetter.

One camera was taken down by its bolts on Dorr near McCord Road. Only the pole remained at the Dorr Street location Sunday.

The two cameras that were turned are facing the road again.

The two cameras that were taken down are in the possession of the sheriff’s office.

Investigators said this is the most Flock camera tampering they have seen in a single weekend.

RELATED: Sixth Flock camera found cut down in Toledo

The incidents are under investigation. No potential suspects have been publicly identified.
