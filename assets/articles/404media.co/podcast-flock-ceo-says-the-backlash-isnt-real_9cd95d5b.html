Podcast: Flock CEO Says the Backlash Isn’t Real

Joseph Cox
· Oct 29, 2026 at 1:34 PM
Leaked audio of Flock's CEO, why Marisa Mayer can't keep pretending, and a CashApp nightmare.

We start this week with Jason and Joseph’s article about leaked audio from a private police conference. In that audio, Flock’s CEO made all sorts of wild claims about the backlash to his company not being real, and his wife’s advice to never apologize. After the break, Sam tells us why former Yahoo CEO Marrisa Mayer can’t keep pretending ‘gender doesn’t matter.’ In the subscribers-only section, Joseph tells us about a woman who is facing years in prison because someone else opened a CashApp account in her name.
