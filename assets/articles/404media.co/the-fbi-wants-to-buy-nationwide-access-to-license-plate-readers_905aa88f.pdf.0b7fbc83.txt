PHOTO BY DAVID TRINKS ON UNSPLASH
The FBI wants to buy access to automated license plate readers (ALPRs)
nationwide, which would likely allow the agency to track the movements of
vehicles—and by extension people—across the country without a warrant,
according to FBI procurement records reviewed by 404 Media.
The documents show that ALPRs continue to be a sought-after tool for law
enforcement, not just for local police and individual communities, but federal
agencies too. The news also comes as protests and pushback against ALPRs
have spread around the country.
