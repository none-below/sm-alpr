A surveillance company plans to add sensors to automatic license plate readers
(ALPRs) that would mean the devices, as well as capture the license plate of
passing vehicles, would also sweep up unique identifiers of mobile phones,
wearables, and other Bluetooth-enabled devices in those cars, potentially letting
law enforcement identify specific drivers or passengers.
The technology, called SignalTrace, would turn ALPR cameras from devices
focused on tracking cars to ones that can more readily track the location of
particular people. ALPR cameras have become a commonly deployed
technology all across the U.S.; SignalTrace would make some of those cameras
capable of collecting much more data.
