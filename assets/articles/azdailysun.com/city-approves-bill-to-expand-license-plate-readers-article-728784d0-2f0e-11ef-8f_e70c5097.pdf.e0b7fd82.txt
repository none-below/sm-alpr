City approves bill to expand license plate readers
By Spencer Pauley The Center Square
Jun 20, 2024
License plate reader.
palmspringsca.gov
Seattle City Council has approved a bill to expand Seattle Police
Department’s use of license plate reading technology with amendments
addressing privacy concerns.
Council Bill 120778 expands the use of Automated License Plate Reader, or
ALPR, technology to 360 SPD vehicles, including six patrol boats and
roughly 270 marked patrol cars.
