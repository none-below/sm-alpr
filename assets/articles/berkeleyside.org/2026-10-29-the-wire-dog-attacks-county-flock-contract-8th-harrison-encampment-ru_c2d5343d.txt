In a 2-1 decision, the 9th U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals upheld a federal judge’s orders that the city can continue removing homeless people from the encampment at Eighth and Harrison streets but must provide them with accommodations, such as a new tent if an old one is destroyed, and ruled that disability accommodation requirements should remain in place for now. ( SF Chronicle , Berkeley Scanner ) Police are investigating after a spate of dog attacks in Berkeley and Oakland that some people say stem from encounters with a pair of two Cane Corsos and their owner. ( Daily Cal , SFist , ABC7 ) Alameda County supervisors approved a 10-year contract for two drone docking stations for the Alameda County Sheriff’s Office. Docking stations aim to speed up emergency responses, but privacy advocates warn of potential surveillance overreach. ( Mercury News ) Actor Patrick Stewart , who died Wednesday, performed in Harold Pinter’s “No Man’s Land” at the Berkeley Rep in 2013 alongside Ian McKellen. ( SF Chronicle ) Berkeley’s public financing program has given city council candidates more than $400,000 this election cycle. ( Daily Cal ) A UC Berkeley grad student was paid $250 to make a political ad in support of Proposition 40, as supporters of the billionaire tax rely on thrifty methods like hiring influencers to counter the message of their deep-pocketed opponents. ( New York Times ) The high cost of housing has driven the all-in price of attending UC Berkeley to above $54,674 this year for new in-state students living in a campus residence hall.
