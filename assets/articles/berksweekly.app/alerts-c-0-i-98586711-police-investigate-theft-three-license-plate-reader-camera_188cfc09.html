Police investigate theft of three license plate reader cameras from Tilden Township Lowe’s

Alert issued: Tuesday, November 3 2026 - 06:16 PM

Via Tilden Township Police: 11/03/2026 On 11/01/2026, our department was contacted by the Tilden Lowe’s that unknown person(s) had cut down and removed three Flock aLPR cameras from the Lowe’s property. The criminal mischief and theft happened sometime overnight between 10/31/2026 and 11/01/2026.

The Berks County Detectives Forensic Services Unit (FSU) responded to the scene to assist in processing evidence.

This is an active investigation.

If anyone has information on this incident, please contact the Tilden Township Police Department at (610) 562-9001, crimetips@tildentownship.com or anonymously for potential cash to Crime Alert Berks at (877) 373-9913.



*Information above is based on preliminary reports and may change as the situation develops.
