Most popular browsers support the following keyboard shortcuts for increasing/decreasing the magnification level:

Windows:

  • Press the Ctrl and the + (plus) keys together to increase.
  • Press the Ctrl and the – (minus) keys together to decrease.
  • Press the Ctrl and the 0 (zero) keys together to reset the size.

Mac:

  • Press the Cmd and the + (plus) keys together to increase the size of the entire page content. Hold down the ⌥ (option) key as well to increase the font size alone.
  • Press the Cmd and the – (minus) keys together to decrease the size of the entire page content. Hold down the ⌥ (option) key as well to decrease the font size alone.
  • Press the Cmd and the 0 (zero) keys together to reset all content to its default size (normally 100%).

RE: Chestnut Hill Realty plans to install AI-powered license plate reader cameras in South Brookline

by David ButcherOctober 14, 2025October 14, 2025

The fact that this was passed 5-0 before any guardrails could be put in place or without comment from the citizens is extremely concerning. First, describing these as just “license plate readers” is disingenuous and downplays the capabilities and dangers associated with these. These are AI-enhanced surveillance cameras that capture both still and video footage that is searchable for things far beyond just license plate numbers. This opens up many more paths of abuse than just a license plate reader. It is also noted in the article that there “is no cost to the town.” The memorandum states that there is a 12-month free trial of the premium service at which point it gets downgraded. This creates a clear path for Flock to begin contracts directly with the city. We all want Brookline to continue being one of the safest places to live. But we must always weigh the cost of any new safety technology on the freedoms and liberties of the 99% of citizens that are not committing crimes. There are too many examples of this technology being abused. ICE raids, abortion patient tracking, law enforcement officers tracking people for personal purposes…
