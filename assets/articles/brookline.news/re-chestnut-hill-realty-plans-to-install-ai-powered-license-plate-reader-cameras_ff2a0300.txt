First, describing these as just “license plate readers” is disingenuous and downplays the capabilities and dangers associated with these. These are AI-enhanced surveillance cameras that capture both still and video footage that is searchable for things far beyond just license plate numbers. This opens up many more paths of abuse than just a license plate reader. It is also noted in the article that there “is no cost to the town.” The memorandum states that there is a 12-month free trial of the premium service at which point it gets downgraded. This creates a clear path for Flock to begin contracts directly with the city. We all want Brookline to continue being one of the safest places to live. But we must always weigh the cost of any new safety technology on the freedoms and liberties of the 99% of citizens that are not committing crimes. There are too many examples of this technology being abused.
