(John Benson/cleveland.com) John Benson/cleveland.com Add us on Google By John Benson, special to cleveland.com OLMSTED FALLS, Ohio -- Two years after adding license plate reader (LPR) cameras throughout the community, the Olmsted Falls police department recently added an additional Flock unit at the Bagley Road and Brentwood Drive intersection. “We’re finding that we are getting consistent hits for various things -- and it’s not just warrants, suspended licenses and the historically common law-enforcement things,” Olmsted Falls Police Chief Odis B. Rogers said. “It’s also Amber Alerts, Silver Alerts. “We used it once to help locate a missing and endangered adult elderly female. We were able to track through the Flock system out on the far east side. So these cameras have been a really good tool for us. I’m appreciative of City Council and the mayor for getting these cameras into our city.” Individually a $3,000 annual cost, Flock cameras are also located at the intersections of Columbia and Cook roads, Bagley and Lewis roads and Columbia and Sprague roads. Recently, the latter Flock camera notified the police department that a wanted person was driving north on Columbia Road from Sprague Road. “An officer started heading that way, located the vehicle and made the traffic stop,” he said. “The officer discovered that the driver was the wanted person and made the arrest. “During the subsequent inventory search of the vehicle, they located a loaded gun, multiple magazines, ammo and two different containers of suspected drugs.” The police chief stressed Flock cameras read vehicle colors, types and license plates.
