Evanston Deactivates Flock Cameras and
Terminates Contract
On August 26, the City of Evanston and the Evanston Police Department
deactivated all 19 Flock Safety ALPR cameras. The City is no longer
collecting or providing license plate reader data to the Flock network.
The findings of the Illinois Secretary of State’s audit, combined with Flock’s
admission that it failed to establish distinct permissions and protocols to
ensure local compliance while running a pilot program with federal users,
are deeply troubling. As a result, the City has deactivated the cameras and
issued a termination notice to Flock, effective September 26, 2025.
During this interim period until September 26, data collected by other
agencies and shared on the Flock network will remain available for
Evanston’s investigations into reported crimes. However, Evanston will not
collect or share any data itself.
