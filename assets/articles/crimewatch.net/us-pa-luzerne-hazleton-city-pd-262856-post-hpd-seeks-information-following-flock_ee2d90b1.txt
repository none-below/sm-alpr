HPD Seeks Information Following Flock Camera Damage 🚨 COMMUNITY ASSISTANCE REQUESTED 🚨 The Hazleton City Police Department is asking for the community’s assistance following an act of vandalism involving the City’s Flock Camera system. Throughout the evening of October 31, 2026, multiple Flock camera locations throughout Hazleton were vandalized, resulting in damage to the system. We are asking anyone who may have witnessed suspicious activity or has information regarding this incident to please come forward. Anyone with information is encouraged to submit an anonymous tip through our Crimewatch page. Your information could help us identify those responsible. We appreciate the continued support and assistance of our community!
