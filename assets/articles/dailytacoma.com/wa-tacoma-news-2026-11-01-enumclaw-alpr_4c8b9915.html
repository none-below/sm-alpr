Enumclaw police search for three suspects who cut down two license plate reader cameras

6The Daily Tacoma teamFrom Sunday’s edition · 1 min read · 2 sources

ShareEmailFacebookX

If you live in Enumclaw, police want to hear from you. Three people in an early-2000s white, short-body cargo van cut down two automated license plate readers around 12:55 a.m. Saturday, Oct. 31, the Enumclaw Police Department said. They left the camera equipment behind.

Investigators recovered surveillance footage of the suspects and van, plus soil impressions and an item that may hold fingerprints or DNA. Cutting down the cameras is often called “deflocking,” and the Courier-Herald notes eight cameras were damaged in Covington last August.

“People may disagree about the use of ALPR technology. That disagreement does not authorize someone with criminal intent to destroy taxpayer-funded, city-owned property.”

Enumclaw Police Department
  • Charge: first-degree malicious mischief, a Class B felony
  • Penalty: up to 10 years, a $20,000 fine, or both
  • Tips: call 360-825-3505 and cite Case No. 26-5044

Sources: Courier-Herald, KOMO
