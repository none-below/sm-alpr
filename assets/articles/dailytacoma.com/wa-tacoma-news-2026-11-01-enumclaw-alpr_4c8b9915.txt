Three people in an early-2000s white, short-body cargo van cut down two automated license plate readers around 12:55 a.m. Saturday, Oct. 31, the Enumclaw Police Department said. They left the camera equipment behind. Two cameras, three suspects, one overnight cut 2 license plate readers cut down 3 suspects in a white cargo van 12:55 a.m. when police say it happened Sources: Courier-Herald , KOMO Investigators recovered surveillance footage of the suspects and van, plus soil impressions and an item that may hold fingerprints or DNA. Cutting down the cameras is often called “ deflocking ,” and the Courier-Herald notes eight cameras were damaged in Covington last August. “ People may disagree about the use of ALPR technology. That disagreement does not authorize someone with criminal intent to destroy taxpayer-funded, city-owned property. ” Enumclaw Police Department Charge: first-degree malicious mischief, a Class B felony Penalty: up to 10 years, a $20,000 fine, or both Tips: call 360-825-3505 and cite Case No. 26-5044 Police are working several leads in the camera case Evidence Status Surveillance footage Recovered Soil impressions Recovered Possible fingerprint or DNA item Going to State Patrol crime lab Cell-site location records Warrants planned Sources: Courier-Herald , KOMO Sources: Courier-Herald , KOMO Read it in the full edition → ⚡️ Sunday’s edition ’s TLDR 1 Puyallup wins the 4A SPSL title 31-24 in overtime 2 Whitworth scores the last 13 points to beat Pacific Lutheran 3 Prosser Piano & Organ closes after 71 years 4 Enumclaw police seek three suspects in license plate reader case 5 A Chase Sapphire Lounge is coming to Sea-Tac Read the full edition → Start your morning the right way.
