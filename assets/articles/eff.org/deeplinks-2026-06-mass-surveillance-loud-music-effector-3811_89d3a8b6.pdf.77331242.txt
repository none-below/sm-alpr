🔊 Mass Surveillance for… Loud Music? |
EFFector 38.11
JUNE 10, 2026
eff.org
Across the country, surveillance companies have spun a vast web of tens of thousands of
license plate cameras. The people selling this tech want you to believe that it's for your
safety, but how are authorities really using automated license plate readers (ALPR)? In this
week's EFFector newsletter, we're looking at how these powerful surveillance networks have
become universal people-trackers used for noise complaints and other low-level
investigations.
