Your location isn't just a pin on a map—it can expose some of the most
intimate details about your life. The value of this information to advertisers and
others has turned the location data business into a multi-billion dollar
industry. In our latest EFFector newsletter, we're covering a new EFF
report on how ad libraries encourage apps to leak user location data—
potentially without app developers themselves even realizing it.
