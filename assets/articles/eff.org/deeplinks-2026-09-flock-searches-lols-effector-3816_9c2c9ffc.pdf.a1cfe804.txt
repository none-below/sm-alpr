👮 Flock Searches for the LOLs |
EFFector 38.16
SEPTEMBER 16, 2026
eff.org
Mass surveillance isn't a joke. But police are treating it like one when using
automated license plate reader (ALPR) networks. In our latest EFFector
newsletter, we're covering a new EFF report on how officers across the country
are routinely logging completely nonsensical "reasons" for their Flock
searches, including "LOL," "LMAO," and even (yuck) "Sexy."
