Pueblo City Council approves CATPA
grant, but ALPR protests won't stop
Updated Sept. 18, 2026, 4:57 p.m. ET
While the Pueblo Police Department welcomed Pueblo City Council's unanimous
acceptance of a state grant funding agreement to crack down on motor vehicle theft, local
protests of automated license plate readers (ALPRs) are set to continue in Pueblo.
