Sedro-Woolley turns off law enforcement cameras while
it seeks court ruling
VINCE RICHARDSON and MILO WHITMAN Skagit Valley Herald
Oct 24, 2025
This photo provided by Flock Safety shows what the company’s cameras look like.
Flock Safety photo
The city of Sedro-Woolley is no longer using cameras that read license plates
while it seeks a court ruling on whether images recorded by the cameras are
considered public records.
Police Chief Dan McIlraith said the seven Flock Safety cameras that went live in
Sedro-Woolley in March were disabled in June.
