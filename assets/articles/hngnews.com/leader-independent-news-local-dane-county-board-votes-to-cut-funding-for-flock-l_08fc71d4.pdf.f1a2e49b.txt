The Dane County Board has voted to remove funding from the Sheriff’s Office for
use of its Flock Safety automated license plate reader system (ALPR) and prohibit
further spending on the system.
According to a Dane County Board news release, on April 16, the Board voted to
amend the 2026 county budget — which had included $80,000 for the annual
Flock subscription — to remove that funding. The current subscription runs
through May 31.
