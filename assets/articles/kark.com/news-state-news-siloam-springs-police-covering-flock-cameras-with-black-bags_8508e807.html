Siloam Springs police covering Flock cameras with black bags

by: Edward McKinnon

Posted: Oct 23, 2026 / 04:42 PM CDT

Updated: Oct 23, 2026 / 04:51 PM CDT

by: Edward McKinnon

Posted: Oct 23, 2026 / 04:42 PM CDT

Updated: Oct 23, 2026 / 04:51 PM CDT

SHARE

SILOAM SPRINGS, Ark. (KNWA/KFTA) — The city of Siloam Springs has covered all of its license plate readers with black bags after the city’s board of directors voted earlier this week to turn them off.

The Siloam Springs Board of Directors voted 6-1 on Tuesday to turn off the city’s six automated license plate readers operated by the company Flock Safety. The decision comes as the city considers its existing contract with the manufacturer.

The following day, the Siloam Springs Police Department began working with Flock Safety to disable the cameras, according to an Oct. 23 statement from Interim Chief Chase Fine.

Huntsville officer, K9 under evaluation after incident at softball game

Later that day, the department decided to cover the cameras with black bags as a preemptive measure, ensuring no more photos could be uploaded into the system. On Friday, crews covered the final camera with assistance from the city’s electric department, Fine wrote.

“As always, the Siloam Springs Police Department will continue to thoroughly investigate crimes using the wide range of tools, training, and investigative techniques already in place,” Fine said in the statement. “Your officers regularly rely on witness statements, physical evidence, patrol strategies, and strong partnerships with surrounding agencies. These proven methods will continue to support our efforts to identify suspects and reduce crime.”

Fine added that the department will provide additional updates once a final decision is made on the Flock Safety contract.
