CORALVILLE, Iowa (KCRG) - For more than two hours, council members for the City of Coralville
discussed the use of automated license plate readers before voting to approve the policy of there
use.
People spoke at the council meeting Tuesday night sharing concerns about location of Flock
cameras as well as privacy concerns. Mayor Meghann Foster mentioned her concerns, as well as
how authorities in Marshalltown were able to make an arrest in a shooting recently with the use of
these kinds of cameras.
A Flock Representative was on hand, sharing more about the company and how the cameras are
used. Council members continued to ask questions about their concerns with data use and
surveillance. People from the community also had their chance to ask questions as well.
City Council members then discussed they had signed a contract with Flock, what would it mean
if they broke it, and the positive and negative aspects of using the cameras.
From there, the council voted and approved the resolution approving a policy for the license plate
reader technology.
