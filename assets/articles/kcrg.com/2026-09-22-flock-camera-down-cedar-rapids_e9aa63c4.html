Drunk driver hits flock camera in Cedar Rapids

Pole snapped at Wilson Avenue SW and 6th Street
By Elizabeth Schultz
Published: Sep. 22, 2026 at 10:50 PM UTC|Updated: 13 hours ago
Add Us On GoogleAdd as a preferred source on Google

CEDAR RAPIDS, Iowa (KCRG) - A Flock camera at the intersection of Wilson Avenue and 6th Street Southwest in Cedar Rapids is no longer standing after police say it was hit by a drunk driver.

Tire tracks, a snapped pole and a bike lane sign that was knocked down are visible at the intersection.

Police say the driver unintentionally hit the camera.

TV9 has previously reported on people intentionally taking down Flock cameras across Iowa.
