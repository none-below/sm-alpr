CEDAR RAPIDS, Iowa (KCRG) - A Flock camera at the intersection of Wilson Avenue and 6th
Street Southwest in Cedar Rapids is no longer standing after police say it was hit by a drunk
driver.
Tire tracks, a snapped pole and a bike lane sign that was knocked down are visible at the
intersection.
Police say the driver unintentionally hit the camera.
