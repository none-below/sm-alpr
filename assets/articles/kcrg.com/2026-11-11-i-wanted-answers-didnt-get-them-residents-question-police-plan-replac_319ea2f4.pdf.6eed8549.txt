CEDAR RAPIDS, Iowa (KCRG) - Cedar Rapids police and city leaders hosted a question-and-
answer meeting Tuesday night on the department’s planned switch from Flock to Axon license
plate reader cameras. Residents at the meeting asked who can access the data and what
safeguards prevent misuse.
The department says the cameras help solve crimes and find missing people.
Police want to replace Flock with Axon
Across Cedar Rapids, 70 cameras scan license plates. The city has used license plate readers
since 2015.
Police now want to replace them with cameras provided by Axon, a company that already
supplies the department’s body cameras.
Police say Axon would work better with technology officers already use. The proposed contract
amendment lists an annual cost of $98,415.
Resident raises concerns about searches
Echo Elam came to the meeting looking for answers. Elam wants to know who can search the
information collected by the cameras and what keeps that access in check.
“I believe that what they are planning and what they are doing violates everyone’s Fourth
Amendment rights,” Elam said.
“The issue comes in where there’s no warrant required for the search. You just see the case
number. It can be any case number,” Elam said.
Those concerns were part of a broader discussion about privacy, police oversight and how the
department plans to use the technology. The public submitted more than 100 questions about the
technology and police oversight ahead of the meeting.

Police say the cameras capture license plates, not faces, and the data is kept for 30 days.
Searches must be tied to a law enforcement purpose.
Interim Cedar Rapids Police Chief Tom Jonker said the department knows the public wants
safeguards.
“We know that the public wants safeguards for this kind of technology. We have guidelines in
place to make sure that that is successful,” Jonker said.
Elam said switching companies does not answer the bigger question of who is monitoring how
the data is used.
“I wanted to get some answers, and I didn’t get them,” Elam said.
