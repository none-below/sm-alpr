Amazon's smart doorbell maker, Ring, has ended its partnership with police surveillance tech company Flock Safety. A recent Ring advertisement aired during the Super Bowl sparked backlash, showcasing a network of cameras that helped locate a lost dog. The ad raised concerns over a potential dystopian surveillance society. The feature highlighted in the advertisement, called Search Party, was not connected to Flock Safety. Ring's announcement did not cite the advertisement as a reason for the "joint decision" to end the partnership. Last year, Ring and Flock Safety had announced plans to collaborate, allowing Ring camera owners to share footage in response to law enforcement emergencies.
