Bay Area Assault weapon seized, 4 arrested in multi-city Bay Bridge stolen vehicle police chase by: Tor Smith Posted: Oct 29, 2026 / 03:09 PM PDT Updated: Oct 29, 2026 / 03:10 PM PDT Photo: SFPD. by: Tor Smith Posted: Oct 29, 2026 / 03:09 PM PDT Updated: Oct 29, 2026 / 03:10 PM PDT SHARE SAN FRANCISCO (KRON) — Four suspects, including a juvenile, face multiple charges after authorities said they led San Francisco police on a multi-city pursuit in a stolen vehicle early Thursday. The suspects were arrested after crashing the car, according to the police department. SFPD said automated license plate reader (ALPR) data identified the suspected stolen vehicle heading into San Francisco from the Bay Bridge at 1:06 a.m. Police soon used a drone to track the car. “The suspect vehicle fled from SFPD officers through city streets before going back onto the Bay Bridge. The driver fled through Oakland and Alameda and into San Leandro where the vehicle crashed into a guard rail,” police said. The California Highway Patrol’s air unit helped SFPD make the arrests. The suspects have been identified by police as: Tyshun Lane, 19, of San Francisco Reiyon Jackson, 18, of San Francisco Isiah Burns, 22, of San Francisco A juvenile suspect Police said the list of charges includes possession of an assault weapon, possession of a loaded firearm, possession of a high-capacity magazine, possession of an AR-15-style assault weapon and felony evasion. Photo: SFPD. “San Francisco Police officers will stop at nothing to arrest violent criminals on our streets. Because of officers’ swift response and diligent coordination, these dangerous suspects and firearms were taken off our streets to prevent further harm to the community,” police said. This remains an active case. Anyone with information is asked to contact SFPD at (415) 575-4444 and reference case No. 260638823. Add as preferred source on Google Copyright 2026 Nexstar Media Inc.
