Ardmore commission unanimously decides to put Flock to a
public vote
Kendall Timms, KTEN News
Sep 21, 2026
Kendall Timms
reporter/producer
ARDMORE, Okla. (KTEN) - Monday night, commissioners were in a long discussion during their
meeting about the use of Flock cameras, and they voted unanimously to allow the citizens to
vote on the fate of Flock.
This comes after commissioners originally voted to renew the cameras without a public vote
last month.
