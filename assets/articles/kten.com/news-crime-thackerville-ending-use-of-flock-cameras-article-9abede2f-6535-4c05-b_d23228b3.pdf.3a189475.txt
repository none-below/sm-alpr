Thackerville ending use of Flock cameras
KTEN News
Sep 3, 2026
THACKERVILLE, Okla. (KTEN) — Thackerville is joining the list of cities that will no longer use
Flock Safety cameras.
Police Chief Johnathan Buckaloo said the decision comes after reviewing the department's
needs, resources, and costs.
“Technology can be a valuable tool in law enforcement, but it will never replace proactive
policing, good communication, strong relationships with our citizens and officers who are
willing to be present and involved," he explained in a statement posted on Facebook.
Buckaloo added that the decision to suspend the use of the automated license plate
recognition system does not represent a reduction in the department’s commitment to public
safety.
Critics of Flock and other surveillance camera systems cite civil liberties and privacy concerns.
According to DeFlock Maps, which tracks the deployment of such cameras, there are about 10
installed in and around Thackerville.
