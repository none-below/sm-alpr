Carter County Sheriff weighs in on Flock cameras
Sabine Welsh, KTEN News
Sep 3, 2026
CARTER COUNTY, Okla. (KTEN) — We've seen the national headlines about Flock cameras, the
license plate readers that law enforcement has been using along roads and highways.
Reports say some police officers are misusing the cameras.
In light of the news, Carter County Sheriff DJ Long has an update on where those cameras stand
in his jurisdiction.
In a social media post, Long explained that the Flock cameras are a useful tool for solving
crimes.
They snap pictures that can be used to track a suspect's license plate so law enforcement can
locate them.
However, the sheriff said he has decided to not participate in the Flock system over privacy
concerns.
"What does concern me is how they may be utilized in the future,” Long wrote. “I do not want to
have a hand in assisting with the infrastructure that could be used for nefarious reasons at
some point."
Recently, Ardmore city commissioners decided to extend a contract with Flock for cameras
within the city limits, which is separate from the Carter County Sheriff's Office jurisdiction.
