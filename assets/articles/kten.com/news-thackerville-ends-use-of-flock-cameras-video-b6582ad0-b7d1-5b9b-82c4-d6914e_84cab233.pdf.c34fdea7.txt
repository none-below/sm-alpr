Thackerville ending use of Flock cameras
Thackerville's police department has decided to discontinue the use of Flock Safety
cameras after evaluating the benefits, costs, and community needs. Chief Buckaloo
emphasized that while technology aids policing, it cannot replace proactive engagement
and strong community relationships.
