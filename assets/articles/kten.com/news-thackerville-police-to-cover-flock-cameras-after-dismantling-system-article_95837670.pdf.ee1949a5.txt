Thackerville police to cover Flock cameras after dismantling
system
Kendall Timms, KTEN News
Oct 25, 2026
Kendall Timms
reporter/producer
THACKERVILLE, Okla. (KTEN) - Thackerville Police Department announcing that beginning
Monday, October 26th, they are placing covers over Flock license plate readers in their city.
This comes after the department's decision to cancel their use of Flock cameras in an effort to
maintain public trust, transparency and privacy of citizens.
