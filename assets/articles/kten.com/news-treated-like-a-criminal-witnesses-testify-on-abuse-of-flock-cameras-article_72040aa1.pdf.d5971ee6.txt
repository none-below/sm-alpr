(The Center Square) – Advocates of the growing network of AI-driven surveillance cameras
argue the technology can help make communities across the U.S. safer.
But the controversial cameras have also wreaked havoc on the lives of innocent American
citizens, including Florida resident Lindsey Isaacs, who spent 13 days in jail and received severe
media attention for a crime she didn’t commit.
