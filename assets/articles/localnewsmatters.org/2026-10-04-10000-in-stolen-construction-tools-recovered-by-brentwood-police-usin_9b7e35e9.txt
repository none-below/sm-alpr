Police recovered more than $10,000 worth of stolen tools within two days after identifying the suspects' vehicle (inset photos) using license plate reader technology following the theft on Sunday, Sept. 27. (Brentwood Police Department via Bay City News) Officers have recovered tools worth $10,000 stolen from a construction site using automated license plate reader cameras, the Brentwood Police Department announced this week. On Sunday, two suspects broke into a construction site in downtown Brentwood and stole the tools from a secure electrician’s toolbox, according to police.
