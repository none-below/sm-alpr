Moore was joined by seven candidates for Berkeley Councilmember seats in denouncing the use of mass surveillance technology at the press conference. (L to R) District 7 candidate Syrak Micael, District 1 candidate Daria Wrubel, District 8 candidate Michael Wallman, District 4 candidate Majdi Gaith Abuhamdieh, District 8 candidate Mari Mendonca, District 1 candidate Moni Gangopadhyay, John “Chip” Moore, and District 7 candidate Aidan Hill. (Tanay Gokhale/Bay City News) Seven candidates running in the upcoming Berkeley City Council elections denounced the use of mass surveillance technology, including automated license plate reader cameras, at a press conference held Tuesday on the steps of City Hall. The candidates were District 1 contenders Daria Wrubel and Moni Gangopadhyay, District 4 contender Majdi Gaith Abuhamdieh, District 7 contenders Aidan Hill and Syrak Micael, and District 8 contenders Michael Wallman and Mari Mendonca.
