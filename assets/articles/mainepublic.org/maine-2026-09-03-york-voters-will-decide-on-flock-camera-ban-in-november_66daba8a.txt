Residents in York will vote this November on whether to ban Flock cameras, or automated license plate readers. York residents submitted a citizen's initiative to ban the cameras, which use artificial intelligence to scan and log license plate information on public roadways. The town's selectboard agreed this week to add the measure to the Nov. 3 ballot. If it passes, the town must remove the cameras and cancel contracts with Flock Safety. Some residents said the cameras, which are placed on Spur Road and Route 1 leading to the Maine Turnpike, violate their Fourth amendment rights. Law enforcement agencies have said the cameras help with criminal investigations. Auburn is considering a similar citizens initiative and has not yet decided whether to place it on an upcoming ballot. The city has declined to continue its existing contract with Flock. Earlier this summer, South Portland had its seven Flock cameras removed following a vote in city council and debate with residents.
