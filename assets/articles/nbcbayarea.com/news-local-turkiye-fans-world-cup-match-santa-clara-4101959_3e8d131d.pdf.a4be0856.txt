Türkiye fans flock to Santa Clara to watch team play in World
Cup match
By Robert Handa • Published June 19, 2026 • Updated on June 19, 2026 at 7:03 pm
Thousands of Turkish Americans gathered in Santa Clara Friday to cheer on Türkiye in its World
Cup match against Paraguay at San Francisco Bay Area Stadium.

The Turkish American fans who showed up in the South Bay didn’t just come from around the Bay
Area. They traveled from all over the world.

"I met Turks from Australia, from Europe, from Asia, from Canada," Ergun Kirlikovli of Los Angeles
said. "Of course we’re like long lost brothers. We hug, we kiss, and we say where are you from and
all that."
One of the prominent Turkish Americans on scene in Santa Clara was Hamit Altintop, a soccer
hero in Türkiye.
"All cultures, all communities, all generations are coming together and we are speaking only for
one month just about fútbol," he said. "This is insane."
The gathering near San Francisco Bay Area Stadium was just one of many events taking place
Friday. The Ataturk cultural house in Mountain View, run by the Turkish American Association of
California, will serve as another major gathering place throughout the tournament.
"The World Cup means that we are one," Turkish American Association President Nalan Ozisik
said. "No matter what we think politically, how we observe our various religious beliefs or what our
ethnicity is, none of that matters anymore. This is World Cup and Türkiye is there after 24 years.
That's all that matters today."
