About 30 cats have been removed from hazardous conditions in a Glenville trailer. Also, the Troy City Council is suing Mayor Carmella Mantello over Flock contracts. These stories, and more, are in your five things to know, sponsored by the Lia Auto Group. 5 things to know sponsored by Lia 1. Rescue efforts begin for cats trapped inside Glenville trailer A Schenectady animal organization has begun removing the nearly 40 cats found trapped in a mobile home in Glenville last week. Voice for the Voiceless said they have removed 30 cats as of Sunday. 2. NY Appellate Division orders release of Sarcone admonition A New York appeals court ruled that the public has a First Amendment right to see a confidential ethics admonition issued against First Assistant U.S. Attorney John A. Sarcone III. While the court agreed to keep Sarcone’s written responses sealed to protect his reputation, it ordered the release of the formal disciplinary notice regarding professional misconduct. 3. Queensbury family turns tragedy into purpose after losing their dog Many animals shelters across our region are reaching capacity– leaving resources stretched thin. On Saturday, one Queensbury family who lost their own dog helped host a Pet Adoption Clinic at the Saratoga County Animal Shelter. 4. Albany Police identify human remains found during cold case investigation The Albany Police Department has identified the human remains found a property on Marlborough Court earlier this week. The remains have been identified as those of 24-year-old Ashley Carroll, who was reported missing from the City of Rensselaer in 2010. 5. Troy City Council suing Mayor Carmella Mantello over Flock contract Troy City Council overrode Mayor Carmella Mantello’s veto of legislation that limits Flock Safety camera data storage to just 72 hours.
