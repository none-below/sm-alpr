( NEWS10 ) — A Rhinebeck man was arrested on Sunday after he allegedly set a Flock camera on fire, leading to a brush fire, according to the Dutchess County Sheriff’s Office. John Coleman, 26, faces a felony charge. Download our WTEN+ streaming app on Roku, Fire TV and Apple TV! New York State Police and Dutchess County sheriff’s deputies were called to the area of 6052 Route 9 at about 11:50 p.m. on Saturday following a report of a brush fire. The fire was subsequently extinguished and an investigation determined that the blaze began after Coleman set a nearby Flock camera on fire, authorities said. Coleman was arrested in the early morning hours of Sunday and charged with third-degree criminal mischief and 5th-degree arson, according to the sheriff’s office. He was reportedly released on appearance tickets returnable to Town of Rhinebeck Court on Nov. 5. The incident came just weeks after the Dutchess County District Attorney and Dutchess County Sheriff withdrew their support for the use of Flock technology. The decision reportedly stemmed from concerns raised by residents at recent community forums and officials are now working to discontinue the technology across the county.
