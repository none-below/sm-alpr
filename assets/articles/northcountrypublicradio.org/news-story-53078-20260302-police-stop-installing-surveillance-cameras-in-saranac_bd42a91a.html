Police stop installing surveillance cameras in Saranac Lake. For now

Share
Mar 02, 2026 — by Richard Haller (News Intern)
A Flock Safety license plate reader is seen along a public road, Thursday, Oct. 16, 2025, in Houston. (AP Photo/David Goldman)

Mar 02, 2026 —

The Village of Saranac Lake has stopped installing surveillance cameras. 

In a statement, Saranac Lake Mayor Jimmy Williams said the project has been halted until the Police Chief returns from a trip to the FBI academy. Williams said they are listening to the community's concerns and will work with the Village Board, Police, and public to make a final decision on the cameras. 

Last week, the Village Police Department said it was installing Flock Safety Cameras around the village to identify vehicles connected to criminal activity.

A report from the Adirondack Daily Enterprise said that three cameras are already installed throughout the village, and that 6 were planned in total.

The cameras sparked a divided reaction online. Some people said they’re concerned that they threaten privacy and civil liberties. 

Similar cameras have been installed in cities and towns across the state, including Albany, Syracuse, and Ithaca. According to an unofficial online database, they’re also in Gouverneur, Lowville, and Plattsburgh.
