Police stop installing surveillance
cameras in Saranac Lake. For now
(https://www.northcountrypublicradio

BY RICHARD HALLER (NEWS INTERN) (/NEWS/REPORTERS/309/RICHARD-HALLER)
Mar 02, 2026 —
The Village of Saranac Lake has
stopped installing surveillance
cameras. 
In a statement,

fbid=1329835449171555&set=a.652950796860027) Saranac Lake Mayor Jimmy
Williams said the project has been halted until the Police Chief returns from a trip

Last week, the Village Police Department said
(https://www.facebook.com/saranaclakepolice) it was installing Flock Safety
Cameras around the village to identify vehicles connected to criminal activity.
A report from the Adirondack Daily Enterprise
(https://www.adirondackdailyenterprise.com/news/local-news/2026/02/new-flock-
police-cameras-take-roost-in-saranac-lake/) said that three cameras are already
installed throughout the village, and that 6 were planned in total. 
The cameras sparked a divided reaction online. Some people said they’re
concerned that they threaten privacy and civil liberties. 
Similar cameras have been installed in cities and towns across the state, including
Albany, Syracuse, and Ithaca. According to an unofficial online database,
(https://deflock.org/map) they’re also in Gouverneur, Lowville, and Plattsburgh.
