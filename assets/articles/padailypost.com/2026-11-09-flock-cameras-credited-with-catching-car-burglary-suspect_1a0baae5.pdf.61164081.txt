Thanks to Flock cameras, which photograph license
plates of nearby cars, Menlo Park police say they
were able to arrest a suspect in several car
burglaries and recover items that were stolen from
those cars.
Officers recently investigated two vehicle burglaries
at an apartment complex on Haven Avenue in
Menlo Park. In the incidents, victims reported stolen
property including designer sunglasses, electronic
accessories, cash, and other personal items valued
at more than $1,500.
During the investigation, officers identified a suspect
vehicle believed to be involved in both crimes.
Officers also determined the same vehicle may be
connected to a separate auto burglary that occurred
in July 2026.
Shortly after the reports were taken, a Flock alert
identified the suspect vehicle in the area of Bell
Street and University Avenue. Officers responded to
the area, found the car and contacted the driver.
A search of the vehicle resulted in the recovery of
stolen property connected to the reported
burglaries, as well as suspected drugs, police said.
Officers also determined the driver matched the
suspect captured in surveillance footage from the
auto burglary investigations.

The suspect, Salvador Ibarra-Pena, 46, of Sunnyvale,
was booked into jail on charges related to the auto
burglaries, possession of stolen property, drug-
related offenses, and other violations, police said.
“This case highlights the proactive work of patrol
officers and the value of technology in supporting
criminal investigations,” Menlo Park police said in a
statement. “A Flock Safety alert helped officers
quickly locate a vehicle linked to multiple crimes,
resulting in the recovery of stolen property, the
arrest of a suspect, and the potential resolution of
additional cases.”
