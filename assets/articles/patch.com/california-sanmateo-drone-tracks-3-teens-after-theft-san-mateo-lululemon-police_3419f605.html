Drone Tracks 3 Teens After Theft At San Mateo Lululemon: Police

Police said officers used a drone and coordinated search to locate three suspects who fled the store with merchandise.

Albert Gregory, Patch Staff
Posted Fri, Nov 6, 2026 at 10:39 am PT

SAN MATEO, CA — Three teens were taken into custody after police said two suspects ran from a Lululemon store at Hillsdale Shopping Center with stolen merchandise, leading to a pursuit Wednesday evening.

San Mateo police responded to the store at 17 Hillsdale Mall around 5:14 p.m. on Wednesday after employees reported two repeat theft suspects concealing merchandise inside the business.

When two teen boys ran from the store, officers pursued them on foot and with the drone.
