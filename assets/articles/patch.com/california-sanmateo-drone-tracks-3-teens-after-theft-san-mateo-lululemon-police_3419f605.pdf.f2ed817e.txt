Drone Tracks 3 Teens After Theft At San Mateo
Lululemon: Police
Police said officers used a drone and coordinated search to locate three suspects who
fled the store with merchandise.
Albert Gregory, Patch Staff
Posted Fri, Nov 6, 2026 at 10:39 am PT
SAN MATEO, CA — Three teens were taken into custody after police said two
suspects ran from a Lululemon store at Hillsdale Shopping Center with stolen
merchandise, leading to a pursuit Wednesday evening.

San Mateo police responded to the store at 17 Hillsdale Mall around 5:14 p.m.
on Wednesday after employees reported two repeat theft suspects concealing
merchandise inside the business.
When two teen boys ran from the store, officers pursued them on foot and
with the drone.

One suspect was detained nearby, while another was found partially
concealed in the mall parking lot, police said.
A third teen was later found hiding in an employee stairwell outside the store.
He was wearing a mask and hood and carrying backpacks belonging to the
other two suspects, according to police.
All three were arrested and later released to their parents, police said.

The 15-year-old San Mateo resident and a 14-year-old San Bruno resident

said.
