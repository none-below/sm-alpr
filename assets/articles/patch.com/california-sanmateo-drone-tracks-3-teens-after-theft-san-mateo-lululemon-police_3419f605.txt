Albert Gregory , Patch Staff Posted Fri, Nov 6, 2026 at 10:39 am PT SAN MATEO, CA — Three teens were taken into custody after police said two suspects ran from a Lululemon store at Hillsdale Shopping Center with stolen merchandise, leading to a pursuit Wednesday evening. San Mateo police responded to the store at 17 Hillsdale Mall around 5:14 p.m. on Wednesday after employees reported two repeat theft suspects concealing merchandise inside the business.
