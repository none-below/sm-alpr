FULL STORY
Last week I wrote about a Canadian YouTuber who was visited at his home by police after he
built a license plate reader camera designed to only track law enforcement vehicles.
The YouTuber, Ontario resident Anthony Sistilli, caught the attention of the police after
producing two short-form videos about the project that ended up earning more than a
million views across social media.
The police said they were concerned that Sistilli’s setup — built with a cheap Amazon
camera, a laptop, and some AI-generated software — could be used to create a map of their
movements and thus allow criminals to get the upper hand or even target cops.
Sistilli had no intention of building such a map but found the visit rather ironic given that the
