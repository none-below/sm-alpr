SIA issues guidance on ALPR use

SIA issues guidance on ALPR use

General News

By SSN Staff

Updated 9:39 AM CDT, Thu September 10, 2026

SILVER SPRING, Md.—The Security Industry Association (SIA) has issued a call for the responsible and effective use of automated license plate reader (ALPR) technology and released policy principles designed to guide the development of administrative and legislative actions by states and federal governments. “SIA firmly believes that all technology products, including automated license plate recognition, must only be used for purposes that are lawful, ethical and nondiscriminatory,” said Don Erickson, CEO. “ALPR is not - and must never become - a tool for mass surveillance or tracking law-abiding citizens. Public policy governing the use of ALPR systems should ensure a heightened level of accountability for government use, provide strong prohibitions against misuse and authorize meaningful enforcement mechanisms. SIA stands ready to wholeheartedly support responsible and balanced approaches to ensuring appropriate use of ALPR in the public and commercial sectors.” The guidance comes as automated license plate reader technology faces growing scrutiny nationwide, driven in part by controversy surrounding Flock Safety, a major provider of ALPR technology.

The guidance issued by SIA includes:

  • An overview of ALPR technology and ALPR data
  • How constitutional protections should be safeguarded when using ALPR systems
  • An exploration of concerns around “warrantless” use of ALPR systems
  • How policymakers should address concerns about ALPR
  • Policy principles designed to guard against misuse, protect privacy, mitigate operational or procedural issues and ensure safeguards specific to law enforcement use, while preserving public safety benefits
  • Examples of ALPR effectiveness and value
  • Recommended law enforcement community resources on ALPR technology and policy

View SIA’s ALPR statement, policy principles and guidance here.
