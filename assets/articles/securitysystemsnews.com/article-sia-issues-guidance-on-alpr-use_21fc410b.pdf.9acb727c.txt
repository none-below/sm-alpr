SIA ISSUES GUIDANCE ON ALPR USE
General News
SSN Staff
Updated 9:39 AM CDT, Thu September 10, 2026
SILVER SPRING, Md.—The Security Industry Association (SIA) has issued a call for the
responsible and effective use of automated license plate reader (ALPR) technology and
released policy principles designed to guide the development of administrative and
legislative actions by states and federal governments. “SIA firmly believes that all technology
products, including automated license plate recognition, must only be used for purposes
that are lawful, ethical and nondiscriminatory,” said Don Erickson, CEO. “ALPR is not - and
must never become - a tool for mass surveillance or tracking law-abiding citizens. Public
policy governing the use of ALPR systems should ensure a heightened level of
accountability for government use, provide strong prohibitions against misuse and authorize
