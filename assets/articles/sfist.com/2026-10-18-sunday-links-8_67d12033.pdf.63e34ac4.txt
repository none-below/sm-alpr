Canadian officials are reporting that multiple people were killed
and dozens more injured following an explosion on Saturday at a
Canadian military base in Alberta province. Canada Prime
Minister Mark Carney is calling the incident "one of the worst
tragedies for the forces on Canadian soil in peacetime." [ABC7]
SFPD say a man was fatally shot near an elementary school in San
Francisco’s Potrero Hill neighborhood on Friday night. No further
details, including information on the shooter, is currently
available. [Chronicle]
French President Emmanuel Macron voiced "anger" and "shame"
over rioting and looting as nationwide protests over living
conditions erupt across France. [NPR]
The Golden State Valkyries beat the Atlanta Dream 68-64 at home
yesterday to take Game 1 of the WNBA Finals. [ABC7]
Emergency crews successfully airlifted a woman from a creek in
Wine Country on Saturday after she fell from a cliff. [Chronicle]
MapQuest has added a new "surveillance layer" feature that allows
users to flag Flock cameras on routes. [KRON4]
One child and one adult are dead in addition to hospitalizations
for 11 others after a vehicle drove onto a Kansas soccer field on
Saturday. [KTVU]
A man from San Francisco has been arrested for allegedly striking
two children, both six, as they were riding a bicycle in Half Moon
Bay before fleeing the scene, per authorities. [Chronicle]
