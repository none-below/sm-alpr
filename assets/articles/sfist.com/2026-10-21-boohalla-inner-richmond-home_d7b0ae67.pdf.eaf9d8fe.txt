Much like other members of Congress around the Bay Area who
received suspicious pieces of mail containing a powdery white
substance last week, Nancy Pelosi's office has confirmed they got
one too. Everyone in Pelosi's office is reportedly safe, but the
suspicious substance still has not been identified. [KRON4]
In addition to the widely publicized arrest in San Francisco of 51-
year-old Milton Ramos-Rodriguez by armed ICE agents, ICE
confirmed the arrests of two other men in the Bay Area. Edgar
Fernando Guerrero Romo of Mexico has a DUI arrest on his
record, ICE said, and Eduardo Antonio Ruiz-Garcia of Honduras is
accused of assaulting officers during his arrest, and he was
subsequently hospitalized with the agency noting he had a
"preexisting injury from falling from a ladder two days prior."
