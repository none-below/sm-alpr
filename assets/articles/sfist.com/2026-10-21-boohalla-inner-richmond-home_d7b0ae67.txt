Everyone in Pelosi's office is reportedly safe, but the suspicious substance still has not been identified. [ KRON4 ] In addition to the widely publicized arrest in San Francisco of 51-year-old Milton Ramos-Rodriguez by armed ICE agents, ICE confirmed the arrests of two other men in the Bay Area. Edgar Fernando Guerrero Romo of Mexico has a DUI arrest on his record, ICE said, and Eduardo Antonio Ruiz-Garcia of Honduras is accused of assaulting officers during his arrest, and he was subsequently hospitalized with the agency noting he had a "preexisting injury from falling from a ladder two days prior." [ KRON4 ] EBay îs the latest Bay Area company to announce layoffs, with an estimated 1,000 jobs or 9% of the workforce being eliminated. [ Chronicle ] National: Doctors say that Pope Leo will not need further treatment following the partial lung removal, and that the cancer was localized. [ KPIX ] The fallout continues for Republicans in Ohio following a gross attack ad paid for by Republican gubernatorial nominee Vivek Ramaswamy's campaign featuring the estranged mother of Democratic candidate Amy Acton — a woman Acton has not seen since she was 12 years old, who stayed with a man who faced criminal charges for sexually assaulting Acton. [ CNN ] Trader Joe's has announced plans for 30 new stores nationwide next year, including three in California. [ KTVU ] Video: 'Boohalla,' a home in the Inner Richmond that's decorated both for Halloween and for the Valkyries' playoff run, is getting local TV coverage as fans flock to take photos there.
