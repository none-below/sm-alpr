Home Blog Public Safety View All Posts Mar 18 Flock Safety Camera Turned Off Posted on March 18, 2026 at 9:48 AM by Eric Wilson On March 9, Shorewood City Council discussed the Flock Safety camera located in Shorewood. The Council requested the South Lake Minnetonka Police Department turn off this camera until the completion of an independent audit of the Department’s compliance procedures and use. The audit is required by Minnesota Statutes 13.824 to be completed biennially, or once every two years, for any Minnesota law enforcement agency using an automated license plate reader. The camera has been turned off and Council plans to revisit the use of Flock following the audit, which is expected to be completed in May. More information about this discussion can be found in the March 9, 2026 Council Agenda . More information about Flock and the SLMPD can be found on the Department’s policy webpage and its Flock Transparency Portal .
