Burlingame council supports exploring citywide
surveillance technology policy
By Holly Rusch, Daily Journal staff
Apr 10, 2026
 
Amidst ongoing, national conversations around public surveillance and data
safety, a majority of Burlingame councilmembers said they’d be in support of
exploring a citywide surveillance technology policy that would define data access
and protection rules.

--- page 2 ---
Flock Safety, an automated license plate reader vendor that records data typically
used in law enforcement operations, has been a particular point of concern in
data safety discussions.
