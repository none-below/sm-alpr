License plate reader helps catch theft suspects in
Redwood City
Bay City News
Mar 11, 2026
 
Two San Jose men were arrested Tuesday after Redwood City officers used
automated license plate reader technology to track a vehicle linked to a January
theft of more than $4,000 in merchandise from a motorcycle store, police said.

--- page 2 ---
The theft occurred Jan. 29 at the Cycle Gear store at 2426 El Camino Real,
according to the Redwood City Police Department. Investigators said two suspects
entered the store, grabbed multiple clothing items, and quickly left without
paying before driving away in a red Subaru Impreza.
