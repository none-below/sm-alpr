Millbrae council approves 23 new surveillance cameras 
City officials OK 5-year contract for license plate reading cameras to aid law enforcement
By Corey Browning Daily Journal staff
Nov 29, 2021
 
Millbrae will soon get a host of new surveillance cameras, with the City Council
approving a five-year contract to install 23 license plate reading devices around
major transit arteries in the city.
The council has long sought
implementation of the cameras,
called automated license plate
readers, or ALPRs, but delays related
to permitting from Caltrans have
slowed the process. The cameras are
an increasingly popular law
enforcement tool in the region,
capable also of identifying things
like vehicle make and model, color,
bumper stickers or other
distinguishers.
