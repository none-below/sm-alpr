New surveillance cameras on hold for Burlingame
Councilmembers voice a need for more policy framework, cite privacy and policing concerns
Corey Browning, Daily Journal staff
Mar 1, 2022
 
Burlingame councilmembers last week indicated they were still unready to
approve the use of automated license plate reading cameras within the city, citing
a need to further understand how the technology would be applied and how the
