The city is signing on to an eight-year purchase agreement with the company, which includes a one-year free trial period and payments beginning in June 2028. During the trial, the San Mateo Police Department will deploy Axon ALPRs to the same locations as the current cameras supplied by Flock Safety, another prominent law enforcement vendor, and conduct a side-by-side comparison to determine which system is preferred.
