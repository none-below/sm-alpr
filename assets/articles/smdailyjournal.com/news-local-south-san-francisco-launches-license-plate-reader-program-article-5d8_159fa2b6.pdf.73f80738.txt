South San Francisco launches license plate reader
program
Nov 22, 2022
 
South San Francisco officials announced the city has launched an automated
license plate recognition program with 28 solar-powered cameras installed at key
public locations, as a way to aid solving increased property crimes in the hotel
corridor.

--- page 2 ---
“The use of this technology has already proven to be a game changer in the fight
against crime and a valuable tool for our officers to help solve otherwise difficult-
to-solve cases,” Police Chief Scott Campbell said in a press release. “The safety of
our residents and visitors is paramount and the investment in this program
shows how committed we are to keeping South San Francisco a safe place.”
