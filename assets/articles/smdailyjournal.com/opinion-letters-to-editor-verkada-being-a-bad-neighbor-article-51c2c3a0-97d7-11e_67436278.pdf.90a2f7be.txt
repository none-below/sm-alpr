Verkada being a bad neighbor?
Dec 11, 2023
 
Editor, 
Verkada has been refusing to comply with its requirement to open its parking
garages at 405 E. Fourth Ave. and 406 E. Third Ave., even though its lease
stipulates it, it was a condition of the building being approved (in exchange for
benefits the developer and presumably the tenants would benefit from). Heck
there are 3-foot square signs posted outside both garages serving as a daily
reminder to anyone entering the properties.
Parking downtown is challenging, especially on nights and weekends. Our
downtown businesses rely heavily on holiday traffic, and parking is desperately
needed for families, and seniors, particularly with the colder, rainier weather we
experience now. Verkada has known about this for over 40 days, via the developer
and the city. We’ve reached out via social media, and have received no response,
no ETA, and frustratingly, no access.
It raises the bigger question of developer concessions. Every project asks for
waivers, concessions and/or bonuses, ostensibly for community benefit. With no
one policing this, and if businesses like Verkada can seemingly ignore these
requirements, one must ask — why are we granting these at all, and what will the
impact be on our beloved city?
Michael Weinhauer
San Mateo
