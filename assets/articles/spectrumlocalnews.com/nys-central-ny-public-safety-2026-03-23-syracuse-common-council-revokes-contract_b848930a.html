Syracuse Common Council revokes contract with license plate reader tech­nology

BY Spectrum News Staff Syracuse
PUBLISHED 5:48 PM ET Mar. 23, 2026 PUBLISHED 5:48 PM EDT Mar. 23, 2026
SHARE

The Syracuse Common Council voted Monday to revoke its contract with license plate reader technology company Flock Safety.

The company had been providing license plate readers for the city, but the council has decided to go with Axon instead.

City leaders expressed concerns about Flock collecting and sharing personal information after the Syracuse Police Department mistakenly agreed to share that type of information.

The technology automatically photographs every car that passes, creating a detailed log of when and where people travel and with whom, regardless of any suspected crime. That data is then sent to a national database that can, in some cases, be accessed by law enforcement agencies across the country. That network allows for collaborative crime solving but also opens the door to unwanted data sharing.

Flock says it does not share personal information without permission from their clients.

"This item may be small to some people, but it is very symbolic to us as a city that we are not going to sit here and just say go ahead and trample all over people's constitutional rights," Democratic Councilor Jimmy Monto said.
