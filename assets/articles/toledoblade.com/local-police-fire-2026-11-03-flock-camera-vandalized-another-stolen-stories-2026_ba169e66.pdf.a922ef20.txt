Flock camera vandalized, another stolen
 BLADE STAFF
 
NOV 3, 2026
 
5:52 PM
Unknown individuals tampered with a pair of Flock cameras in East Toledo over
the weekend, police said.
The camera at East Broadway Street and Woodville Road had its wires cut, while
the Flock pole several blocks away, in the 800 block of Stillman Street, was cut
down and stolen, police said.
