Editorial: Follow the lead of Chief Braun
 
NOV 10, 2026
 
11:33 AM
There’s no better illustration of dysfunctional Toledo city government than the
dueling views on the Toledo Police Department’s Flock cameras held by council
and the police department.
Six councilmen, despite a city charter that wisely prohibits them from operational
authority over city safety forces, have been pushing a resolution for weeks, urging
the Kapszukiewicz administration to terminate the TPD contract with Flock.
Read more editorials
New TPD Chief Kevin Braun, one week into his new job, told Blade reporter
Kelly Kaczala the need to embrace new technology is a primary focus for his
leadership because there are going to be fewer officers.
Chief Braun says that’s a universal police department problem. In Toledo, thanks
to the failure of the four-year, 0.25-percent income tax increase for neighborhood

--- page 2 ---
safety, the city is looking at significant layoffs and will not be able to fund a new
police class.
There’s no doubt that irresponsible rhetoric from Toledo City Council on “mass
surveillance” created a climate of distrust that contributed to the levy’s failure with
voters.
With big police and fire layoffs inevitable and understaffing likely for years
because of both the elimination of a class of new recruits and a $50 million
structural deficit in the city budget, the absolute last thing TPD should do is
eliminate Flock cameras.
“It is by far the most useful tool we have ever had in this department. Flock works.
We enjoy, on average, a homicide clearance rate between 80 [percent] to 90
percent every year. It is a tool our detectives use in just about every single case
they investigate,” Chief Braun said. “It would be so detrimental to lose that. It
would set us back in a big way.”
As we mull over Toledo City Council’s attempts to “set back” the police
department, let’s remember these meddlers have no authority for management of
TPD and have been told repeatedly by former Chief Michael Troendle and current
Chief Braun of Flock’s effectiveness as a crime fighting tool.
Council has the final say on the city budget. And because it’s so tight, that’s where
their attention should be focused.
New TPD Chief Braun has shown deep understanding of the structural financial
challenges police can only overcome through the savvy use of technology.
Council should listen, learn, and let Chief Braun lead without getting in the way of
effective law enforcement by overstepping the authority granted them by the
citizens of Toledo.
