JACKSON, Tenn. (WBBJ) - A federal grand jury indicted former Jackson Police Department patrol officer Najwa Zandlo on one
count of cyberstalking on Tuesday, following an investigation by the Jackson Police Department and the FBI.
Zandlo was taken into custody by FBI agents on the morning of Oct. 16, 2026.
Internal investigation
In October 2025, while Zandlo was employed with the Jackson Police Department, a local citizen filed a formal complaint
regarding her conduct. JPD placed Zandlo on administrative leave and opened an internal investigation after receiving the
