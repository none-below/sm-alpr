LOUISVILLE, Ky. (WDRB+WAVE) — A Georgetown Police Department officer was arrested
Thursday for alleged misuse of the Flock system.

--- page 2 ---
According to Indiana State Police, a detective met with the Georgetown police chief after the chief
was alerted to irregular search activity within the Flock system.
ISP said an internal review completed by Georgetown police showed officer Justin Craig
conducted numerous searches in 2026 that “did not appear connected to any authorized
investigation.”
Court records reveal an ISP trooper found 88 searches for the car Craig’s wife drives. He also
said he found more than 230 searches for a car and motorcycle owned by a “previous
acquaintance” of hers.
Investigators reviewed logs and determined Craig conducted repeated searches using reasons
like traffic violations, drug offenses, assault or vehicle theft. Georgetown’s police chief said none
of the vehicles were tied to any active investigations.
According to ISP, the searches were conducted during Craig’s scheduled duty hours.
ISP determined Craig accessed Flock information “in a manner inconsistent with department
policy and without lawful purpose.”

A warrant for Craig was served Thursday and he was taken into custody. He has since been
released on bond.
Craig was charged with two counts of fraud and one count of official misconduct.
Craig’s attorney Matthew Lemme released the following statement:
“Flock sells this same technology to cities, homeowners associations, and shopping malls. When
public criticism of it came, the response was to put it on the shoulders of individual police officers.
The conduct alleged does not violate any law or department policy. If the public or police
agencies believe it should, the answer is to write that law or policy. Having that public
conversation about privacy and practice is appropriate. Charging officers before it has even
started is not.
Officer Craig will file a motion to dismiss the charges against him."

The Georgetown Police Department released a statement on Friday:
“On Aug. 31, 2026, Officer Justin Craig was placed on paid administrative leave following an
allegation of misuse and audit of the department’s LPR system.
Indiana State Police conducted an investigation and Officer Justin Craig was arrested on Oct. 22,
2026.
Due to pending criminal case, the Georgetown Police Department will not be providing any
additional information at this time."
