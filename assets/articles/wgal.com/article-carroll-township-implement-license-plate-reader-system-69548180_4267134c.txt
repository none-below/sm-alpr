DILLSBURG, Pa. — The Carroll Township Police Department in York County is planning to implement a license plate reader system to enhance security and efficiency in the community. Carroll Township police said the reader captures four key elements: license plate numbers, time, date, and direction of travel. This pole-mounted reader is expected to aid in crime prevention, faster recovery of stolen vehicles, locating missing or endangered persons, enhancing neighborhood security, reducing officer workload, and increasing efficiency, all without accessing personal information or tracking daily routines. The system logs license plates as they pass by, similar to what an officer could observe while patrolling. Advertisement Nearby in Newberry Township, the police chief said their license plate reader has already led to several successes, including the recovery of a stolen motorcycle. "Next thing you know, he took off, right. To steal the vehicle. If it wasn't for our license plate reader cameras, we wouldn't have been able to see which vehicle brought that individual to that meeting place. Provide us with the investigative leads to, eventually make an arrest on that of the person that are the the individuals that stole that motorcycle," Chief Steven Lutz said. The police department said only authorized personnel can view the license plate reader data, and all access is logged and can be audited. The data is stored for one year.
