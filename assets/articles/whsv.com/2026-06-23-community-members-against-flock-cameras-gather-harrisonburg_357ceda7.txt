(WHSV) - The debate over Flock license plate reader cameras continues in Harrisonburg. Opponents of the technology gathered Monday night, arguing the cameras pose privacy concerns and urging city leaders to remove them. The event was aimed at raising awareness and building support for their campaign against the cameras. The group said it has collected thousands of petition signatures and sent thousands of emails to city leaders. “We’re already seeing wins for the movement here. The event is just about getting together as a large group, having a potluck, talking to each other, building relationships, and seeing what we can do to kind of bring people together and bring more awareness to this issue,” Noah Etka, member of Deflock Harrisonburg said. Etka said organizers plan to continue canvassing and meeting with residents as they push for a future city council vote on the camera system. Copyright 2026 WHSV. All rights reserved.
