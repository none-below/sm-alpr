Orangeburg County Flock cameras vandalized, deputies say

By Elizabeth West
Published: Nov. 3, 2026 at 10:11 PM UTC
Add Us On GoogleAdd as a preferred source on Google

NOTE: The above video is a livestream of WIS featuring current newscasts, Soda City Living and Gray Media’s Local News Live.

EUTAWVILLE, S.C. (WIS) - Various Flock camera systems throughout Orangeburg County had reported damages on Oct. 25, according to the Orangeburg County Sheriff’s Office (OCSO).

Deputies said they were notified of the damage by Eutawville Police Department Interim Chief Lt. R. H. Grant.

Multiple incident reports were filed by OCSO that said the camera systems were “tampered with and damaged.”

The Flock cameras are owned by the Eutawville Police Department, but the locations fall within Orangeburg County’s jurisdiction.

Law enforcement has not found the identity of anyone responsible. The case will be investigated by OCSO’s Criminal Investigations Division.
