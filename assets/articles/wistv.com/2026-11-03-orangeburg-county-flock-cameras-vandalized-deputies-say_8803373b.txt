EUTAWVILLE, S.C. (WIS) - Various Flock camera systems throughout Orangeburg County had reported damages on Oct. 25, according to the Orangeburg County Sheriff’s Office (OCSO). Deputies said they were notified of the damage by Eutawville Police Department Interim Chief Lt. R. H. Grant. Multiple incident reports were filed by OCSO that said the camera systems were “tampered with and damaged.” The Flock cameras are owned by the Eutawville Police Department, but the locations fall within Orangeburg County’s jurisdiction. Law enforcement has not found the identity of anyone responsible. The case will be investigated by OCSO’s Criminal Investigations Division. Feel more informed, prepared, and connected with WIS.
