(KOLN) -A man was charged after reportedly damaging a Flock camera in eastern Nebraska. On Oct. 11, the Platte County Sheriff’s Office was called to the area of 48th Avenue and 53rd Street after a report of someone damaging a Flock Camera. The person was seen walking through a field wearing a ski mask. According to court records, a deputy saw the suspect walking eastbound along a private driveway that was posted no trespassing. The suspect, who refused to identify himself, was later identified as 26-year-old Caeden Hartman. Hartman refused all involvement with the tampering and destruction of the camera. 26-year-old Caeden Hartman (10/11) A handgun and black metal pole were found in the field where Hartman was walking through. Deputies examined the Flock Camera at the intersection of 48th Avenue and 53rd Street. Deputies estimated that the camera, which was worth $6,000, had about $550 worth of damage. According to court records, Hartman had met with another person, who Hartman tried to convince to join in “flocking.” Hartman had reportedly lifted his shirt and displayed a firearm that was holstered in his waistband. The person also saw Hartman with a black metal pole, which Hartman reportedly used to damage the camera. According to court records, that Hartman was charged with terroristic threats, use of a firearm to commit a felony, criminal mischief costing over $5,000, use of a deadly weapon to commit a felony, and second-degree criminal trespass.
