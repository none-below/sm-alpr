Advertisement By Lance Wilhelm, WRAL Reporter A Harnett County detective was indicted for misusing government computer systems. Jayne L. Holland, 45, a detective with the Harnett County Sheriff's Office, is facing charges of two counts of automated license plate system violations—which are misdemeanor offenses—and one count of accessing government computers for personal reasons, a felony. Other WRAL Top Stories The dates of the offenses are listed as January 22 and April 1 on court documents. She turned herself in to the Wake County Jail on Tuesday, around 1 p.m., and agents with the State Bureau of Investigation (SBI) met her there, according to NC SBI. She posted a $5,000 bond. She is set to appear in court in Harnett County on Dec. 14 at 9:30 a.m. Holland is still listed as a detective on the Harnett County Sheriff's Office website.
