# SMPD case-ID harvest

Case-ID candidates extracted from SMPD search reasons across the three
audit-log corpora already in this repo. Local extraction only — no lookups
against any case system have been performed. This is the input set for a
future hydration step (CitizenRIMS et al.), kept as shared data so parallel
work can join against it.

Generated by `scripts/harvest_case_ids.py` (deterministic; re-running on the
same inputs is byte-identical). Not wired into the site build.

## Scope

Searches from **2026** (all months available locally) plus the **January
2025** slice — the one 2025 window where Redwood City's production carries
`Reason` and searcher names (`PRA_26_217_2025_1`, Jan 1–15).

| source | rows 2025-01 | rows 2026 | what it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `smpd_audit` | 5,321 | 18,323 | SMPD's own produced audit rows (`pra-W012541-041426.json` Jan 2023–Mar 2026 + `pra-W012818-053026.json` Apr–May 2026); userId redacted by the city |
| `la_network` | 4,023 | 16,206 | Rows with `Org Name == "San Mateo CA PD"` in Los Altos PRA 26-366 network audits (Reason released essentially in full; through Jul 6 2026) |
| `rwc_network` | 1,730 | — | SMPD rows in Redwood City PRA 26-217's Jan 1–15 2025 release (Reason + searcher names survive there) |

RWC's 2026-era releases (26-217 2026 files, 26-741) carry **no Reason at all**
for partner rows and are skipped. RWC fall-2023 reasons and LA/RWC
Feb–Dec 2025 are in-repo but out of scope for now.

## Identifier kinds

| kind | shape | example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cad_event` | YYMMDD + 3–4-digit event; officer-typed variants `20YYMMDDNNNN`, `YY-MMDDNNN`, `YY-MMDD-NNN`, and 8-digit YYMMDD+NN all canonicalize to digits-only. Embedded date validated, emitted as `cad_date`. | `2602190261` → 2026-02-19 ev 261 |
| `court_case` | County criminal-case style; `county` attribute kept | `25SM013446A` |
| `agency_rms` | Two-letter agency prefix + YY + 9 digits — neighboring agencies' RMS numbers typed by SMPD officers (SH=SMCSO, DP=Daly City, EP=East Palo Alto, GT) | `SH25000089394` |
| `hyphen_case` | `YY-NNNNN(N)` classic RMS style; often carries a foreign-agency label in the reason text (`24-016265 ALCO`, `26-4453 sunnyvale PD`) | `22-2002` |

Digit runs that match no pattern (or fail date validation) are preserved
per-row in `unclassified_numbers`, never silently dropped. Reasons that are a
bare UUID (a Flock artifact, clustered on 2026-01-07) are classed `uuid` and
contribute no IDs.

## Files

- `search_rows.ndjson.gz` — one line per in-scope SMPD search row:
  `source`, `source_file`, `period`, `search_time` (ISO UTC), `reason` (as
  produced), `category`/`detail` (split on the post-Dec-2025 `" - "`
  convention), `reason_class`, `case_ids`, plus `name`, `case_field`,
  `search_type`, `networks`, `devices`, `time_frame`, `row_id` where the
  source provides them. Redaction markers (`***`, `REDACTED`) are normalized
  to absent.
- `case_ids.json` — `stats` plus the deduplicated `case_ids` index: per unique
  ID, `kind`, `n_searches`, `by_source` counts, `first_search`/`last_search`,
  `categories` (Flock category strings it appeared under), `multi_category`,
  `searchers` (names as produced — RWC initials only), up to 3
  `sample_reasons`, and `cad_date`/`county`/`agency_prefix` where applicable.

## Headline numbers (this snapshot)

- 45,603 rows → 9,813 with ≥1 case ID → **940 unique case IDs**
  (870 cad_event, 29 court_case, 29 hyphen_case, 12 agency_rms).
- 92 case IDs were searched under **more than one crime category**
  (e.g. `2602190261` as both Motor Vehicle Theft and Counterfeiting/Forgery).
- **Source asymmetry, the big one:** 939 of 940 IDs come only from partner
  recordings; exactly one (`2601070225`) also appears in SMPD's own produced
  rows. In Jan 2026 Los Altos recorded ~1,437 SMPD searches with case-number
  reasons; SMPD's own produced rows for the same month contain zero. In Jan
  2025, partners recorded bare CAD numbers as SMPD reasons while 92% of
  SMPD's own produced rows read `investigation`. Whether this is a
  field-selection difference in Flock's audit-PDF export or something else is
  unresolved — same-event triangulation (timestamp matching) is the next
  step. Do not cite as a finding until then.
- The Mar–Apr 2026 empty-detail epoch is visible in the partner-recorded rows
  too, so that collapse was data-side, not an artifact of SMPD's export.
- CAD dates span 2020–2026; 249 IDs date to 2025 and 578 to 2026, i.e. most
  of the set falls inside CitizenRIMS's dense coverage (Mar 2025+).
- 0 cad_event IDs have a first search strictly before their embedded CAD
  date (UTC-safe comparison).
- 121 IDs are independently corroborated by both LA and RWC recordings
  (Jan 2025 overlap).

## Caveats

- `n_searches` counts produced rows, not distinct search events: the same
  physical search can appear in `smpd_audit` and in each partner's network
  audit. Use `by_source` and, later, timestamp matching for event-level work.
- Search times are UTC as produced; CAD dates are presumably local Pacific.
  A late-evening Pacific search carries the next UTC date.
- RWC searcher names are initials as produced; LA partner-row names are
  Flock-blanked (`***`) or city-redacted.
- `agency_rms` and labeled `hyphen_case` IDs are other agencies' case
  numbers — evidence of searches touching outside matters, not SMPD RMS keys;
  they will not resolve in SMPD's case system.
