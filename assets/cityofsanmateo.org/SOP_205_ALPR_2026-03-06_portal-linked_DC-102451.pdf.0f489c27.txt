Automated License Plate Reader Operating
Procedure

205.1 PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This Standard Operating Procedure provides guidelines for the operation and accessing of the
Department's Automated License Plate Reader (ALPR) program and data. This document is
intended to supplement Policy 463.

Our Department currently uses three independent ALPR technology platforms - Flock Safety
Inc.,Vigilant Solutions, and AXON Fleet 3 Police Vehicle In-Car Camera System. Our Department
only subscribes to Vigilant Solutions to access available ALPR data and we do not upload or share
any ALPR data to their platform.

In accordance with the California Values Act, ALPR data may not be used for the purposes of
immigration enforcement or other similar duties.

This Standard Operating Procedure, along with Policy 463, shall be posted conspicuously on our
Department website.

205.2 DEFINITIONS
The following definitions for 'Operator' and 'End-User,' are listed in our Policy 463, and come from
California Civil Code 1798.

"Automated license plate recognition operator" or "ALPR operator," means a person that
operates an ALPR system. For our Department purposes, this will usually refer to our personnel or
authorized designee operating a vehicle or other item of equipment with ALPR cameras collecting
license plate data.

"Automated license plate recognition end-user" or "ALPR end-user" means a person that
accesses or uses an ALPR system. For our Department purposes, this will usually refer to our
personnel or authorized designee accessing historical data that is collected and stored in a
centralized database(s).

205.3 ALPR OPERATOR TRAINING

Prior to the operation of the our Department approved ALPR system, any person operating a
system shall complete Department approved training. The training will be coordinated by the
Department Training Division, and shall include the following:

. A general review of the functionality of the vendor's software system

. The authorized purposes for operating the ALPR system.

. How to access the software system, by way of a login/password protected registry
. How our vendor maintains logs of access and use

. How our vendor retains and purges data from its system

205.4 ALPR END-USER TRAINING

Prior to accessing the ALPR platforms to review data as end-users, any person doing so shall
complete Department approved training. The training will be coordinated by the Department
Training Division, and shall include the following:

. A general review of how the ALPR Platform works
. The authorized purposes for accessing the ALPR platform

. Every ALPR data search shall be documented with a reason for the search, usually
by agency case number, incident number, or warrant number.

. How to access the software system, by way of a login/password protected registry

. How the ALPR platform maintains logs of operator access and use

. How the ALPR platform retains and purges data from its systems
Any person(s) using the ALPR platform as end-user shall complete Department approved training.
205.5 ALPR SYSTEMS AUDITING
The Field Operations Captain has administrative responsibility for the ALPR platforms. This
responsibility may be delegated to a designee as appropriate. ALPR systems audit should be

completed quarterly to ensure compliance of department policies. The results of this audit should
be submitted to the Fleld Operations Captain for their review.

205.5.1
Motorola's VehicleManager (formerly Vigilant)

Motorola's VehicleManager platform utilizes analytics to enable authorized users to query and
review Automated License Plate Recognition (ALPR) detection data.

At this time, the San Mateo Police Department does not operate Motorola VehicleManager ALPR
cameras and does not upload or share ALPR data in the VehicleManager system.

Motorola VehicleManager stores ALPR information within a dedicated environment designed to
meet Criminal Justice Information Services (CJIS) security requirements. The system utilizes
industry-standard encryption to protect ALPR data.

The VehicleManager application and associated databases operate within the Motorola Solutions
cloud environment, which incorporates administrative, technical, and physical safeguards
designed to protect ALPR information consistent with CJIS security standards.

Flock Safety

The Flock Safety Automated License Plate Recognition (ALPR) system consists of networked
cameras that automatically capture license plate images and associated vehicle characteristics,
including make, model, color, and other identifying attributes as vehicles pass within view of the
cameras.

The primary purpose of the system is to assist law enforcement in preventing, deterring, and
investigating crime by generating searchable ALPR data and providing real-time alerts when a
detected license plate is associated with vehicles linked to stolen vehicles, vehicles involved in
criminal activity, missing persons, AMBER Alerts, Silver Alerts, wanted individuals, or missing
persons.

All ALPR detections collected through the Flock Safety system are stored within Flock Safety's
secure cloud-based infrastructure. The system utilizes industry-standard encryption to protect
ALPR information both in transit and at rest. Administrative, technical, and physical safeguards
are implemented to protect ALPR information from unauthorized access, disclosure, modification,
or destruction, consistent with applicable security standards.

Access to ALPR information is restricted to authorized personnel for legitimate law enforcement
purposes, and system activity is subject to audit logging.

Axon Fleet 3 Police In-Vehicle Camera System

The Axon Fleet 3 Police In-Vehicle Camera System is an in-car video and digital evidence
management platform designed for public safety vehicles. The system integrates video recording,
automated license plate recognition (ALPR) functionality, and real-time connectivity into a single
platform to support law enforcement operations and evidence collection.

The system may capture ALPR detections and associated vehicle characteristics when equipped
with ALPR capabilities. This information may be used to assist law enforcement personnel
in investigating crimes, identifying vehicles associated with missing persons, criminal activity,
locating stolen vehicles, and supporting public safety operations.

Axon Fleet 3 incorporates security controls designed to protect digital evidence and ALPR
information. Data collected by the system is encrypted during transmission and while stored, and
is maintained within CJIS-compliant cloud storage environments. Access to the system and data
is restricted through role-based access controls and authentication measure
