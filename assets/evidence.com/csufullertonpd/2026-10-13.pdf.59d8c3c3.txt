California State University Police - Fullerton
ALPR Policy and Usage
Last Updated: Oct 13, 2026 05:05 AM -07:00
Overview
Policy Settings
California State University Police - Fullerton uses Axon Automatic License Plate
Reader (ALPR) technology to capture objective evidence without compromising
individual privacy. We utilize real-time alerting in patrol vehicles to arrest wanted
criminals, recover stolen vehicles and locate missing persons that are on various
hotlists. We also retain vehicle and plate images for a period of time to perform
searches when investigating reported crimes. All of this data is secure, not sold to
3rd parties, and only used for law enforcement purposes by authorized personnel.
This page shows our ALPR policies and usage statistics so that you may
understand how this technology is used to help keep your community safer.
ALPR Terms
Hotlist
A list of known license plates and vehicles of interest, which are stolen, wanted
for or potentially associated with unlawful activity or missing persons.
Read Record
A record of a plate that was read that was not associated with any hotlist at
the time it was captured.
Hit Record
A record of a plate that was read that matched a plate number on a hotlist and
generated an alert.
What is detected
License plate image, vehicle image, plate text, date, time and location.
What is not detected
Facial images, people, gender, or race.
Acceptable Use Policy
Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is agency-owned and is
never sold to 3rd parties.
Prohibited Uses
Harassment, intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (race, sex,
religion, etc....), personal use.
Data Policy Access
All data searches require a valid search reason or case number which is
captured in audit logs and stored indefinitely.
Hotlist Policy
Hotlist alerts are required to be human-verified before taking action.
