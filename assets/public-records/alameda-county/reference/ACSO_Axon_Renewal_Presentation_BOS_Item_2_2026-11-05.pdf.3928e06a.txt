Background:
The Alameda County Sheriff’s Office first implemented body-worn cameras in 
2012. In 2017, following an extensive evaluation and testing process, the 
Agency selected Axon as its body-worn camera provider and equipped all 
sworn personnel with cameras. 
As part of its ongoing commitment to safety, accountability, transparency, 
and effective public service, the Agency has evaluated opportunities to 
expand its use of the Axon ecosystem to improve evidence management, 
enhance operational efficiency, support officer and community safety, and 
strengthen service to the communities we serve.

Taser 7 to Taser 10:
The TASER 10 represents the next generation of the Agency’s less-lethal energy weapon platform, providing 
greater range, more deployment opportunities, and increased flexibility compared with the TASER 7.
TASER 7 
•
Maximum range of approximately 25 feet
•
Two cartridges / up to 4 deployed probes
•
Probe spread largely determined by cartridge and distance
TASER 10
•
Maximum range of 45 feet
•
Up to 10 individually targeted probes
•
Individual probe deployment allows the user to establish the 
appropriate probe spread
•
Additional probes provide opportunities to correct an 
ineffective or missed deployment without reloading
•
Audible and visual Warning Alert provides an additional 
opportunity to seek compliance without deploying probes 
•
Designed to provide officers additional time and distance during 
critical incidents
5
Because of these capabilities, TASER 10 can be used effectively in a broader range of situations, decreasing 
the likelihood of needing another, potentially more damaging force option.
Source: Axon

DRAFT one:
More Time for Community Engagement
By reducing the time personnel spend drafting routine report narratives, Draft One can help return valuable time to the field, allowing deputies to remain more available for calls for service, problem-solving, and meaningful 
interaction with the communities they serve.
deputy Remains Responsible 
Every narrative must be reviewed, corrected, and affirmatively approved by the deputy. Important information like observations and decisions are 
purposely left to be completed by the deputy, who remains responsible for the accuracy of the final report.
Evidence-Based Drafting 
Drafts are generated primarily from the recorded audio and transcript of the incident.
Human Review is Required 
Draft One cannot independently submit a report. Personnel must review the narrative and provide required information.
Transparency & Auditability
Draft One is recorded in an audit trail, documenting when the tool was used, by whom, and the evidence associated with the draft.
Consistency & Report Quality
The technology can assist with organization, grammar, terminology, and consistency.
Supports Existing Review Processes 
AI assistance does not replace supervisory review, investigative review, evidence review, or other existing quality-control and accountability requirements.
CJIS Security Compliant

Axon assistant:
Axon Assistant provides real-time, two-way voice translation directly through the Axon Body 4 
camera, helping personnel communicate more effectively with community members who speak 
different languages.
Improved Community Access 
Supports communication with individuals who may otherwise experience language barriers during calls for service, traffic stops, investigations, and other public contacts.
Two-Way Communication 
Translates both the deputy’s speech and the community member’s response, allowing for a more natural exchange without relying solely on a separate device.
Transparency & Review 
Translation interactions are captured as part of the body-worn camera recording and transcript, allowing later review and verification.
Supplemental Tool 
Real-Time Translation can assist with routine field communication but does not replace certified interpreters when formal or legally required interpretation is necessary.

Body Worn Camera Livestreaming:
The proposed renewal includes live-streaming capability from Axon body-worn cameras, allowing authorized 
personnel to view an active camera feed during developing incidents when appropriate.
Deputy-Initiated Use 
Deputies may initiate live streaming when circumstances warrant, such as a contentious encounter, an allegation being made against the deputy, or when supervisory assistance is requested.
Supervisor-Initiated Use 
Authorized supervisors or managers may also initiate a live stream when operationally appropriate*
Real-Time Situational Awareness 
Provides supervisors with a direct view of developing events, helping them better understand conditions and provide timely guidance or resources.
Accountability & Transparency 
Can provide an additional real-time perspective during sensitive or disputed encounters while preserving the underlying body-worn camera recording.
* Activation criteria, permissions, access controls, and supervisory expectations will be established through the Agency’s policy development and implementation process prior to deployment.

Why 2026?:
Preserve Current Pricing 
An early renewal allows the Agency to secure 2026 pricing rather than anticipated 2027 pricing, reducing the projected five-year contract cost by 
approximately 21.5%.
Greater Contract Value 
Early renewal increases the negotiated discount and contract credit by approximately 76% compared with waiting until the next pricing cycle.
No Accelerated Payment 
Although the agreement would be renewed early, the first payment would not be due until August 2027, consistent with the timing of the current 
contract.
15

--- page 16 ---
Why 2026?:
Orderly Transition 
Early renewal provides time to implement new technologies deliberately rather than waiting until the existing agreement is nearing expiration.
Time for Policy & Training 
New capabilities will not necessarily be activated immediately. For example, Draft One deployment is planned to be phased through August, allowing 
time for policy development, employee training, and implementation of appropriate safeguards.
Proactive Contract Management 
Addressing the agreement well before its August 2027 expiration provides sufficient time for review, discussion, implementation planning, and an 
orderly transition.
