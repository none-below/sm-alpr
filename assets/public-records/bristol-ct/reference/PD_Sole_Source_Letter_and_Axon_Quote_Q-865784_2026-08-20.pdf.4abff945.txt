Dear Chairman Maikowski and Members of the Board of Finance:

The Bristol Police Department is transitioning from FLOCK, our current license plate reader
(LPR) vendor, upon the expiration of our contract in February 2027. The Department intends to
incorporate these 14 LPR cameras into our existing Axon Technology Suite, allowing all LPR
cameras in the City to operate on a single platform.

The funding for these cameras has already been approved and budgeted. Accordingly, pursuant
to Section 2-1, Proprietary and Sole Source Transactions, of the City of Bristol Purchasing
Manual, please consider this correspondence a formal request to contract with Axon as a sole-
source transaction.

Axon possesses unique proprietary technology and functionality that allows its LPR system to
integrate directly with the Department's existing Axon platform and technology ecosystem.

Utilizing Axon will provide a unified operating system, improve interoperability, and maximize
the City's existing investment while eliminating the need to maintain separate LPR platforms.

For these reasons, the Department respectfully requests approval to proceed with Axon as the
sole-source provider for these 14 LPR cameras.

Thank you for your consideration.

Respectfully,
