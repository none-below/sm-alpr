Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the following records concerning Grant Agreement
BSCC 1150-23, the Organized Retail Theft Prevention Grant Program award to the Daly City Police Department (Submittable application id.
41327693), with partner agencies the San Bruno Police Department and the San Mateo Police Department.
1. All Quarterly Progress Reports submitted to the BSCC under Grant Agreement BSCC 1150-23 from the start of the grant service period
(October 1, 2023) to the present, including any attachments, data tables, or narrative supplements submitted with them.
2. All invoices, reimbursement requests, and Form 201 submissions under BSCC 1150-23, together with any supporting documentation submitted
to or requested by the BSCC to substantiate claimed costs.
3. All budget modification requests, purchase pre-authorization requests, and sole-source or alternative-procurement requests submitted
under BSCC 1150-23, together with the BSCC's approvals, denials, or responses to each.
4. The policy documents uploaded as attachments to Submittable application id. 41327693, specifically the files titled
SMPD_Policy_ALPRs.pdf; SMPD_Policy._Public_Safety_Video_Surveillance_System.pdf; SMPD_Bias-Based_Policing.pdf; and
SMPD.StarChase_Pursuit_Management_System_Procedure.pdf. As the application's confidentiality notice states, all documents submitted as part
of an Organized Retail Theft Prevention Grant Program proposal are public documents subject to the Public Records Act.
5. All records of BSCC monitoring of BSCC 1150-23, including desk review records, on-site monitoring visit reports or checklists, findings,
corrective action requests, and any program or financial audit records.
6. The executed Memorandum of Understanding among the Daly City, San Bruno, and San Mateo Police Departments referenced in BSCC 1150-23,
including the signature page and execution dates.
7. All correspondence between the BSCC and the Daly City Police Department, the San Bruno Police Department, or the San Mateo Police
Department concerning Grant Agreement BSCC 1150-23, from October 1, 2023 to the present, including communications from the assigned BSCC
Field Representative.
8. Any guidance, technical assistance materials, bulletins, FAQs, or written direction the BSCC issued to Organized Retail Theft Prevention
Grant Program grantees concerning the use of automated license plate readers, compliance with Civil Code § 1798.90.5 et seq., or the
surveillance-technology and privacy provisions of the authorizing legislation.
9. All documentation submitted to the BSCC in support of desk reviews of invoices under BSCC 1150-23, including any partner-agency level
backup substantiating costs claimed on behalf of the San Bruno Police Department or the San Mateo Police Department.
I request records in electronic format where available. If any portion of a responsive record is withheld, please identify the legal
authority relied upon and produce any reasonably segregable portions.
