Greetings Judy,
 
Thank you, we have received Invoice 2 for ORT 23 Daly City PD 1150-23.  After reviewing 
Invoice 2, there are some corrections that will need to be completed before it can be submitted 
for final review. Please see my suggestions below:
 
SALARIES & BENEFITS:
Top Pay Rate for Officers.  The budget narrative shows the top hourly rate for Officers as 
$122hr; however, Invoice 2 has an Officer listed at $125.712 which exceeds the top hourly rate. 
The expenditures in the budget narrative includes the max amount the grant will reimburse for. 
You can always claim less than the stated pay range, you just can’t claim more. To expense for 
more than the top hourly pay rate, a budget modification will need to be completed first. 
However to move forward with payment, you could also exclude the officer that exceeds the top 
rate from Invoice 2, do a budget mod, and then include the officer on Invoice 3 after the budget 
mod has been approved. Let me know what you would prefer.
 
PROFESSIONAL SERVICES:
Condense expenditures into totals only.  Since San Bruno PD and San Mateo PD don’t 
provide Daly City PD with functional timesheets and both send invoices to Daly City PD for 
payment, you don’t have to list out all the individuals employees hours and rates in the 
Professional Services expenditures description cell. Please condense down the expenditures 
and include only a total for San Mateo PD, a total for San Bruno PD, and an overall total for 
both. This will streamline the invoice and avoid potential budget mods. See example below:
San Bruno PD $33,261, San Mateo PD $49,942, Total  = $83,203
 
Equipment/Fixed Assets:
Message Board Trailer . Please list expenditure as ALPR Trailer or ALPR Message Board 
Trailer, or something similar with ALPR in the title to help better align with budget narrative line-
item, ALPR Trailer. Please check the total for the Trailer Hitch Mount  since the amount was 
$10 off. Also please add all the totals for the trailer and hitch mount together and provide one 
overall total to better align with the budget narrative line-item, ALPR Trailer. See example 
below: 
#1, #2 = One (1) ALPR Message Board Trailer: $16,831.63
