Description: Staffing challenges in the Sheriff’s Office continue to impact more effective pursuit of property 
crimes.  The agency continues to recruit and utilize overtime.  Other sub-grantee agencies have reported

The Sheriff’s Office and sub-grantee agencies are consistently running the same data extracts from 
respective databases for grant reporting, but some inconsistencies still exist in how we may identify ORT 
cases.  Organized retail theft incidents reported above reflect the PC 490.4 cases specifically.  All other 
theft-related cases could start with a different offense code and only through reviewing case narratives or 
revisiting cases later in time may we confirm they are ORT-related.  We hope to work with our evaluator to 
confirm a consistent method to collect the data, train staff as each agency onboards to the C3.ai LE platform 
and updated data collection methods to hopefully yield more consistent data, and eventually leverage C3.ai 
Ex Machina to pull the data using a single automated format.
