1. Additional Narrative. Please provide any additional information necessary to understand the lead 
agency and its associated partners activities as it relates to ORT, MVT, MVAT, or cargo theft prior to 
the grant project. If providing additional details in reference to a section within this report, please cite 
relevant sections.  
 
Description: On Metrics for ORT and MVT/MVAT: There currently exists a challenge in the literature 
regarding the most appropriate way of measuring ORT at any point prior to the prosecutorial decision 
to pursue a case with an ORT related charge. Due to this conceptual challenge and faced with the 
need to calculate short-term statistics that may be collected at the moment of occurrence or shortly 
thereafter, we interpret ORT and MVT/MVAT aggregate figures as potential incidents/arrests/referrals 
related to ORT and MVT/MVAT. An alternative interpretation could also be ORT-adjacent or 
MVT/MVAT adjacent events. Incidents were calculated using CAD calls with call types that were 
related to the category in question. Any incidents that were communicated to the police departments 
through alternative avenues are not included in these estimates. In order to most appropriately count 
cases and arrests related to ORT, we implemented textual analysis that interpreted in case/arrest 
charge description fields terms closely associated with retail theft, grand/petty theft, and shoplifting. 
Additionally, we cross-checked these numbers with the specific charge numbers associated with those 
calls. Similarly, for MVT/MVAT, we identified the case/arrest charge descriptions that were related to 
vehicle theft, vehicle accessory theft, and tampering with vehicles. Throughout this reporting process 
and hereafter, we will continue to analyze the methodology used to count these events for reliability 
and consistency. Acknowledging the challenges mentioned above, we include below some additional 
numbers that refer to the specific number of arrests and referrals made with an Organized Retail Theft 
charge: Incidents (54), Arrests (55) and DA referrals (59). Incidents for ORT-specific numbers were 
calculated using the total number of cases with offenses specifically related to ORT and are not 
calculated using CAD incoming calls. Full-time FTE times are calculated based on total on-scene time 
for each ORT adjacent or MVT/MVAT incident plus a one-hour estimation on cases that resulted in DA 
referrals (referrals include arrests) per case due to additional time needed for staff to complete 
required documentation and investigative steps for cases that result in an arrest or referral.

SECTION G: Additional Narrative 
 
1. Additional Narrative. Please provide any additional information necessary to detail the project’s 
progress during the reporting period. Any additional information that is project specific, which may help 
inform project progress, may be included here. If providing additional details in reference to a section 
within this report, please cite relevant sections.  
 
Description: On Metrics for ORT and MVT/MVAT: There currently exists a challenge in the literature 
regarding the most appropriate way of measuring ORT at any point prior to the prosecutorial decision 
to pursue a case with an ORT related charge. Due to this conceptual challenge and faced with the 
need to calculate short-term statistics that may be collected at the moment of occurrence or shortly 
thereafter, we interpret ORT and MVT/MVAT aggregate figures as potential incidents/arrests/referrals 
related to ORT and MVT/MVAT. An alternative interpretation could also be ORT-adjacent or 
MVT/MVAT adjacent events. Incidents were calculated using CAD calls with call types that were 
related to the category in question. Any incidents that were communicated to the police departments 
through alternative avenues are not included in these estimates. In order to most appropriately count 
cases and arrests related to ORT, we implemented textual analysis that interpreted in case/arrest 
charge description fields terms closely associated with retail theft, grand/petty theft, and shoplifting. 
Additionally, we cross-checked these numbers with the specific charge numbers associated with those 
calls. Similarly, for MVT/MVAT, we identified the case/arrest charge descriptions that were related to 
vehicle theft, vehicle accessory theft, and tampering with vehicles. Throughout this reporting process 
and hereafter, we will continue to analyze the methodology used to count these events for reliability 
and consistency. Acknowledging the challenges mentioned above, we include below some additional 
numbers that refer to the specific number of arrests and referrals made with an Organized Retail Theft 
charge: Incidents (10), Arrests (15) and DA referrals (15). Incidents for ORT-specific numbers were 
calculated using the total number of cases with offenses specifically related to ORT and are not 
calculated using CAD incoming calls.  Full-time FTE times are calculated based on total on-scene time 
for each ORT adjacent or MVT/MVAT incident plus a one-hour estimation on cases that resulted in DA 
referrals (referrals include arrests) per case due to additional time needed for staff to complete 
required documentation and investigative steps for cases that result in an arrest or referral.
