1. Challenges: Describe the challenges encountered during the reporting period, as well any as 
efforts to overcome the challenges. 
Description: DCPD is experiencing delays in the hiring process for the CSO position due to 
extensive background processes that must be completed. DCPD is working to address the hiring 
issue and plans to have a CSO in place in Q4.
SMPD noticed through their on-going communication with retailers, that they greatly appreciate

are working with our ALPR vendor to increase the permitting and installation process to ensure this 
equipment is installed in a timely manner. We are also all experiencing issues with CIBRS in 
relation to reporting.

SECTION G: Additional Narrative
1. Additional Narrative. Please provide any additional information necessary to detail the project’s 
progress during the reporting period. Any additional information that is project specific, which may 
help inform project progress, may be included here. If providing additional details in reference to a 
section within this report, please cite relevant sections. 
Description: On Metrics for ORT and MVT/MVAT: There currently exists a challenge in the 
literature regarding the most appropriate way of measuring ORT at any point prior to the 
prosecutorial decision to pursue a case with an ORT related charge. Due to this conceptual 
challenge and faced with the need to calculate short-term statistics that may be collected at the 
moment of occurrence or shortly thereafter, we interpret ORT and MVT/MVAT aggregate figures as 
potential incidents/arrests/referrals related to ORT and MVT/MVAT. An alternative interpretation 
could also be ORT-adjacent or MVT/MVAT-adjacent events. Incidents were calculated using CAD 
calls with call types that were related to the category in question. Any incidents that were 
communicated to the police departments through alternative avenues are not included in these 
estimates. In order to most appropriately count cases and arrests related to ORT, we implemented 
textual analysis that interpreted in case/arrest charge description fields terms closely associated 
with retail theft, grand/petty theft, and shoplifting. Additionally, we cross-checked these numbers 
with the specific charge numbers associated with those calls. Similarly, for MVT/MVAT, we 
identified the case/arrest charge descriptions that were related to vehicle theft, vehicle accessory 
theft, and tampering with vehicles. Throughout this reporting process and hereafter, we will 
continue to analyze the methodology used to count these events for reliability and consistency. 
Acknowledging the challenges mentioned above, we include below some additional numbers that 
refer to the specific number of arrests and referrals made with an Organized Retail Theft 
charge: Cases (34), Arrests (35) and DA referrals (23). Incidents for ORT-specific numbers were 
calculated using the total number of cases with offenses specifically related to ORT and are not 
calculated using CAD incoming calls.
