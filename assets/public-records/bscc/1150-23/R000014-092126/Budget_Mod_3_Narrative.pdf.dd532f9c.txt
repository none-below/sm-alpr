Narrative 
1. We realized a savings of $31,855 in the overtime or straight-time pay for the Organized Retail Theft Task 
Force (ORTTF) as the actual overtime/straight-time expenses for Year 1 were lower than the budgeted 
amount. We would like to reallocate the $31,855 savings to Equipment/Fixed Assets to purchase a 
monitor system (hardware, software, installation fees, etc.) for the real-time crime center (RTCC) to 
enhance the department’s capabilities in addressing and preventing organized retail crime. 
 
 
2. Given the significant and sustained decline in motor vehicle thefts (MVT) and motor vehicle accessory 
thefts (MVAT) observed over the past year, we are requesting to redirect funds allocated the ORTTF 
details, MVT/MVAT details, as well as tracking devices (Equipment/Fixed Assets), toward the RTCC 
monitor system. In addition, as the need for trackers decreases, we would like to reallocate a portion of 
the funds earmarked for trackers to the RTCC. We will continue to closely monitor crime data and adjust 
as necessary if the trend reverses. 
a. $55,930 from the overtime or straight-time pay for ORTTF details (for monitoring merchandise 
equipped with trackers to be placed in the bait car) to the RTCC monitor system 
b. $50,870 from the overtime or straight-time pay for MVT/MVAT details to the RTCC monitor 
system 
c. $7,482 from tracking devices to the RTCC monitor system
