Equipment/Fixed Assets (security camera systems). 
 
Narrative 
1. We realized a savings of $76,722 in salaries and benefits for the Community Service Officer position as 
the actual expenses for Year 2 were below the budgeted amount. We would like to reallocate the savings 
to the Equipment/Fixed Assets category to fund the installation of security camera systems in areas with 
high rates of retail theft, organized retail theft (ORT), motor vehicle theft (MVT), and motor vehicle 
accessory theft (MVAT). These cameras will be placed strategically throughout the city to live stream 
videos to the department’s Real-Time Intelligence Center (RTIC) and record footage for investigative 
purposes, increasing the likelihood of capturing individuals involved in retail theft, ORT, MVT, and MVAT. 
2. We realized a savings of $61,022 in straight-time or overtime pay allocated to the ORT Task Force as the 
actual costs for Year 2 were lower than the budgeted amount. We would like to reallocate the savings to 
the Equipment/Fixed Assets category for the security camera systems. 
3. We realized a savings of $16,890 in straight-time or overtime pay for the ORT Task Force as the actual 
expenses for Year 2 were lower than the budgeted amount. We would like to relocate the savings to the 
straight-time or overtime pay for the MVT/MVAT details. 
4. We realized a savings of $4,032 in straight-time or overtime pay for the Administrative Assistant position 
as the actual costs for Year 2 were lower than the budgeted amount. We would like to reallocate the 
savings to the Equipment/Fixed Assets category for the security camera systems.
