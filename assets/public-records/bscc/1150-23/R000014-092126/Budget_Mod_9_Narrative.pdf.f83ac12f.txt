DALY CITY 
Budget Modification 
 
Transfer 
Moving the following to create new line items for Daly City PD: 
 
 
Camera trailer 
o Move $56,030 from the Salaries & Benefits category (Community Service Officer) to 
Equipment/Fixed Assets line for a camera trailer. 
o Move $43,970 from the Salaries & Benefits category (ORT Task Force deployments, training, 
investigations, and other related activities) to the Equipment/Fixed Assets category for a 
camera trailer. 
 
Camera integration devices 
o Move $10,792 from the Salaries & Benefits category (ORT Task Force deployments, training, 
investigations, and other related activities) to the Equipment/Fixed Assets category for 
camera integration devices. 
o Move $19,208 from the Salaries & Benefits category (MVT/MVAT details) to the 
Equipment/Fixed Assets category for camera integration devices. 
 
Narrative 
 
We realized savings of $56,030 in salaries and benefits for the Community Service Officer 
position as the actual expenses for Years 1 through 3 were below the budgeted amount. We would 
like to reallocate the savings to the Equipment/Fixed Assets category to purchase a camera trailer 
to support efforts to address ORT and MVT/MVAT. 
 
We are requesting to redirect a portion of the remaining funds earmarked for the ORT Task Force 
and MVT/MVAT details toward a camera trailer and camera integration devices. 
o We would like to reallocate $43,970 from the overtime and straight-time pay for ORT Task 
Force deployments, training, investigations, and other related activities line item to the 
Equipment/Fixed Assets budget for the camera trailer. 
o We would like to reallocate $10,792 from the overtime and straight-time pay for ORT Task 
Force deployments, training, investigations, and other related activities line item to the 
Equipment/Fixed Assets budget to fund a rebate program for camera integration devices, 
which would allow participating businesses to connect their existing security cameras 
directly to our Real-Time Intelligence Center, giving the department access to live feeds 
during emergencies. 
o We would like to reallocate $19,208 from the overtime and straight-time pay for MVT/MVAT 
details, training, investigations, and other related activities line item to the 
Equipment/Fixed Assets budget for the camera integration devices.
