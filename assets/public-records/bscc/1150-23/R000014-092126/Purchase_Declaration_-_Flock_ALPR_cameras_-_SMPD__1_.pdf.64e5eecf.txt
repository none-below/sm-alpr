April 12th, 2024 
 
Board of State and Community Corrections 
Organized Retail Theft Program Grant Administration 
Re: Purchase Declaration for Purchase of Flock ALPR Cameras 
 
 
To Whom it May Concern; 
 
This letter serves to provide a purchase declaration from the San Mateo Police Department for 
our intended purchase of 15 Flock ALPR cameras as part of the Organized Retail Theft Program 
grant. These cameras will be used for services directly associated with the BSCC Organized 
Retail Theft Grant project, and they are essential to the success of our BSCC Organized Retail 
Theft Grant project. The ALPR cameras are being leased per the contract, as purchasing them is 
not an option.
