The Redwood City Police Department is requesting for authorization to install License Plate Reader cameras at the intersection of Woodside 
Road and Broadway in Redwood City, California. The proposed installation includes two (2) cameras. One camera is to be mounted on 
Caltrans pole number 25542, and the second camera is to be mounted on pole number 25612. All installation work will be performed by 
Redwood City Police Department personnel in accordance with applicable standards and regulations. 
 
The proposed system is completely self-powered and does not require any external electrical or communication connections. The unit includes 
an integrated solar panel and internal battery system. No trenching, wiring, conduit, or connection to Caltrans power or infrastructure is 
required. 
 
The camera and solar panel assembly will be mounted using non-penetrating stainless-steel hose clamps. The installation will not require 
drilling, bolting, welding, or any modification to the pole. The mounting method is fully reversible and will not damage or alter Caltrans property. 
 
The total weight of the assembly is not to exceed 21 lbs, and further specifications can be provided upon request. The equipment will be 
installed at a height that does not interfere with traffic operations, sightlines, or maintenance access. 
 
The installation is temporary in nature and can be removed promptly upon request.
