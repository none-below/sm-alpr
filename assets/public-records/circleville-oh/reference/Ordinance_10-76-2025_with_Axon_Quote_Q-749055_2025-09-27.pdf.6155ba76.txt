AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR AND/OR THE DIRECTOR OF 
PUBLIC SAFETY TO ENTER INTO A RENEW AL CONTRACT WITH AXON 
ENTERPRISE, INC., A SOLE SOURCE PROVIDER AND STATE BID 
CONTRACTOR, TO PURCHASE TEN (10) IN-CAR POLICE CRUISER VIDEO 
RECORDING SYSTEMS, TWENTY-SEVEN (27) BODY WORN CAMERAS, 
TWENTY-SIX (26) TASER 10 ENERGY WEAPONS, ONE (1), DRONE, TWO (2) 
OUTPOST CAMERAS, ONE (1) INTERVIEW ROOM SYSTEM, ONE (1) FUSUS 
CORE ELITE AND ALL REQUIRED LICENSES FOR CIRCLEVILLE POLICE 
DEPARTMENT TO OPERATE AND MAINTAIN ALL AFOREMENTIONED 
EQUIPMENT, AND DECLARING AN EMERGENCY. 
WHEREAS, this purchase is necessary to outfit the Circleville Police Department cruiser 
fleet with updated in-car video recording systems, to provide body worn cameras for the 
uniform patrol operational packages, and to provide licenses for the Evidence.com platform 
where digital evidence will be stored, monitored, and retained by the Circleville Police 
Department. 
WHEREAS, Circleville police officers are currently equipped with Axon body-worn 
cameras, tasers and in-car video recording systems; and 
WHEREAS, additional body-worn cameras, technology, and investigative tools are needed 
for said department; and 
WHEREAS, under the ten-year (10) contract, Axon will provide the City with cameras, 
licensing, storage, technology devices and maintenance services for Circleville police. 
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF 
CIRCLEVILLE, COUNTY OF PICKAWAY, STATE OF OHIO AS FOLLOWS: 
SECTION I. That the Mayor and/or the Director of Public Safety are hereby authorized to 
enter into a contract with Axon Enterprise Inc., a sole source provider and State bid 
contractor, for the purchase of ten (10) in-car police cruiser video recording systems, twenty-
seven (27) body worn cameras, twenty-six (26) taser 10 energy weapons, one (1 ), drone, two 
(2) outpost cameras, one (1) interview room system, one (1) fusus core elite and all required 
licenses for Circleville Police Department to operate and maintain all aforementioned 
equipment, in the amount of$1 ,674,139.85, which will be paid over a ten (10) year period. A 
copy of the Quote Summary from Axon Enterprise, Inc., is attached hereto as Exhibit "A." 
SECTION II. That funds for said purchase shall be paid from Fund No. 101-0130-55020. 
SECTION III. That it is found and determined that all formal actions of this Council 
concerning and relating to the adoption of this Ordinance were adopted in an open meeting of 
this Council, and that all deliberations of this Council and any of its committees that resulted 
in such formal action were in meetings open to the public in compliance with all legal 
requirements. 
SECTION IV. That this Ordinance is hereby declared to be an emergency measure necessary 
for the immediate preservation of the public health, safety, and welfare of the City of 
Circleville, and for the further reason that this measure is necessary to continue the 
uninterrupted operation of the Axon equipment and related platform, and this Ordinance shall 
become immediately effective upon receiving the affirmative vote of two-thirds of all 
members elected to Council and approval of the Mayor, otherwise from and after the earliest 
period allowed by law.
