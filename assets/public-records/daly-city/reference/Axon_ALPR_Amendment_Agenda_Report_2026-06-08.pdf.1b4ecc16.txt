Meeting Date: June 8, 2026 
 
Subject: Authorize the City Manager to Execute an Amendment to the Police Department’s 
Axon Agreement to Include their Automated License Plate Reader Cameras 
 
Recommended Action 
Staff recommends Council authorize the City Manager or designee to execute an amendment to the 
Police Departments Axon Master Services and Purchasing Agreement, to include Axon’s 
Automated License Plate Reader (ALPR) and camera solutions through Measure Q funding that 
was previously appropriated for related technologies.  
 
Background  
In November 2023, City Council authorized our City’s license plate reader program expansion to 
include 88 fixed ALPR cameras between two contracts, with one expiring May 2027, and the other 
expiring December 2027.  In June of 2025, the Police Department entered into a 10-year agreement 
with Axon for police technology. Axon recently released a fixed camera with license plate reader 
technology that would integrate with our current software, improving efficiency for use in our 
intelligence center. Axon has offered competitive pricing and an opportunity to pilot the new 
technology at no cost.  
 
The Police Department complies with California Civil Code Section 1798.90.5 et seq. regarding 
ALPR technologies. Public agencies are required to provide an opportunity for public comment at a 
regularly scheduled public meeting of the agency’s governing body before implementing or 
modifying an existing program. 
 
Discussion 
Our City has experienced positive results for community safety and crime reduction because of our 
license plate reader program. We currently have 88 license plate readers strategically placed 
throughout the city focused on crime prevention. Over the last two years, they have assisted with 
nearly 400 cases, including recovery of missing people as well as arrests of individuals involved in 
criminal activity. 
 
Axon has been a provider of hardware and software for our department for many years, and in June 
2025, Council supported a 10-year renewal agreement, to include future access to newly developed 
technology. We have utilized Axon dash cameras with license plate reader technology for years, 
and they recently released their fixed camera with license plate reader technology. Their fixed 
solution operates in the same software as the mobile solution and more efficiently integrates in the 
platform heavily utilized by officers in the field and our intelligence center. All security and policy 
requirements governing department use will apply to these systems, ensuring best practice and 
adherence to state and local laws.

We are receiving two license plate reader camera options from Axon as part of the agreement. The 
Outpost is a compact solution, like our existing solution, and the Lightpost is a larger pan, tilt, 
zoom (PTZ) camera that mounts on existing city light poles. Both solutions allow live-view 
monitoring for critical awareness by our intelligence center, enhancing officer response for 
improved safety, while also utilizing vehicle license plate reader technology.  
 
Axon is offering competitive pricing and a no-cost pilot through a phased approach for the 
Outpost/Lighpost solution. We will receive 40 Outpost cameras and 6 Lightpost PTZ cameras FY 
26/27 for trial, and the remaining 50 Outpost cameras for trial in FY 27/28. We also receive 9 
donor Outpost cameras we can share with community businesses or stakeholders to address 
targeted enforcement as needed.  In total we are receiving 99 Outpost cameras and 6 Lightpost PTZ 
cameras.  
 
During the trial phases, we will evaluate the reliability, performance and integration with existing 
software for efficiency, allowing us to determine the best solution for camera and license plate 
reader technology moving forward. Our existing license plate reader contracts and this agreement 
for the Axon solution align following the trial period so no additional funding is expected. This 
agreement also covers upgrades and support costs for the equipment throughout the agreement’s 
full term.  This agreement is structured to provide an estimated discount of $2,659,434 over 9 
years, as well as additional savings from reduced contract costs compared to our existing solution.  
 
Fiscal Impact 
This agreement includes a no-cost pilot of Axon’s Fixed ALPR solutions through June of 2027. If 
the pilot is successful, the first payment of $120,000 will be due in June of 2027. The subsequent 
payment of $240,000 will be in September of 2028, followed by a 4% COLA adjustment each year 
thereafter for the remainder of the agreement. The total estimated cost of the 9-year agreement with 
Axon is $2,005,283. Currently, funding is available in FY27 Measure Q funds appropriated during 
the last budget cycle to support the initial $120,000 payment. The remaining years of the agreement 
will be included in future budget requests. Staff recommends co-terming this agreement with the 
Department’s existing 10-year agreement, which was executed in June 2025.
