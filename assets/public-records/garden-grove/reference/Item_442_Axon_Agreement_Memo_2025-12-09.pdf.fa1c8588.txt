OBJECTIVE 
 
To obtain City Council approval of a 10-year agreement with Axon Enterprise, Inc. (Axon) for 
public safety software and related components in the amount of $464,005 in the first year, 
with 3% annual increase in subsequent years, for a total 10-year cost of $5,314,814 for the 
current Records Management System (RMS); to upgrade, maintain, and manage the 
Department’s interview room software, current body worn cameras (BWC) and in-car video 
system (IVS), the jail management system, and the property and evidence system; and to 
add the Axon Fusus Real-Time Crime Center platform. 
 
BACKGROUND 
 
In 2019, City Council approved an agreement with Axon for the purchase and deployment of 
a Body Worn Camera/In-car Video system (BWC/IVS) with Evidence.com digital evidence 
management. Thus far, the system has exceeded expectations and the Department has 
experienced exceptional customer support and service from the vendor.  
 
In 2021, the Police Department signed a contract with Axon for a RMS/CAD system that would 
integrate into the Axon Evidence.com digital evidence management system, which is 
proprietary technology. The RMS portion of the Axon system has an interview data file 
component for memorializing investigative interviews. It also contains a professional 
standards module, a jail management module, a property and evidence module, and a third-
party video playback feature, all of which directly integrate into the Axon Evidence.com 
system. Another advantage of the Axon ecosystem is the ability to share documents and 
digital evidence directly with the district attorney’s office.  
 
The Axon system is cloud-based, which allows for significant cost savings on servers, storage, 
and maintenance performed by City IT staff. Per IT staff, cloud-based systems are preferred 
as they are less prone to corruption and are not as labor intensive for IT personnel.

DISCUSSION 
 
In November 2025, members of the Garden Grove Police Department began discussions with 
Axon regarding a new contract as the current contract is set to expire in March 2026. During 
these discussions, Axon advised the Police Department of a new pricing model that they were 
implementing to all new customers and contract renewals. The new pricing model consists of 
re-evaluating the cost of the current technology deployed by the agency as the starting point 
of the new contract and then including a 3% year over year increase to each subsequent year 
of the contract until the term of the contract expires.  
 
In these discussions, the Police Department requested to add Axon Fusus to the project, a 
new technology from Axon that will enhance the overall public safety plan moving forward. 
Axon Fusus is a Real-Time Crime Center (RTCC) platform, which integrates video and other 
data from various sources, including public and private cameras, drones, and Axon's own 
body cameras, into a single view. It helps improve situational awareness and incident 
response for law enforcement and first responders by connecting different public safety 
technologies to provide a unified and comprehensive operational picture.  
 
Because of our continued partnership, Axon has agreed to provide the Police Department with 
five (5) “Outpost” fixed LPR cameras, as well as one (1) year of on-site technology services 
through their “TAM” (Technical Account Manager) program at no additional cost. During the 
contract review, the Axon team and the Police Department strategically worked to structure 
the contract in a manner that allowed us to take advantage of Axon’s bundle pricing, which 
resulted in a cost-savings of $3,617,717.49 over the life of the 10-year contract. In order to 
take advantage of the bundle price savings and the free services outlined above, Axon is 
requesting that the contract be executed by December 31, 2025. The new contract would be 
effective on April 1, 2026. 
 
Axon offers advanced technology and continuity of services, which helps the Police 
Department to efficiently continue and improve operations. Therefore, the Police Department 
is requesting that the City Council determine it to be in the best interest of the City to forego 
the bidding process and select Axon Enterprise, Inc. as the vendor for this service, pursuant 
to Garden Grove Municipal Code 2.50.060(G). 
 
FINANCIAL IMPACT 
 
Axon’s pricing structure for its technology and evidence management services is influenced 
by multiple market-driven factors, including labor and manufacturing costs, software 
development expenses, and continuous technological innovations and advancements. These 
factors fluctuate regularly and have a direct impact on the overall cost of providing and 
maintaining Axon’s integrated public safety platform. 
 
To mitigate the effect of future cost escalations and to secure favorable long-term pricing, 
staff recommends approval of a 10-year contract term. The extended term provides cost 
stability and predictability, ensuring the City locks in current pricing for mission-critical 
technology and equipment replacements throughout the agreement. This approach also 
avoids the need for more frequent contract renewals that would likely result in higher annual 
costs due to inflation, new product cycles, and market adjustments.

The contract will cost $460,005 in the first year and includes 3% annual increases, for a total 
amount of $5,314,814 for all equipment, software licenses, cloud data storage, maintenance, 
and system upgrades over the term of the agreement. 
 
The Axon Fusus Real-Time Crime Center platform along with enhancements to the existing 
services, equipment, and software, will add $300,000 per year to the current Axon contract. 
The Police Department’s existing operating budget for the Axon contract is $165,321.22 in 
FY2025-26 and $184,151.24 in FY 2026-27.  To approve this amended contract, an additional 
appropriation of $300,000 will be required for each of these fiscal years.  The additional 
funding for FY2025-26 and FY2026-27 will be provided through SLESF funds (Fund 205).   
 
The ongoing annual additional funding needs will be incorporated into future budget planning 
and may impact the General Fund if alternative funding sources are not identified. 
 
RECOMMENDATION 
 
It is recommended that the City Council: 
 
 
Authorize the request to dispense of the bidding process in the best interest 
of the City pursuant to Garden Grove Municipal Code 2.50.060(G); and 
 
 
Approve Amendment No. 1  in the amount of $5,314,814 to the Axon Master 
Services and Purchasing Agreement to extend the term for 10 years; and 
 
 
Approve additional allocation and appropriation in the amount of $300,000 
for Fiscal Year 2025-26 and Fiscal Year 2026-27 in Fund 205; and 
 
 
Authorize the City Manager, or designee, to execute the agreement, and 
make minor modifications as appropriate thereto.
