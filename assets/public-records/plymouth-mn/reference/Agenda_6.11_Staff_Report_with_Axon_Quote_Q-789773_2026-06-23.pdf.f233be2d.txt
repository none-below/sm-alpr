1. Action Requested: 
Approve the resolution authorizing the city to enter into an agreement with Axon 
Enterprises for the Axon Unified Technology Platform. 
 
2. Background: 
The police department is requesting council approval of an agreement with Axon 
Enterprises to implement the Axon Unified Technology Platform, a comprehensive and 
consolidated system that will support the police department’s operational efficiency, 
evidence management and long-term technology sustainability for the next 10 years. 
This project represents the police department’s goal to consolidate public safety 
systems under a unified, forward-looking infrastructure. The police department’s current 
body worn camera and squad camera system is due to be replaced in 2028, which 
triggered staff to begin a comprehensive analysis of its existing system and capabilities 
while considering alternative options.  
Rather than expanding disparate systems or adding isolated technology components, 
this initiative focuses on consolidation, integration and elimination of redundant 
platforms. The Axon ecosystem provides a single, seamless environment that supports 
frontline response, digital evidence, investigative workflow and community transparency 
initiatives. The technology has been proven throughout the country with more than 1 
million users in their evidence management system. It is utilized successfully by 
numerous police agencies in Hennepin County, such as Hennepin County Sheriff's 
Office, Minnetonka Police Department, Brooklyn Park Police Department, New Hope

Police Department and others.  
Current Challenges 
The police department currently maintains multiple standalone systems for video 
capture, data storage, digital evidence and media management. These systems 
operate independently and require separate maintenance, training, support contracts 
and hardware life cycles.  
This fragmented approach results in: 
• Inefficient workflows and duplicative data entry 
• Inconsistent digital evidence storage processes 
• Increased IT and administrative overhead 
• Limited interoperability between body worn cameras, squad car cameras, 
Tasers, drones and evidence platforms 
• Delayed implementation of system changes to meet emerging demands and 
legislative requirements across numerous software platforms (e.g., video 
retention, redaction standards, transparency reporting). 
 
As public safety technology evolves rapidly, the need for a modern, consolidated, 
cloud-based system is critical. 
Proposed Solution: Axon Unified Technology Platform:  
The Axon solution provides an integrated suite of tools that operate within a single 
cloud-based environment. Key components include: 
1. Body-Worn Cameras: High-reliability cameras with automatic triggers and seamless 
upload to the digital evidence system. 
2. Squad / In-Car Camera Systems: Fully integrated with body-worn cameras, 
allowing synchronized playback and simplified retention management. 
3. Conducted Energy Weapon (Taser) Integration: Taser 10 and Taser 7 devices 
integrate with cameras and evidence systems, capturing use data and event details 
automatically. 
4. Drone Technology & Drone Program Management: Axon Air provides: 
• Secure livestreaming 
• Flight-tracking 
• Automated storage of flight footage 
• Full integration with digital evidence systems that supports our current and future

drone program expansion. 
 
5. Digital Dictation & Transcription: Axon Auto-Transcribe converts officer audio 
recordings and case narratives into searchable digital text—significantly reducing 
administrative time. 
 
6. Video Redaction Tools: Axon Redaction Studio offers automated, AI-supported 
redaction for video and audio—meeting data-privacy standards and reducing staff time 
currently required to redact video for data requests. 
7. Photo & Media Management: Completely integrated storage, categorization and 
distribution of photos and case-related media, which will drastically reduce committed 
staff time currently required for manually uploading photos and media attachments. 
8. Evidence & Case-Sharing Platform: Axon Evidence allows investigators, 
prosecutors and external agencies to share case files securely, eliminating the need for 
discs, USB drives or disconnected systems, drastically reducing current staff time 
required to manually upload case data to external drives to be mailed to prosecutors.  
 
9. Real Time Crime Center: Axon Fusus provides a real-time operational view of 
connected devices and sensors for police staff that enables them to make more 
informed, real-time decisions. All connected devices appear on a map and authorized 
personnel can monitor active devices and access real-time video feeds to help teams 
stay connected and aware of rapidly unfolding events.  
10. Automated License Plate Reader System: The police department plans to 
research and consider Axon's fully integrated automated license plate reader system. 
This system provides similar real-time information as the police department's existing 
ALPR system at a reduced cost. Additional capability testing must be performed, but 
the system, if implemented, may provide the same service as the department's existing 
system at a reduced cost.  
Benefits of a Unified Platform 
Operational Efficiency 
• Reduced downtime and administrative burden due to fewer logins, systems and 
hardware platforms. 
• Automated workflows for evidence upload, categorization and sharing. 
• Streamlined investigative case management. 
 
Cost-Effectiveness & Long-Term Sustainability 
• Consolidation replaces multiple existing contracts, hardware, and storage

• Predictable multi-year pricing reduces budget volatility. 
• Cloud-based platform avoids major server replacements and IT infrastructure

Improved Officer Safety & Community Transparency 
• Automated recording triggers create more complete documentation of incidents. 
• Integrated drone and camera systems improve situational awareness for officers 
in real time.  
• More efficient response to public data requests and evidentiary requirements.

Fiscal Considerations 
A separate attachment will provide the full cost proposal, which includes: 
• Multi-year subscription pricing 
• Hardware replacement cycles 
• Licensing, storage, and support 
 
Costs are consistent with regional technology investments and represent a shift from 
fragmented technology replacement to a single predictable expenditure for a similar 
cost. 
 
Implementation Schedule 
The police department currently has body-worn cameras and squad car cameras that 
are scheduled to be replaced in 2028. If the department were to transition to the Axon 
platform, it would not implement an updated body-worn camera and squad car camera 
system until 2028, when its existing system is due to be replaced. This approach 
reduces initial costs by not prematurely replacing existing equipment and allows for a 
stepped approach to implementing a new technology platform over two years.   
 
Recommendation 
Approve entering into an agreement with Axon Enterprises for the implementation of 
the Axon Unified Technology Platform, authorizing the City Manager to execute all 
necessary documents and proceed with deployment.

the costs through remaining public safety aid funds.

Overview of the proposed Axon Enterprise Unified Technology Platform 
Axon Enterprise offers an integrated public-safety technology ecosystem intended to consolidate many 
or all of the systems listed above into a single, unified operational platform. The Axon solution places 
body-worn cameras, in-car cameras, Tasers, drones, digital evidence storage, transcription tools, video 
redaction workflows, photo management, ALPR functionality, and case-sharing capabilities on one 
cloud-based infrastructure. 
Key attributes of the Axon Unified Platform include: 
• 
Unified Digital Evidence Ecosystem – Body camera, squad camera, drone video, photos, and 
documents all upload automatically to a single chain-of-custody secure cloud. 
• 
Integrated Software Suite – Features such as transcription, redaction, reporting, and case 
management operate in one system rather than multiple standalone tools. 
• 
Seamless Evidence Sharing – Direct, secure integration with prosecutors, reducing case 
preparation time and eliminating export/import burdens. 
• 
Reduced Hardware Duplication – Cameras, Tasers, drones, and vehicle systems are sourced and 
supported by a single vendor. 
• 
Operational Efficiency Improvements – Automation of uploads, cloud storage, and AI-enabled 
tools substantially reduce officer time spent on administrative tasks. 
• 
Lifecycle Cost Stability – Pricing is consolidated into predictable contractual terms, eliminating 
the staggered renewal timelines and replacement cycles associated with current systems. 
Staff conducted extensive research into the Axon System which included informational sessions with 
Axon Staff, real world demonstration and multiple in person visits to neighboring agencies who are 
currently using the system.  Axon’s full 10-year cost proposal totals $5,682,255.76, which is nearly 
$8,500 less over 10 years than our current systems.   
Not factored into these costs are savings expected to be created by efficiencies in the new integrated 
system resulting in a reduction of staff time.
