From: Brian — 2026-09-25T23:12:18.664285-04:00 (Portal)
Subject: California Public Records Act Request: SJPD ALPR audits 2024–2025, annual data usage
reports, and personal-device / off-duty access rules
To Whom It May Concern:
Pursuant to the California Public Records Act, I hereby request the following records:
1. Each audit of Department use of the Flock automated license plate reader (ALPR) system
that covers any search conducted from January 1, 2024 through April 30, 2025, including
any monthly audit and any periodic, random audit conducted under the Accountability
provisions of Duty Manual section L 4207: each audit report or memorandum, worksheet,
checklist, and list of searches sampled or flagged, and any record of the finding or
corrective action for each flagged search.
2. The internal search history audit of the Flock ALPR system (organization audit) for
calendar year 2025 conducted on February 4, 2026, as described in the Chief's February 9,
2026 memorandum "Automated License Plate Readers Data Usage Protocol Update" (File No.
26-215): the audit report, worksheet, criteria applied, and any list of searches examined
or flagged. Also any comparable audit of calendar year 2024 search history.
3. The Department's annual ALPR data usage reports for calendar years 2024 and 2025, as
submitted, including their audit narratives.
4. The memorandum or directive issued in or about September 2026 restricting access to law
enforcement databases from personal electronic devices; any earlier policy change
restricting access to ALPR or other law enforcement sensitive systems from personal
devices; and any written policy, directive, order, or training material in effect at any
time from January 1, 2024 until that memorandum that addresses access to the Flock ALPR
system, or to Department law enforcement databases generally, from a personal device or
while off duty.
This request does not seek internal affairs or personnel investigation records.
Duty Manual section L 4207 provides that "[a]udit trails shall be maintained by the
Department for a minimum of two (2) years." Some of the period covered by this request is
at or near that minimum, so I ask that the audit records responsive to this request, and
the audit trails they reviewed, be preserved while it is pending.
If any record or portion of a record is withheld, please identify the exemption asserted
for it and release any reasonably segregable portion. Please produce records for each item
as they become available rather than holding the request for the slowest item. I request
electronic copies.
The requested documents will be made available to the general public, and this request is
not being made for commercial purposes.
In the event that there are fees, I would be grateful if you would inform me of the total

electronically, by e-mail attachment if available or CD-ROM if not.
Thank you in advance for your anticipated cooperation in this matter. I look forward to
receiving your response to this request within 10 calendar days, as the statute requires.
Sincerely,
Brian
