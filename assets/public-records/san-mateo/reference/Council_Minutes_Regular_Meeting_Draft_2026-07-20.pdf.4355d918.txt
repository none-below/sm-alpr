NEW BUSINESS
22.
November 3, 2026 Election – Consideration of Placing a ¼-Cent Transactions and Use (Sales) Tax Measure on the 
Ballot –Ordinance Introduction
Staff presented a proposal to place an additional one-quarter-cent transactions and use tax before San Mateo 
voters at the November 3, 2026 election, estimated to generate approximately $7 million annually for general 
City services, with independent audits, public reporting, and local control. 
Public Comment: there were no speakers.
The City Council adopted a resolution calling and consolidating the election and submitting the measure to 
voters; appropriated $158,000 for related election costs; authorized two Council Members to submit ballot 
arguments in favor of the measure; and introduced an ordinance establishing the additional one-quarter-cent 
transactions and use tax, subject to voter approval.
              Moved: Newsom, Seconded: Loraine
              Ayes:     Newsom, Loraine, Fernandez, and Diaz Nash
              Noes:    None
Motion passed 4-0.
Enactment: Resolution No. 84 (2026)
PUBLIC HEARING
23.
November 3, 2026 Election – Placement of Charter Amendments on the Ballot
The City Attorney presented proposed Charter amendments addressing district elections, Council vacancies, 
scheduling of regular Council meetings, urgency ordinances, candidate information, and other outdated 
provisions. 
Public Comment: there were no speakers.
The City Council adopted a resolution placing a measure on the November 3, 2026 ballot to revise the City 
Charter to reflect district elections, update the process for scheduling Council meetings and filling Council 
vacancies, and update or remove other outdated provisions.

REPORTS, ANNOUNCEMENTS, AND COMMITTEE UPDATES
City Manager, City Attorney, and Council Members reported on assignments, liaison roles, and community events. 
Council discussed a potential data center proposal at the San Mateo County Event Center. A majority of the Council 
supported staff contacting San Mateo County for additional information and returning with an update regarding 
potential impacts and possible City actions. Deputy Mayor Fernandez requested that staff research policies adopted by 
other jurisdictions regarding the use of City property, facilities, or resources by U.S. Immigration and Customs 
Enforcement (ICE) for immigration enforcement activities. Following discussion with the City Attorney regarding existing 
state law and the City's welcoming resolution, Council supported staff researching the issue and reporting back before 
consideration of a future agenda item. Council Members also reported on San Mateo Consolidated Fire Department 
activities, the Jerry Hill honorary street naming ceremony, July 4 fireworks enforcement, and upcoming community 
events. The City Manager provided an update on community engagement for the King Center Pool and Resiliency Center 
project.
