Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request
the following records from the San Mateo County Sheriff's Office.
This request seeks existing records only (Gov. Code § 7922.530; Sierra Club v. Superior
Court (2013) 57 Cal.4th 157). Each item is limited to records created on or before the
date of this request.
Scope basis. The Sheriff's Office's Memorandum of Understanding with the Northern
California Regional Intelligence Center ("NCRIC"), produced by the San Mateo Police
Department under Public Records Act request W012376-032026, is executed by Greg Munks,
Sheriff of San Mateo County, on behalf of NCRIC. To the extent the Sheriff's Office holds,
creates, or retains records in an administrative or fiscal capacity for NCRIC, those
records are writings "prepared, owned, used, or retained" by the Sheriff's Office within
the meaning of Gov. Code § 7920.530, regardless of whether NCRIC's day-to-day IT
operations are performed by NCRIC staff, a vendor, or another member agency's personnel on
NCRIC's behalf. If the Sheriff's Office takes the position that it does not hold the
records described below and that some other entity is the proper respondent, please
identify that entity and the basis for that position in the written determination.
As you are aware, NCRIC provided ALPR data aggregation and search services to its member
agencies through a platform known as "AstroMetrics," built and operated on NCRIC's behalf
by MaiVERIC, Inc. MaiVERIC's November 17, 2022 press release announcing the platform's
deployment quotes Brian Rodrigues, NCRIC's Assistant Deputy Director for Information
Technology & Cyber Security, and states that AstroMetrics was being provided "to 70 public
safety agencies" and that it "maintains all usage records" and that "[c]lient
administrators also have access to real-time data ingestion and usage logs."
As you are further aware, the vendor's own published technical documentation for the

--- page 2 ---
AstroMetrics platform states that NCRIC used a third-party identity provider, SecureAuth,
configured to authenticate users against NCRIC's own internal Active Directory, and that
users were federated into the AstroMetrics application through that mechanism — an
identity realm the documentation identifies as "SecureAuth5" — rather than through any
login screen operated by the vendor. That same documentation reflects that non-NCRIC
personnel, including accounts associated with federal agency domains, accessed the
platform through this mechanism during at least the period June through December 2023.
Civil Code § 1798.90.52 provides that an ALPR operator that accesses, or provides access
to, ALPR information shall maintain a record of that access including the date and time of
access, the data elements used to query the system, the username of the person accessing
the information, and, as applicable, the organization or entity with which that person is
affiliated, and the purpose for accessing the information. As the entity that operated
AstroMetrics and provided its member agencies' and other users' access to ALPR information
through that platform, NCRIC is an ALPR operator within the meaning of that section as to
the records described below.
Records requested, from January 1, 2021 through the date of this request:
1. Records reflecting the population of user accounts provisioned for the AstroMetrics
platform (or its predecessor or successor NCRIC ALPR aggregation platform, by whatever
name), by agency or organizational affiliation — including any roster, directory export,
account listing, or periodic user-count report, at any point during the period covered by
this request.
2. Records describing or reflecting the process by which a person or account not
affiliated with an NCRIC member agency — including any person or account affiliated with a
federal agency or department — was granted an AstroMetrics account, added to NCRIC's
Active Directory, or federated into AstroMetrics access through the SecureAuth identity
provider or any successor identity provider.
3. Any agreement, memorandum, authorization, policy, or correspondence permitting or
addressing access by any federal agency or department, or by any of its personnel or
contractors, to ALPR information contributed by NCRIC member agencies, whether through
AstroMetrics or any other NCRIC-operated or NCRIC-affiliated system.
4. SecureAuth or Active Directory logs, reports, or exports reflecting user account
creation, modification, or deletion events, and user authentication (login) events, for
the identity realm bound to the AstroMetrics application (identified in the vendor's
documentation as "SecureAuth5"), for the full period such logs exist.
5. Records reflecting NCRIC's or the Sheriff's Office's access to, or possession of, the
access records described in Item 1 of the request captioned "NCRIC platform access data
and its disposition" submitted to the San Mateo Police Department, to the extent
responsive records exist independent of anything SMPD may produce.
If no records are located for any individual item, please respond to that item separately
and continue processing the remaining items.
If any portion of this request is denied, please identify the specific record withheld,
state the specific statutory exemption relied upon, and produce all reasonably segregable
non-exempt portions (Gov. Code §§ 7922.000, 7922.525). If any item is denied on the ground
that the Sheriff's Office does not hold the responsive records in its NCRIC-administrative
capacity, please identify the entity that does and the basis for that position.
Please provide the determination required by Government Code § 7922.535 within 10 days, in
writing, per § 7922.540. Please acknowledge receipt of this request so the record reflects
the date received.
The requested documents will be made available to the general public, and this request is
not being made for commercial purposes.
In the event that there are fees, I would be grateful if you would inform me of the total
charges in advance of fulfilling my request. I would prefer the request filled
electronically, by e-mail attachment if available or CD-ROM if not.
