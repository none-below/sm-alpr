Scope basis. The Sheriff's Office's Memorandum of Understanding with the Northern

agencies through a platform known as "AstroMetrics," built and operated on NCRIC's behalf

Ms. Prado,
Thank you for the November 5 response, and for routing the request to NCRIC. Please share
this follow-up with NCRIC as well. It concerns Items 1 through 4.
Item 3. The response produced NCRIC's 2024 ALPR Policy and describes it as stating that
NCRIC does not share or provide access to federal agencies or any agencies outside
California. The request covers January 1, 2021 through September 13, 2026, and more than
three years of that period precede the 2024 policy. For that earlier period, NCRIC's
published ALPR policy was the version posted at https://ncric.ca.gov/wp-content/
uploads/2021/10/NCRIC-ALPR-Policy.pdf. That policy states that NCRIC uses ALPR technology
"[t]o support authorized law enforcement and public safety purposes of local, state,
federal, and tribal public safety agencies," and, under "Dissemination," that "[t]he NCRIC
may disseminate ALPR data to any governmental entity with an authorized law enforcement or
public safety purpose for access to such data." It addresses access by federal agencies
and is responsive to Item 3. The 2024 policy attributes its narrower rule to "California
Senate Bill 34, California Senate Bill 54, and recent guidance from the California
Attorney General." Senate Bill 34 has been in effect since January 1, 2016.
Under Item 3, please produce the earlier policy and any other version of NCRIC's ALPR
policy in effect between January 1, 2021 and the adoption of the 2024 policy, together
with any memorandum, authorization, or correspondence addressing access by federal
agencies in connection with the revision that produced the 2024 policy. As the request
noted, the vendor's published documentation reflects that accounts associated with federal
agency domains accessed the platform during at least June through December 2023. If NCRIC
holds no record under Items 2 and 3 other than its policies, that would mean it has no
record of how, or on whose authority, those accounts were given access.
Items 1, 2 and 4. The response states that no records of users or user activity, audit
logs, user lists, or records of account provisioning or deprovisioning "are available at
this time." Government Code § 7922.535(a) calls for notice of "the determination and the
reasons therefor." The response does not say whether responsive records existed on
September 13, 2026, the date of the request, or, if records existed and are no longer
held, when and how that changed. For each of Items 1, 2 and 4, please state whether NCRIC
held responsive records on September 13, 2026. If records responsive to an item existed at
any time after January 1, 2021 and are no longer held, please state when and how they were
disposed of and the authority for their disposal.
Three records bear on this. First, the Memorandum of Understanding cited in the request
provides: "An audit log will be maintained for a period of no less than three years to
record user access to shared data, including the name and organization of the user
accessing via the NCRIC-DSP and the date and time when the data was accessed." Second,
NCRIC's earlier ALPR policy provides that "[a]ccess to, and use of, ALPR Data is logged
for audit purposes" and that "[a]udit reports will be provided periodically and on request
to supervisory personnel at the NCRIC and partner agencies"; the 2024 policy produced with
the response contains the same first sentence. Third, on September 17, 2026, the San Mateo
Police Department relayed to me a statement it attributes to NCRIC's Assistant Deputy
Director, which says that "[a]ll searches of the data were logged for audit purposes" and
that "[a]n audit log file spanning June – November 2023 contains six entries of queries by
San Mateo Police Department, including the officer's email address, plate searched, and
associated case number."
The file that statement describes is a record of user activity on the platform. It
identifies users by email address, so it reflects the user accounts provisioned for the
platform and their affiliations, and it is responsive to Item 1. NCRIC used it to prepare
that statement, and Government Code § 7920.530 defines public records to include any
writing "prepared, owned, used, or retained by any state or local agency." Please produce
that file in the form NCRIC reviewed it for the statement. If NCRIC no longer holds it,
please say so for that file specifically and state when NCRIC ceased to hold it.
