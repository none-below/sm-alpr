Memo 
To: 
Woods Cross Mayor and City Council 
From:  James Bigelow, Chief of Police 
Date: 07/14/2026 
 
Re: 
Authorization to Purchase Axon Dash LPR System/Axon Taser 10’s and Consolidate Axon 
Contract 
I am requesting that WXPD be given authorization to purchase Axon Dash LPR System, Axon 
Taser 10’s and consolidate our contract with Axon. These were previously discussed in the last 
council meeting. This will be a ten-year contract (120 months) with replacement tasers, body 
cameras and dash cameras at no additional cost to the city. 
The Woods Cross Police Department is currently using the Axon Dash Camera System, and it is 
requested we purchase the LPR system to go along with the dash cameras.  The LPR system is 
already integrated in the dash cameras, once the software is turned on our officers will 
automatically receive returns from the state system.  This will notify officers of stolen vehicles, 
expired vehicles, vehicles with no insurance etc.   
The current taser the Woods Cross Police Department is using is the Axon Taser 7, we have 
entered the last year of our contract with Axon and the Taser’s will no longer be warrantied 
next year.  Axon has released the Taser 10, which is the replacement for the Taser 7.  Along 
with purchasing the Taser 10’s we are receiving a virtual reality headset that will be used to 
train the officers on the new weapon system.   
This new contract will bundle all of services we currently use through Axon (body cameras, 
dash cameras, tasers, evidence.com) and include the new license plate readers.  This will save
the city approximately 38,157.00 dollars a year and 381,573.88 over the ten-year contract. 
The current quote is for 923,394.55, the first payment will be due August 2026, and the last 
payment will be due August 2035.  The payments will be 92,338.97 for each of those years until 
the last payment in 2035.  I have attached the quote and contract to answer any questions. 
The current quote has expired, and I am waiting for an updated quote from Axon.  I was 
verbally told over the phone that the amount went up by about $2,000 dollars.
