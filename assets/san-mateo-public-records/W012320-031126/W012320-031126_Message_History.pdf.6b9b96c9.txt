Dear Brian C,
 
The City received the following records request from you on March 11, 2026.
 
Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 6250 et seq.), I request the following records 
related to ALPR search activity compliance and audit obligations under SMPD Standard Operating Procedure 
205.
For context: SOP 205 has required quarterly operator audits on the Flock Safety platform since at least 
October 25, 2019, and Flock-specific end-user audits since February 10, 2021. These audits must review: 
operator usage and sign-on information; dates and times of access; entry of required search information by the 
End-User (case number, etc); and connection between searched data and listed justification. Audit results must 
be submitted to the Support Services Captain per SOP 205.5.1.
This request is specifically directed at search activity compliance — whether searches included required 
justification and whether that justification was audited per SOP 205's criteria. Prior PRA responses produced 
three audit memos (Lt. Casazza to Chief Barberini, covering November 2025, December 2025, and January 
2026). Those memos review sharing configuration: user account counts, sharing settings, and whether out-of-
state sharing is revoked. They do not address whether individual searches included the case number or 
justification required by SOP 205's end-user audit criteria. Re-production of those sharing configuration 
memos would not be responsive to this request.
I am not requesting the content of any search query, license plates searched, results of any search, or any 
information that would identify the subject of an investigation. I am requesting only aggregate compliance 
metadata and audit records.
Item 1. SOP 205 quarterly search activity audit records. All quarterly audit memoranda, reports, or other 
documentation that reviewed search activity compliance on the Flock Safety platform, produced pursuant to 
SOP 205. Specifically:
(a) All quarterly end-user audit records verifying search justification compliance (case number entry, 
connection between searched data and stated justification). SOP 205 has required these since at least February 
10, 2021.
(b) All quarterly operator audit records for the Flock Safety platform. SOP 205 has required these since at 
least October 25, 2019.
(c) All records of audit results submitted to the Support Services Captain per SOP 205.5.1, or any alternate 
recipient such as the ALPR Committee Captain or the Chief of Police.
Page 1

--- page 2 ---
(d) The date ranges in this request are based on the understanding that the first Flock Safety devices became 
operational on or about November 12, 2022 (per INV-10888). If any Flock-related audit records, search 
activity, or platform data exists from prior to that date, please include those records and indicate the earliest 
date of Flock activity.
I request all such records from November 2022 through the date of this request. If no quarterly search activity 
audits have ever been conducted under SOP 205, I request confirmation of that fact, including the total number 
of quarterly audit cycles that have elapsed without a documented search activity audit.
Item 2. Aggregate search compliance data. Search activity compliance data from the Flock Safety platform, 
from November 2022 through the date of this request. For quarters where audit records exist per Item 1 and 
contain the quantified data described below, those audits are sufficient. For quarters where audit records exist 
but contain only narrative summaries without underlying counts, I request the platform data that was reviewed 
as part of those audits. For quarters where no audit records are available, I request this data exported from the 
Flock platform at whatever granularity it supports (monthly or quarterly).
To be clear: the data requested below consists of aggregate counts and compliance metadata — not the 
substance of any search, the plates queried, or the results returned. Aggregate counts of whether a justification 
field was populated do not reveal the subject of any investigation.
Specifically:
(a) Total number of ALPR searches conducted, broken out by: (i) searches by SMPD personnel; and (ii) 
searches by external agency personnel accessing SMPD data through Flock's network sharing or direct 
sharing features.
(b) For each category in (a): the count of searches where a case number, incident number, or warrant number 
was entered, and the count where no such identifier was entered.
(c) For each category in (a): the count of searches where the connection between the searched data and the 
listed justification was verified, and the count where it was not — per SOP 205's "Connection between 
searched data and listed justification for the search" audit criterion.
(d) All records of searches flagged, identified, or documented as non-compliant — whether for missing 
justification, invalid or unverifiable case/incident numbers, or failure to demonstrate a connection between the 
searched data and the listed justification. If no responsive records exist, please indicate whether this means (i) 
searches were reviewed and none were found to be non-compliant, or (ii) no review of individual search 
compliance has been conducted.
(e) For any flagged entries in (d): records of the corrective actions taken, including notifications to the 
searching user or agency, referrals for disciplinary review, or changes to system access.
Item 3. External agency search activity. Records identifying which external agencies have conducted searches 
against SMPD's ALPR data since November 2022, including agencies that may have had access during part of 
this period but are no longer on the current access list. Specifically:
Page 2

--- page 3 ---
(a) The name of each agency that has conducted one or more searches against SMPD's Flock data since 
November 2022.
(b) The total number of searches conducted by each such agency.
I am not requesting the substance of any search — only agency names and aggregate counts. If records are not 
available for the full period, I request whatever date range is available together with a statement of the earliest 
date for which records exist.
I request responsive records in their native electronic format (e.g., CSV or Excel exports from Flock Safety's 
audit dashboard, PDF for memoranda). If any portion of this request is denied, please respond to that item 
separately, state the specific legal basis for the denial, and continue processing the remaining items.
 
As you are aware, there may be responsive records to this request. Due to the large volume of emails, we are 
releasing records on a rolling basis.  A good faith and diligent search yielded no records responsive to your 
request. If any responsive records are located, we will further respond to your request with updates no later than
9/8/26.

Kelly,
Thank you for the itemized response. I have follow-ups on several items.
Item 1(a) — End-user audit records: Denied as records of investigation 
(§ 7923.600) and ALPR information (§ 1798.90.55(b)). Access logs are 
administrative records documenting system usage — not "information or 
data collected through the use of an ALPR system" as defined in § 
1798.90.5(b). The California Supreme Court in ACLU v. Superior Court 
(2017) 3 Cal.5th 1032 held that ALPR data collected through mass 
scanning is not exempt as records of investigation because it "is not 
conducted as part of a targeted inquiry into any particular crime."

Dear Brian C, 
 
The City received the following records request on March 11, 2026. 
 
Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 6250 et seq.), I request the following records 
related to ALPR search activity compliance and audit obligations under SMPD Standard Operating 
Procedure 205.
For context: SOP 205 has required quarterly operator audits on the Flock Safety platform since at least 
October 25, 2019, and Flock-specific end-user audits since February 10, 2021. These audits must review: 
operator usage and sign-on information; dates and times of access; entry of required search information by 
the End-User (case number, etc); and connection between searched data and listed justification. Audit 
results must be submitted to the Support Services Captain per SOP 205.5.1.
This request is specifically directed at search activity compliance — whether searches included required 
justification and whether that justification was audited per SOP 205's criteria. Prior PRA responses 
produced three audit memos (Lt. Casazza to Chief Barberini, covering November 2025, December 2025, and 
January 2026). Those memos review sharing configuration: user account counts, sharing settings, and 
whether out-of-state sharing is revoked. They do not address whether individual searches included the case 
number or justification required by SOP 205's end-user audit criteria. Re-production of those sharing 
configuration memos would not be responsive to this request.

I am not requesting the content of any search query, license plates searched, results of any search, or any 
information that would identify the subject of an investigation. I am requesting only aggregate compliance 
metadata and audit records.
Item 1. SOP 205 quarterly search activity audit records. All quarterly audit memoranda, reports, or other 
documentation that reviewed search activity compliance on the Flock Safety platform, produced pursuant to 
SOP 205. Specifically:
(a) All quarterly end-user audit records verifying search justification compliance (case number entry, 
connection between searched data and stated justification). SOP 205 has required these since at least 
February 10, 2021.
(b) All quarterly operator audit records for the Flock Safety platform. SOP 205 has required these since at 
least October 25, 2019.
(c) All records of audit results submitted to the Support Services Captain per SOP 205.5.1, or any alternate 
recipient such as the ALPR Committee Captain or the Chief of Police.
(d) The date ranges in this request are based on the understanding that the first Flock Safety devices became 
operational on or about November 12, 2022 (per INV-10888). If any Flock-related audit records, search 
activity, or platform data exists from prior to that date, please include those records and indicate the earliest 
date of Flock activity.
I request all such records from November 2022 through the date of this request. If no quarterly search 
activity audits have ever been conducted under SOP 205, I request confirmation of that fact, including the 
total number of quarterly audit cycles that have elapsed without a documented search activity audit.
Item 2. Aggregate search compliance data. Search activity compliance data from the Flock Safety platform, 
from November 2022 through the date of this request. For quarters where audit records exist per Item 1 and 
contain the quantified data described below, those audits are sufficient. For quarters where audit records 
exist but contain only narrative summaries without underlying counts, I request the platform data that was 
reviewed as part of those audits. For quarters where no audit records are available, I request this data 
exported from the Flock platform at whatever granularity it supports (monthly or quarterly).
To be clear: the data requested below consists of aggregate counts and compliance metadata — not the 
substance of any search, the plates queried, or the results returned. Aggregate counts of whether a 
justification field was populated do not reveal the subject of any investigation.
Specifically:
(a) Total number of ALPR searches conducted, broken out by: (i) searches by SMPD personnel; and (ii) 
searches by external agency personnel accessing SMPD data through Flock's network sharing or direct 
sharing features.
(b) For each category in (a): the count of searches where a case number, incident number, or warrant 
number was entered, and the count where no such identifier was entered.

(c) For each category in (a): the count of searches where the connection between the searched data and the 
listed justification was verified, and the count where it was not — per SOP 205's "Connection between 
searched data and listed justification for the search" audit criterion.
(d) All records of searches flagged, identified, or documented as non-compliant — whether for missing 
justification, invalid or unverifiable case/incident numbers, or failure to demonstrate a connection between 
the searched data and the listed justification. If no responsive records exist, please indicate whether this 
means (i) searches were reviewed and none were found to be non-compliant, or (ii) no review of individual 
search compliance has been conducted.
(e) For any flagged entries in (d): records of the corrective actions taken, including notifications to the 
searching user or agency, referrals for disciplinary review, or changes to system access.
Item 3. External agency search activity. Records identifying which external agencies have conducted 
searches against SMPD's ALPR data since November 2022, including agencies that may have had access 
during part of this period but are no longer on the current access list. Specifically:
(a) The name of each agency that has conducted one or more searches against SMPD's Flock data since 
November 2022.
(b) The total number of searches conducted by each such agency.
I am not requesting the substance of any search — only agency names and aggregate counts. If records are 
not available for the full period, I request whatever date range is available together with a statement of the 
earliest date for which records exist.
I request responsive records in their native electronic format (e.g., CSV or Excel exports from Flock Safety's 
audit dashboard, PDF for memoranda). If any portion of this request is denied, please respond to that item 
separately, state the specific legal basis for the denial, and continue processing the remaining items.
 
A good faith and diligent search yielded no records responsive to your request for aggregate data. All audit
records maintained by the Department were provided to you in your request #W012129-020926. All other 
requests are exempt from release under the following exemptions:
 
 Cal. Government Code section 7923.600. because they are considered a record of investigation or intelligence 
with the San Mateo Police Department. Section 7923.600 provides that public agencies need not disclose 
records of investigations conducted by police agencies. 
 
 In addition, your request has been denied pursuant to Government Code 7927.705, as records prohibited from 
disclosure under federal or state law. In this case, Civil Code section 1798.90.55, subdivision (b) prohibits a 
public agency from selling, sharing, or transferring ALPR information, except to another public agency, and 
only as otherwise permitted by law.
