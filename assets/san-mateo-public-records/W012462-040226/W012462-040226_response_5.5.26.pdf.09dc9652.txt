As previously noted for #W012596-042226  and #W012597-042226, the City’s system does not 
categorize or organize records into discrete batches based on the specific criteria outlined in a 
request. Instead, records are processed and uploaded in a non-sequential, system-generated order. 
Due to this functionality, responsive records are being reviewed and produced in bulk as they 
become available, rather than grouped according to the categories identified in your request. As a 
result, the City is unable to identify or designate batches as corresponding to specific categories. 
In addition, the City’s system for reviews provides us with the records required for review.  
 
Additionally, the City’s records review system provides documents in the same format in which 
they are made available to you. The City does not have control over formatting issues, such as 
broken images contained within marketing emails. We apologize for any inconvenience this may 
cause.  
 
The City affirms that it is making a good faith and diligent effort to process and respond to your 
request in accordance with the California Public Records Act. This includes conducting a 
reasonable and thorough search for responsive records, reviewing records for applicable 
exemptions, and producing non-exempt records on a rolling basis as they become available, 
consistent with statutory requirements and practical limitations.
