event of a crime in your community. Granting them access now may lead to quicker results should a crime
occur.
To share access to your cameras, you must add Albany CA PD to your access list:
Open User Management
Click on the “Share” button next to the networks list
In the “Cameras to Share” dropdown, select the network you would like to share
In the “Share with” dropdown, select Albany CA PD
Click “Save”
As the owner of your footage, you can revoke this access at any time. If you have questions or need further
assistance, please reply to this message or email support@flocksafety.com .
