Real Time Crime Centers (RTCCs) are having a moment throughout the law enforcement
community. People often think of this when they hear RTCC - a physical location that costs
millions of dollars. Not every agency has the manpower or budget to accomplish this and
agencies deserve better solutions…faster. Join former Chief of Ventura, Ken Corney, the Flock
Safety team and Chiefs around the NorCal area for a roundtable discussion on the future of
situational awareness solutions. 
We look forward to hosting you all to virtually meet and share ideas around:
What does a Real Time Crime Center mean to your agency? 
Are there tools or experiences around gathering intelligence for your agency you think
other agencies could benefit from? 
What gaps do you see in being able to use intelligence with neighboring agencies? 
How do you see real-time information changing in the future?
