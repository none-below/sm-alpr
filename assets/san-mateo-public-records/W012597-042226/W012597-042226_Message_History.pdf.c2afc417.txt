(b) The "I did not locate a log of this information in the Flock platform" answer addresses only the Flock 
platform and does not address whether removal notifications arrived via email.

containing "University of the Pacific" or "UOP" in the subject, body, to/from fields, or attachments. A 9/1/25 
floor is reasonable because UOP's access request to SMPD was dated 09-10-2025.

RESPONSE: All records requested here are exempted under California Government Code section 
7927.705 which exempts records protected by privileges under the Evidence Code. This includes 
attorney-client privileged communications pursuant to California Evidence Code section 954.
The records requested in categories 1 and 2 concern administrative configuration of SMPD's external-agency 
sharing network — specifically UOP's addition to the sharing list in September 2025, its publicly visible 
presence on the Flock transparency portal, and its removal between February 18 and March 11, 2026. Records 
of this kind are not records of a law-enforcement investigation within the meaning of Government Code § 
7923.600; the subsequent production under W012297-030826 of Flock-originated access-request and sharing-
confirmation emails without exemption confirms that categorization. Civil Code § 1798.90.55(b) restricts the 
sharing of ALPR information (plate reads, search results); it does not restrict disclosure of agency names, 
access-request dates, removal dates, permission types, or internal discussion about which agencies should 
appear on a public sharing list.
To the extent Item 1 surfaces emails that contain a mix of administrative content and material the city believes 
is exempt, Government Code § 7922.525(b) requires segregable production: the non-exempt portions — 
including at minimum the From, To, Date, and Subject fields — must be produced with the exempt portions 
redacted. Please do not apply a blanket exemption where segregation is available. For any record withheld in 
whole or in part, please identify the record individually by sender, recipient, date, and subject; cite the specific 
exemption asserted; and — where § 7923.600 is asserted — identify the investigation to which the record 
relates and whether that investigation is active or closed.
 
RESPONSE: In response to your request, the PRA does not require an agency to create a “privilege log,” or 
list, that identifies the specific records being withheld. The response only needs to identify the legal grounds for
nondisclosure  [Haynie v. Superior Court, supra, 26 Cal.4th at p. 1075].
If the city intends to produce any of these records under W012462-040226 (the broader rolling Flock-email 
request), please confirm in writing and identify the batch in which they will be produced.
 
RESPONSE: This request is being processed under #W012462-042226 and will be provided to you on a 
continuing rolling basis. The City’s system does not categorize or organize records into discrete batches 
based on the request’s specified criteria. Instead, records are processed and uploaded in a non-
