Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the following 
records from the San Mateo Police Department.
This request seeks existing records only (Gov. Code § 7922.530; Sierra Club v. Superior Court (2013) 57 
Cal.4th 157).
Context
SMPD's August 2014 MOU with the Northern California Regional Intelligence Center (NCRIC) (produced 
under W012376-032026) contains two operative provisions:
§ 6 (Information Ownership and Release): "Member Agencies remain the official custodian of all 
information contributed to the NCRIC-DSP. To the fullest extent permissible by law, all requests for 
information, California Public Records Act, or Freedom of Information Act, will be referred to the Member 
Agency that is the owner of the requested data, and the Member Agency that is the owner of the requested 
data will be responsible for responding to the request."
§ 9 (Connecting with other data sources and analysis platforms): "Member Agencies grant authority to the 
NCRIC to execute information sharing agreements with new Member Agencies and to incorporate new 
information sharing systems into the NCRIC-DSP. Such agreements will not require further review or 
approval by member agencies."
NCRIC's published Flock transparency portal (captured 2026-04-25) lists 319 outbound recipients, 
including three private institutions:
- University of the Pacific (CA) — confirmed in writing by UOP's Chief of Police on 2026-03-11 to be a 
private institution not subject to CPRA.
- Stanford University CA PD — a department within Stanford University, a private 501(c)(3) institution.
- University of San Francisco CA PD — a department within USF, a private 501(c)(3) institution.
Scope of records
This request reaches records held by Flock Safety, NCRIC, or any other contractor or partner acting as

SMPD's agent on SMPD's data, regardless of which server they physically reside on. Such records are 
"writings ... prepared, owned, used, or retained" by SMPD within the meaning of Gov. Code § 7920.530 and 
City of San Jose v. Superior Court (2017) 2 Cal.5th 608. Four SMPD-sourced instruments confirm this 
directly:
1. Flock Master Services Agreement § 4.1 — SMPD retains ownership of data processed by Flock on 
SMPD's behalf. 
2. SMPD Policy 463 (published Dec 12, 2025) — § 463.5 makes the Field Operations Captain "responsible 
for ensuring systems and processes are in place for the proper collection and retention of ALPR data," 
referring to "ALPR information gathered and retained by this Department."
 
3. SMPD SOP 205 (published Nov 10, 2025) § 205.5.1 — audit records "maintained by Amazon Web 
Services" (for Flock) or by NCRIC are "available for review and audit by SMPD." No responsive records.
4. SMPD–NCRIC MOU § 6 — SMPD is the official custodian of all SMPD information contributed to 
NCRIC-DSP and is responsible for responding to PRA requests about that information. Under the MOU, 
NCRIC refers such requests back to SMPD. No responsive records.
Records requested
1. ALPR data SMPD-originated that was disclosed by NCRIC to University of the Pacific, Stanford 
University, or University of San Francisco (collectively, the "Named Recipients") — plate reads, detections, 
hotlist hit notifications, query results, or any other ALPR information (as defined by Civil Code § 
1798.90.5(c)) transmitted to a Named Recipient through any NCRIC system or platform, including bulk 
exports, canned reports, data feeds, or files. For this item, the temporal scope is from November 2022 
(commencement of SMPD's Flock deployment, per INV-10888) through the date of production. No 
responsive records. 
2. Audit-log entries memorializing each Named Recipient's access to SMPD-originated data via NCRIC 
systems (NCRIC-DSP, Astrometrics, Flock, or otherwise), including: (a) search queries Named Recipient 
user accounts executed against SMPD-originated data; (b) plate reads or results returned in response to 
those queries; (c) timestamps for each query and response; and (d) the Named Recipient user account 
identifiers associated with each query. For this item, the temporal scope is from November 2022 through the 
date of production.
The requested records reflect search activity conducted by law enforcement agencies in connection with 
criminal investigations. Each log entry documents the existence, timing, and nature of an investigative act by 
another agency, including the category of criminal offense under investigation and the time frame of the search. 
Disclosure of these records would reveal confidential investigative activity. These records are therefore exempt 
from disclosure as investigatory records compiled by a law enforcement agency for law enforcement purposes 
pursuant to Government Code section 7923.600
 
3. Any documentation NCRIC provided to a Named Recipient describing the scope of that recipient's access 
to SMPD-originated data — camera lists, coverage maps, retention-window notices, hotlist or alert-rule 
descriptions. No temporal floor; this item covers any such documentation in existence. No responsive

Anticipated exemption issues
Civil Code § 1798.90.55(b) (via Gov. Code § 7927.705). The statute's sharing restriction operates against a 
public agency's disclosure of ALPR information to non-public entities. The records requested here 
document onward disclosures that have already occurred to recipients (UOP, Stanford, USF) that are not § 
1798.90.5(f) public agencies. Information NCRIC provided to SMPD, or maintained internally by NCRIC, 
does not implicate that provision's sharing restriction.
Gov. Code § 7923.600 (investigation records). § 7923.600 exempts records of complaints to, or investigations 
conducted by, a state or local police agency, and investigatory files compiled by such an agency. Two 
features of the present situation sit outside that scope: (a) the records requested are not investigative files 
compiled for a specific SMPD investigation, but bulk ALPR data SMPD made broadly accessible to 
NCRIC's outbound network for undefined use; and (b) any investigative use by the Named Recipients is 
investigation by private universities — not by a state or local police agency within § 7923.600's text.
Records held by NCRIC or Flock Safety. Per MOU § 6, SMPD remains the official custodian of SMPD-
contributed data residing in NCRIC systems and is responsible for responding to PRA requests about that 
data. Per Gov. Code § 7920.530 and City of San Jose v. Superior Court (2017) 2 Cal.5th 608, records of

SMPD-originated data held by Flock Safety as SMPD's contractor are SMPD records regardless of physical 
location.
Cal. Const. Art. I § 1 / third-party privacy. If plate data implicating third-party privacy appears in the 
produced records, the appropriate redaction baseline is the one applied when the data was originally 
disclosed to NCRIC and onward to the Named Recipients. Whatever redactions were applied (or were not 
applied) at that disclosure reflect SMPD's operative privacy judgment on that data.
Segregability (§ 7922.525(b)). Items 3 through 5 (scope documentation, agreements, awareness records) 
concern the fact and terms of the sharing arrangement rather than underlying plate data, and should be 
producible with minimal or no redaction regardless of how Items 1 and 2 are handled.
If any portion is denied, please:
1. provide specific exemption citations per Gov. Code § 7922.540;
2. identify the withheld record type and volume;
3. indicate whether the denial rests on a claim that the records are held by NCRIC or Flock Safety rather 
than SMPD; and
4. if the denial invokes Civil Code § 1798.90.55(b) or any other rationale treating the records as protected 
"ALPR information" restricted from disclosure to non-public entities, state whether SMPD considers 
NCRIC's onward disclosure of SMPD-originated ALPR data to UOP, Stanford, and USF — non-public 
institutions — to have been lawful under that provision, and identify the legal authority supporting that 
position.
Thank you.
