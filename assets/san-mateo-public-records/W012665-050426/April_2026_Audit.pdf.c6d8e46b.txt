Policy 463.  I verified that data sharing is limited to agencies within California, with no sharing

that the “Revoke Out-of-State Sharing” setting is selected.

Mateo Police Department Transparency Portal.  I was informed the update should appear in 
about 24 hours.  A follow-up audit will be conducted in June to review ALPR usage for the 
month of May.
