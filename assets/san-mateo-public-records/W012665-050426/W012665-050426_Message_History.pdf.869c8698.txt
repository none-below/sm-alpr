The City received the following records request from you on May 04, 2026.
Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the following records 
from the San Mateo Police Department.
This request seeks existing records only (Gov. Code § 7922.530; Sierra Club v. Superior Court (2013) 57 
Cal.4th 157).
Context
This request is a temporal follow-up to W012461-040226 (closed 2026-04-08), which sought audit records 
produced under Policy 463.10 (added to Policy 463 December 22, 2025) and SOP 205.5.1 (in effect under SOP 
205 since at least 2019).
§ 463.10 (ALPR Data Search Audits) provides: "It is the responsibility of the ALPR Committee Captain or the 
authorized designee to ensure an audit is conducted of ALPR data searches at least monthly for internal ALPR 
users and quarterly for external CA public agency users. The audit shall be documented in the form of an 
internal department memorandum to the Chief of Police. The memorandum shall include any data errors found 
so any such errors can be corrected. After review by the Chief of Police, the memorandum and any associated 
documentation shall be filed and retained."
Two distinct audit populations are required: (a) internal ALPR users (SMPD personnel) on a monthly cadence, 
and (b) external CA public agency users (other agencies with access to SMPD's ALPR data) on a quarterly 
cadence. Both audits are internal SMPD memoranda to the Chief. "Internal" and "external" refer to who is 
audited, not who conducts the audit.
Under W012461, SMPD produced four monthly internal audit memoranda addressed to the Chief of Police 
(December 2025, January 2026, February 2026, March 2026), dated within 1–14 days after the audited month. 
The memoranda contain language describing audits of "internal and external ALPR usage" — appearing to 
address both audit populations on a monthly cadence — but their substantive content reviews SMPD's sharing 
configuration (verification that out-of-state sharing is revoked, that the "Revoke Out-of-State Sharing" setting 
is selected, that no sharing occurs with federal law enforcement). § 463.10 specifies "audit of ALPR data 
searches"; the produced memoranda do not appear to review search activity conducted by external CA public 
agency users with access to SMPD's data.
