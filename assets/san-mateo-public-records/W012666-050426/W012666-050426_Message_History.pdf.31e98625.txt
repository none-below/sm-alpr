This request seeks existing records only (Gov. Code § 7922.530; Sierra Club v. Superior Court (2013) 57

Context
SOP 205 has, in versions produced under W012198-022326, included quarterly operator audit 
requirements since at least October 25, 2019, and Flock-specific end-user audit requirements since at 
least February 10, 2021. SOP 205.5.1 requires quarterly operator audits of all ALPR platforms named in 
SOP 205 (Flock, Vigilant, BOSS, PIPS, NCRIC) submitted to the Support Services Captain, and 
quarterly end-user audits including review of search justifications and case-number entries also 
submitted to the Support Services Captain.
These requirements are distinct from Policy 463.10 (added to Policy 463 December 22, 2025), which 
establishes monthly audits of internal ALPR users and quarterly audits of external CA public agency 
users, both addressed to the Chief of Police. SOP 205.5.1 specifies a different recipient (Support Services 
Captain), broader platform scope (all five ALPR platforms in SOP 205), and additional end-user audit 
criteria (search-justification and case-number review).
SMPD has stated under W012129-020926 that it no longer actively uses Vigilant, BOSS, or PIPS. SOP 
205.5.1's all-five-platform scope obligation applies regardless of current active use, since a 
decommissioned platform may retain historical SMPD data, retain user accounts with persistent access 
to that data, or remain accessible to SMPD personnel reviewing legacy records.

Policy 463 and SOP 205.5.1 establish distinct audit obligations — different recipients, scope, and criteria 
— each producing distinct records. This request is for SOP 205.5.1 records only; Policy 463 records are 
sought separately.
Background only: in response to W012160-021226 (closed 2026-02-23), which requested SOP 205 audit 
records from 2020 through 2025, SMPD responded that "a good faith and diligent search yielded no 
records responsive to your request." In response to W012461-040226 (closed 2026-04-08), SMPD 
responded "We have no additional records" when asked to confirm that no records exist matching the 
distinct requirements of SOP 205.5.1. Item 4 of W012461, requesting NCRIC end-user audit records, 
was answered "No responsive records." Together, these prior responses establish that no SOP 205.5.1 
audits had been produced as of April 8, 2026. This request seeks any SOP 205.5.1 records that may exist 
now.
Records requested — covering the period from October 25, 2019 (earliest date SOP 205 mandated 
quarterly operator audits, per SOP 205 versions produced under W012198-022326) through the date of 
production:
1. Any quarterly operator audit records produced under SOP 205.5.1, submitted to the Support Services 
Captain — including audits covering platforms other than Flock (Vigilant, BOSS, PIPS, or NCRIC), and 
any audit confirming the decommissioned status of platforms SMPD has stated it no longer uses 
(Vigilant, BOSS, PIPS).
2. Any quarterly end-user audit records produced under SOP 205.5.1, submitted to the Support Services 
Captain — including review of search justifications and case-number entries as specified in SOP 205.5.1.
3. Any end-user audit records for the NCRIC platform produced under SOP 205.5.1, including any audit 
of SMPD's data sharing with NCRIC via the Flock network.
4. Any platform exports, search logs, user-activity reports, or other records the auditor reviewed during 
the audits being requested in items 1–3 to assess access, search reasons, and compliance.
5. Any records produced, created, generated, or captured by the auditor during the conduct of any SOP 
205.5.1 audits being requested in items 1–3 — including review notes, screenshots, intermediate query 
results, draft memoranda, working calculations, or any other records reflecting the audit's review 
process.
6. Any internal SMPD communications regarding SOP 205.5.1 audit findings, scope, methodology, 
compliance status, or whether SOP 205.5.1 audits have been or will be conducted, from the earliest such 
communication through the date of production.
7. Any internal records — other than peace officer personnel records exempt under Penal Code §§ 
832.7–832.8 — reflecting SMPD's institutional response to the SOP 205.5.1 audit obligation, including 
memoranda directing that SOP 205.5.1 audits be performed, internal reviews or assessments of SOP 
205.5.1 compliance status, action items from management meetings addressing the audit obligation, or 
written communications acknowledging that the audit obligation has not been met.
