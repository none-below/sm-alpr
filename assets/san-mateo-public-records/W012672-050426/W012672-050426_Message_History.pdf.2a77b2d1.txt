Context
SMPD Policy 463 and SOP 205 each impose pre-access training requirements on SMPD personnel, and 
each identifies a designated custodian for the resulting training records:
- Policy 463.4(d) (published December 12, 2025): "No member of this department or authorized designee 
shall operate ALPR equipment or access ALPR data without first completing department-approved 
training. This training is further described in SOP 205."
- Policy 463.9 (TRAINING): "The ALPR Administrator shall ensure that members receive department-
approved training to operate or access the ALPR system (Civil Code § 1798.90.51; Civil Code § 
1798.90.53). This training is further described in SOP 205. The department POST Coordinator will 
maintain all training records in METR."
- SOP 205.3 (ALPR OPERATOR TRAINING): "Prior to the operation of the our Department approved 
ALPR system, any person operating a system shall complete Department approved training. The

being re-requested here.

Anticipated exemption issues:
- Penal Code §§ 832.7–832.8 (peace officer personnel records). The training-completion data and 
curriculum requested here are not "personnel records" within the meaning of Penal Code § 832.8(a) — 
they do not concern personal data, medical history, employee benefits, employee advancement, or 
appraisal/discipline of any officer's job performance. Training-completion records that document the 
agency's compliance with the mandatory-training provisions of Civil Code §§ 1798.90.51 and 1798.90.53 
are operational compliance records of the ALPR program. To the extent any individual record contains 
both compliance-tracking content and personnel-record content, Government Code § 7922.525(b) 
requires segregable production.
- Civil Code § 1798.90.55(b). The statute restricts sharing of ALPR information (license-plate reads, 
search results, hit notifications). Training-completion records, training curriculum, and training-
administration records are not "ALPR information" as defined in Civil Code § 1798.90.5(c).
- Gov. Code § 7923.600 (investigation records). Training-administration records concern the operational 
readiness and compliance of agency personnel; they are not files compiled in connection with any 
particular investigation.
- Records held outside the Department. Per Policy 463.9, the POST Coordinator maintains training 
records in METR. METR records concerning SMPD personnel maintained on SMPD's behalf are 
SMPD records within the meaning of Gov. Code § 7920.530.
If any portion is denied, please provide specific exemption citations per Gov. Code § 7922.540. If volume 
is burdensome, please contact me to narrow scope rather than denying categorically.
