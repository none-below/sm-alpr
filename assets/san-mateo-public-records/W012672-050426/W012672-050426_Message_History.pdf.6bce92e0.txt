Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the following records 
from the San Mateo Police Department.
This request seeks existing records only (Gov. Code § 7922.530; Sierra Club v. Superior Court (2013) 57 
Cal.4th 157).
Context
SMPD Policy 463 and SOP 205 each impose pre-access training requirements on SMPD personnel, and each 
identifies a designated custodian for the resulting training records:
- Policy 463.4(d) (published December 12, 2025): "No member of this department or authorized designee shall 
operate ALPR equipment or access ALPR data without first completing department-approved training. This 
training is further described in SOP 205."
- Policy 463.9 (TRAINING): "The ALPR Administrator shall ensure that members receive department-
approved training to operate or access the ALPR system (Civil Code § 1798.90.51; Civil Code § 1798.90.53). 
This training is further described in SOP 205. The department POST Coordinator will maintain all training 
records in METR."
- SOP 205.3 (ALPR OPERATOR TRAINING): "Prior to the operation of the our Department approved ALPR 
system, any person operating a system shall complete Department approved training. The training will be 
coordinated by the Department Training Division ... Records of this training shall be maintained by our 
Training Division."
- SOP 205.4 (ALPR END-USER TRAINING): "Prior to accessing the ALPR platforms to review data as end-
users, any person doing so shall complete Department approved training. The training will be coordinated by 
the Department Training Division ..."
These provisions identify three custodial locations for SMPD-personnel training records: the Department 
Training Division (per SOP 205.3 and 205.4), the POST Coordinator's METR records (per Policy 463.9), and 
the ALPR Administrator's records of having ensured training (per Policy 463.9).
Scope. This request seeks records concerning training of internal SMPD personnel — operators and end-users 
employed by or assigned to SMPD. Training of personnel from external agencies that have been granted 
access to SMPD's ALPR data was the subject of PRA W012174-021826 Item 4 and is not being re-requested

unwarranted invasion of personal privacy.
2. All records reflecting completion, attendance, or participation by SMPD personnel in end-user training

certificates of completion, and any equivalent record reflecting that a named SMPD member completed the 
required end-user training and the date on which it was completed. Responsive
Portions of your request are being withheld pursuant to California Government Code section 7927.700, 
which exempts personnel, medical, or similar files, the disclosure of which would constitute an 
unwarranted invasion of personal privacy.
3. Training curriculum, materials, slide decks, instructor notes, lesson plans, scripts, handouts, recorded 
videos, or other instructional records used to deliver SOP 205.3 operator training or SOP 205.4 end-user 
training, in any version that has been used to date. SOP 205.3 and SOP 205.4 each enumerate required 
training elements; this item covers the materials by which those elements are delivered. Responsive
Portions of your request are being withheld pursuant to Government Code section 7923.600, as they 
constitute law-enforcement investigative records.

constitute preliminary drafts, notes, or interagency or intra-agency memorandums that are not retained 
by the public agency in the ordinary course of business.
4. Any records of training, briefings, supplementary training, written notices, roll-call updates, or other

463 — including but not limited to materials addressing the revised policy provisions, and records identifying

5. Any records of training acknowledgments, certifications, attestations, or signed receipts by SMPD personnel 
concerning ALPR training under SOP 205.3, SOP 205.4, or Policy 463 — including signed acknowledgments

Anticipated exemption issues:
- Penal Code §§ 832.7–832.8 (peace officer personnel records). The training-completion data and curriculum 
requested here are not "personnel records" within the meaning of Penal Code § 832.8(a) — they do not 
concern personal data, medical history, employee benefits, employee advancement, or appraisal/discipline of 
any officer's job performance. Training-completion records that document the agency's compliance with the 
mandatory-training provisions of Civil Code §§ 1798.90.51 and 1798.90.53 are operational compliance 
records of the ALPR program. To the extent any individual record contains both compliance-tracking content 
and personnel-record content, Government Code § 7922.525(b) requires segregable production.
- Civil Code § 1798.90.55(b). The statute restricts sharing of ALPR information (license-plate reads, search 
results, hit notifications). Training-completion records, training curriculum, and training-administration 
records are not "ALPR information" as defined in Civil Code § 1798.90.5(c).
- Gov. Code § 7923.600 (investigation records). Training-administration records concern the operational 
readiness and compliance of agency personnel; they are not files compiled in connection with any particular 
investigation.
- Records held outside the Department. Per Policy 463.9, the POST Coordinator maintains training records in 
METR. METR records concerning SMPD personnel maintained on SMPD's behalf are SMPD records within 
the meaning of Gov. Code § 7920.530.
If any portion is denied, please provide specific exemption citations per Gov. Code § 7922.540. If volume is 
burdensome, please contact me to narrow scope rather than denying categorically.
