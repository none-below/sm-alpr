ALPR OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES 
This memo serves to provide department personnel with an overview of our various ALPR 
platforms and guidelines when access, utilization and response to real-time ALPR notifications are 
determined to be necessary.  SMPD utilizes camera hardware systems through Flock Safety and 
PIPs Technology to capture real-time license plate data.  Personnel will receive ALPR notifications 
from Flock and BOSS in real time when logged into those platforms.  Officers also have access to 
the NCRIC and Vigilant Solutions platforms to access digital license plate data for investigative 
purposes.
In no case shall the ALPR Platforms be used for any purpose other than legitimate law enforcement 
or public safety purpose.  In accordance with the California Values Act, ALPR data may not be 
used for the purposes of immigration enforcement or other similar duties.  
Management, Maintenance and Administration of the ALPR Platforms:
The Support Services Captain, or their designee, will have overall management responsibility for 
the APLR Platforms.  The Investigations Lieutenant will chair the ALPR Steering Committee and 
Subcommittee.  System Administrators for the ALPR platforms will be the Investigation Lieutenant 
and Sergeant.  Issues with the fixed ALPR cameras should be immediately brought to the 
Investigations Lieutenant or his/her designated Sergeant.   Daytime Patrol Lieutenant will have 
oversite of ALPR operations in Patrol to include vehicle ALPR cameras, connectivity or software 
issues, mobile traffic trailers.  Any camera or software functionality issues will be brought to the 
Patrol Lieutenant, or his/her designee, who will work to resolve the issue.  
ALPR System Overview:
ALPR platforms are emerging as an important investigative tool and the department is expanding 
access to this technology.  Officers and Dispatchers currently have access to the NCRIC for BOSS 
ALPR data and will now have access to the Flock Safety and Vigilant Solutions ALPR data for 
expanded investigative capability.  They are required to receive departmental approved training 
before accessing these ALPR platforms.  
1) PIP’s Technology: Cameras currently on four patrol vehicles and two trailers that collect 
data and upload it via Pagis software into the BOSS platform.  BOSS is maintained in the 
NCRIC cloud server. We log into BOSS to access our, and other agencies, ALPR data.

When logged into BOSS, Officers can receive real-time notifications of hits from our PIPs 
Technology cameras.
2) Flock Safety: In contract with Flock Safety for 22 ALPR cameras.  19 at fixed locations 
monitoring vehicles entering and leaving the city and three on three individual trailers.  Our 
Flock Safety ALPR cameras capture digital license plate data and upload it to their cloud 
server.  Personnel log in to the Flock Safety website to access our data as well as the data 
from other agencies that have chosen to share with us.  When logged in, Officers can receive 
real-time notifications of hits from our Flock Safety cameras.
3) Vigilant Solutions:  We do not own or operate any Vigilant cameras.  We have an account 
with and access to their search platform for ALPR data collected by other agencies utilizing 
their technology.  Personnel must have completed the department approved training then 
can access to the search platform.  Vigilant is a much larger company and contains a large 
amount of ALPR data.  
Actionable Information:
It is the policy of this department to consider appropriate response to actionable information such as 
Stolen Vehicles, Felony Vehicle (wanted person or vehicle) or Missing Persons.  ALPR platforms 
also hit on vehicles in DOJ/NCIC related to sexual offenders, protection orders, supervised release, 
violent offenders, gang or terrorism related vehicles and stolen or lost license plates.  These hits are 
not actionable without additional information, i.e. matches description of suspect vehicle or 
registered owner is recognized as an investigative lead in a crime.
Accessing ALPR Technology and databases:
Personnel must comply with department Lexipol Policy 462 and SOP 205 when accessing any of 
the ALPR platforms.  Sharing your login or password is prohibited.  Personnel shall use and access 
the ALPR databases for authorized investigative purposes only.  When accessing any of the APLR 
databases, personnel shall:
1) Include the sign-on information (username / password) 
2) Required search information by End-User (case number, justification for search, etc.)  
Procedure:
Standard Operating Procedure for our current PIPs/BOSS ALPR platform will be used for both for 
Flock Safety hits as well.  With the introduction of fixed ALPR cameras, officers that follow up on 
hits they receive from fixed ALPR locations should be in the Beat of the ALPR hit location before 
considering investigation to ensure a timely response to the information.  The following procedure 
will be followed:

Officers logged into ALPR platforms will receive hits notifications when a flagged vehicle 
in DOJ/NCIC is captured by an ALPR System.  

Officers should confirm and compare that the plate data and photo shown on the search 
platform are the same (not a misread) 

Advise Dispatch over the air of the ALPR hit, location, and fixed or mobile. Examples:

“A12 Fixed ALPR hit W/B Poplar & Amphlett” or “A12, Mobile ALPR hit at 3rd and 
Norfolk” and provide the license plate information.

Advise Dispatch if you have visual of the vehicle.

Officers will not coordinate car to car without first notifying Dispatch to ensure proper 
supervision of Officer activity. 

Dispatch will query the license plate and will provide voice return over the air.

Dispatch will ensure a Watch Sergeant is aware of the ALPR hit over the radio and the 
Sergeant or Supervisor will acknowledge over the air.

Officers will take appropriate enforcement action.  
Training:
Prior to accessing the ALPR platforms, personnel will complete the department approved training as 
outlined in Lexipol Policy 462.
