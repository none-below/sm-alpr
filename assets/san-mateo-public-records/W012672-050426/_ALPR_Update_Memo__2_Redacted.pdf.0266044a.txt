ALPR Update
The ALPR Committee met to discuss, identify and address any issues related to our ALPR 
platforms.  In general, there is a lack of consistency in the below listed areas, and this memo serves 
to correct them and further improve this program.  The following is a summary of the discussed 
changes.
INCONSISTENT ENTRY OF “REASON” FOR SEARCH
It is mandatory that we conduct a quarterly audit of our ALPR usage and to ensure we remain in 
compliance, we need to enter a reason for each query.    
1. Not consistent or not entered at all- required for audit
2. Query related to an incident - that incident or case number MUST be entered into the 
REASON section when the search is made
3. Often queries are made prior to a case or incident number issued and officers need to get the 
information ASAP.  If there is no incident or case number, provide descriptive information 
like why and location
a.
Examples: 10851 hit veh descript, 211-Laurie Meadows
4. Unacceptable: “Locate” and “Identify”.  This leads to the question of “locate” or “identify” 
what and why.  Additional descriptive information is required.  
NEW DISPATCH LANGUAGE
Since rolling out the Flock ALPR platform, officers and dispatchers have modified the language 
originally outlined in our SOP.  This is due to practical use of the Flock platform which has allowed 
us to effectively share they necessary information and remove the unnecessary.  The following are 
the changes to ALPR communications:
1. “Fixed” or “mobile” is not necessary
2. No need to say you have a “visual” or “no visual.”  “I will be on an area check” is an 
indicator to Dispatch that there is no visual of the vehicle.  If/when Officers have a visual of 
the vehicle they will notify Dispatch.  
3. Adopted Language 
a. Officer:  D87 ALPR hit at 3rd & Humboldt.
b. Dispatch: Go ahead
c. Officer: A123BCD, I will be on an area check
d. Dispatch: 10-4, the vehicle is coming back 10-30 out of San Francisco...(etc.)
e. Officer:  10-4

ENTRY OF OUTSIDE AGENCY INFORMATION INTO SMPD HOTLIST
1. Hotlist is important information sharing tool and officers are encouraged to use the Hotlist.
2. All entries of information from outside agencies or TRAK fliers will be approved 
through a supervisor before they are entered.
PIPs MOBILE ALPR CAMERAS MAINTENANCE SUSPENDED
The PIPs ALPR cameras are constantly plagued with operational issues and has caused a significant 
personnel commitment to keep them working.  With the ALPR technologies now available to law 
enforcement, the ALPR committee had decided to sever our relationship with Neology, Inc., the 
parent company of PIPs, and are looking for updated reliable technology to replace our mobile 
ALPR cameras.  We will not have ALPR support form PIPs moving forward.  For equipment issues 
with the PIPs cameras, continue to contact George Herrera until PIPs removes their systems from 
our patrol vehicles.
