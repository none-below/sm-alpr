Tips & Tricks

Search with Wildcards

Wildcards can help narrow your search if you know most of a plate except for specific characters.

To use a wildcard, replace unknown characters with an asterisk *

Your search results will contain any plates that follow the pattern of your search. For example,
searching for ABC*234 could return any of the following:

ABCD234
ABC1234
ABC234
ABC-234
ABCD1234

Search for a Partial Match

Partial searches can help narrow your search if you know only some of the characters on a plate.
To use a partial plate search, enter the characters you know.

Your search results will contain any plates that contain the sequence you entered. For example,
searching for ABC could return any of the following:

ABC1234
1234ABC
12ABC34
