Creating custom hotlist -O’Rourke/Trujillo- ALPR End-Users and 
Operators can enter vehicles into the Hotlist when investigating 
Misdemeanor/Felony crimes and must have vehicle license 
description/plate. The following outlines the criteria and process for making 
Hotlist entries:
-
1) Identify a need for a Hotlist entry
-
2) Get Watch Commander or Supervisor approval for Hotlist entry and 
required email.
-
3) The email will contain the following information:
-
a. Vehicle license plate/description
-
b. Crime associated with the investigation
-
c. Case number
-
d. Case Investigator name
-
e. Length of time they wish to keep this info in the Hotlist
-
f. Instructions for Officers that encounter the Hotlist vehicle. 
-
4) Send the email to HOTLIST@cityofsanmateo.org. and copy the 
approving supervisor. This email will include the current ALPR Committee 
Members assigned to Patrol that will make the Hotlist entry. ( O’Rourke, 
Trujillo, Sam Leung, & Venikov)
-
5) Hotlist entry will be removed 2 weeks after it is entered. 
-
6) Hotlist extension requests are sent to HOTLIST@cityofsanmateo.org 
indicating the extension request, timeframe, and the approving supervisor. 
Copy the approving supervisor.
-
Hotlist Example: Officer Jones requests to enter the below content into the 
Hotlist for 14 days/weeks. I have obtained approval from Sgt. Pink. Vehicle 
License Plate 1ABC234, blue Honda Civic, armed 211, firearm outstanding, 
Case 220728022, Officer Jones, Hotlist entry instructions: Please stop, ID 
and photograph the vehicle occupants. Call Officer Jones before releasing 
the vehicle and its occupants.
