I wanted to follow up specifically on Item 6 of the original request:
"Any policy, procedure, or system documentation describing how SMPD monitors or tracks vendor-initiated 
disclosures under Section 5.3."
Item 6 asks about documentation of a monitoring or tracking mechanism — a policy, procedure, 
configuration, or standing arrangement — rather than about email content. If such a record exists, it would 
likely be a policy document, a contract addendum, an internal procedure, or a system configuration, rather 
than an email in the W012462 corpus.
I also want to note that Section 5.3 authorizes Flock to act on its own good-faith belief without requiring 
SMPD's approval or involvement. A disclosure under that provision would not necessarily generate an email 
in SMPD's inbox, which is why Item 6 asks separately whether any mechanism exists by which the City 
would become aware of such a disclosure.
With that in mind: does any record exist, of any kind, describing how SMPD would learn of a Section 5.3 
disclosure? If no responsive records exist for Item 6 specifically, a statement to that effect would resolve this 
item and I would not expect it to require review of the W012462 email corpus.
Thank you,
Brian
