(c) The extension of W012459-040226 (records of the Council-requested 2020 ALPR/sUAS report-back) into 
the same rolling email-search framing on 5/6/2026 at 5:04 PM — as you are aware, also after the same 
deprioritization email.

Attorney’s office, in accordance with Government Code § 7922.525(b). During this review, it was
