Anticipated exemption issues
- Civil Code § 1798.90.55(b). The records requested describe sharing-configuration metadata — share 
identifiers, permission scopes, lifecycle events, vendor documentation, current-state confirmations, and 
internal/vendor communications — not "ALPR information" as defined in § 1798.90.5(c). SMPD's 
productions under W012570-041926 and W012664-050426 (UOP_Termination.png) included 
configuration metadata of this exact type.
- Gov. Code § 7923.600 (investigation records). The records concern administrative configuration and 
operational lifecycle of a data-sharing platform, not files compiled for any particular SMPD 
investigation, and contain no plate data or investigative content.
- Records held by Flock Safety. Per the scope-of-records argument previously accepted in W012570-
041926 and W012664-050426, share-lifecycle records and current-state records held within Flock on 
SMPD's behalf are SMPD records under Gov. Code § 7920.530 and City of San Jose v. Superior Court 
(2017) 2 Cal.5th 608.
- "Previously produced" as a basis for non-response. A response that responsive records were previously 
produced under another PRA is not, on its own, a complete answer where the present request was scoped
beyond that prior production. The W012664 filing explicitly distinguished "further events" from the two 
events produced under W012570. To the extent any item of this request is answered with reference to a 
prior production, please identify the specific previously-produced record or records that the Department 
considers responsive to that item, and confirm that no further responsive records exist beyond those.
- Segregability (Gov. Code § 7922.525(b)). Gov. Code § 7922.525(b) requires production of any 
reasonably segregable portion of a record after deletion of exempt portions. Items 1 through 6 are 
administrative configuration data, vendor communications, and platform-behavior documentation 
containing no investigative content; if any individual record contains both responsive and exempt 
content, the non-exempt portions are subject to that requirement.
