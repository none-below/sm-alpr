RECORDS REQUESTED
1. Per-case activity / audit logs from the PRA management system. For each request within scope, the 
complete activity log, audit trail, case-note log, or equivalent timestamped record maintained by the 
Department's and/or the City's public records request management platform (e.g., NextRequest, 
JustFOIA, GovQA, or whichever software is in use), including: every logged entry, the timestamp of the 
entry, the action taken (e.g., assignment, document upload, internal note, status change, extension, 
response sent), and the user or staff member associated with the entry.
2. Per-case staff-time / labor attribution records. Any report, dashboard view, spreadsheet, export, 
internal memo, or other record that attributes or aggregates staff hours, labor time, or labor cost to 
specific PRA case numbers — including any per-PRA hour totals, breakdowns, or rollups in which any 
case number within scope appears.
3. Timesheet / labor-distribution records linkable to my case numbers. Timesheet entries, time-tracking 
system records, labor distribution records, or activity coding records in which Department or City staff 
have coded hours to a "Public Records," "CPRA," or equivalent project, task, cost center, or case-
number code, to the extent those entries are linkable to any case number within scope. Standard 
personnel-information redactions (e.g., Social Security number, home address, payroll detail) are 
acceptable on a cell-by-cell basis; the data sought is the per-case staff-hour attribution, not employee 
compensation detail. If the time-tracking system records hours at a project- or department-aggregate 
level rather than per case, please produce the aggregate-level records reflecting "Public Records" or 
equivalent coding for the staff members who appear in the per-case activity logs produced under Item 1.
4. Internal communications referencing time on my requests. Internal communications (email, Microsoft 
Teams, Slack, SMS, or other channel) among Department or City staff — including but not limited to 
the City Attorney's office, the City Clerk, the Records Unit, and Department supervisors — that 
reference the time required, anticipated, or expended on any case number within scope, or that reference 
my requests in the aggregate by reference to staff time, workload, or burden.
5. Communications-policy and time-tracking SOPs. Any standard operating procedure, written 
guideline, training material, or policy document governing how Department and City staff are expected 
to log, track, code, or attribute staff time to public records requests, and any document describing the 
configuration of the PRA management system's case-note, activity-log, or time-tracking features.
