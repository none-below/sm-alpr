1102.1   PURPOSE
This procedure provides direction to personnel assigned to the Real Time Information Center on
how to monitor and utilize the ALPR system.
1102.2   ALPR ALERTS
Personnel assigned to the Real Time Information Center will be expected to proactively monitor
the ALPR system, creating calls for service when ALPR hits are validated as wanted vehicles.
1102.2.1   CREATING ALPR CALLS FOR SERVICE
•
Login Procedure:
o
Personnel assigned to the Real-Time Information Center (RTIC) must log into
the Flock system at the start of their shift.
o
Verify that alert notifications are enabled for cameras maintained by the San
Mateo Police Department.
•
Alert Evaluation/Call Creation:
o
Upon receiving an alert, the operator must evaluate the alert details to confirm
that the system accurately captured the license plate characters.
o
Validate the plate returns an SVS hit and is not a misread.
o
Log the information in a CAD incident utilizing the call type "ALPR".
o
If the alert is determined to be a valid alert for a wanted vehicle, the operator shall
put all relevant details in the incident and create a call to be dispatched to patrol.
o
If the alert is determined to be a misread or is no longer a wanted vehicle, the
operator can close the incident with a disposition of "Cancelled".
•
Call Management:
o
If the call remains pending without dispatch for 15 minutes, the operator may
close the incident.
o
Use the disposition "canceled" for the closure and document the reason for
cancellation.
1102.3   SEARCHING FOR VEHICLES IN ALPR
Personnel assigned to the Real Time Information Center are expected to utilize the ALPR system
when searching for vehicles associated to crimes or life or death emergencies.
1102.3.1   QUERYING LICENSE PLATES
•
When the license plate of a vehicle is known, the RTIC operator shall query the ALPR
system to retrieve relevant information.

1102.3.2   SEARCHING FOR VEHICLES BASED ON DESCRIPTION
•
When presented with a vehicle description but no license plate, RTIC operators shall
use the ALPR system to isolate specific cameras to identify potential matches.
•
Operators should utilize available details such as:
1.
Color descriptions of the vehicle.
2.
Partial plate information, if available.
3.
Relevant timeframes to focus the search.
•
Any potential matches identified during the search must be documented in the
incident report as possible plate matches only to avoid confirmation bias or
misrepresentation.
