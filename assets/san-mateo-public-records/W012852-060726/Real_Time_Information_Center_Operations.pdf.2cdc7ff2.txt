Real Time Information Center Operations
1100.1   PURPOSE
The purpose of this SOP is to define the daily expectations and radio procedures for the Real
Time Information Center.
1100.2   RADIO & CAD ETIQUETTE
RTIC Operators are responsible for maintaining professional radio etiquette with both field
personnel and Dispatch. To ensure clear, efficient, and cohesive communications, operators shall
adhere to the following standards:
(a)
CAD Log-In/Log-Out
o
Log on to CAD at the start of shift and log off at the end of shift.
(b)
Status Accuracy
o
Maintain accurate and current status within CAD to reflect availability to
Dispatch.
(c)
Field Requests
o
Communicate when assuming responsibility for handling requests from field
units.
(d)
Intelligence Sharing
o
Document and relay information developed from intelligence sources in a timely
and accurate manner.
(e)
Radio Discipline
o
Limit transmissions to operationally relevant communications and avoid
unnecessary or frivolous radio traffic.
1100.3   DAILY EXPECTATIONS
Upon arrival for their assigned shift, RTIC Operators shall complete the following tasks to ensure
operational readiness:
(a)
System Log-In
o
Activate RTIC wall monitors to display San Mateo–owned cameras, the Flock
database, and other mission-relevant platforms.
(b)
Technology Access
o
Confirm access to all critical technologies.
o
Promptly troubleshoot or report any access issues to ensure full system
functionality.
(c)
Fusus Operations
o
Log into the Fusus platform and verify that all displays are functioning properly.
