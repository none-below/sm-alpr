In response to your Public Records Act request, the Department has identified and attached all responsive 
records currently in existence regarding Automated License Plate Reader (ALPR) procedures applicable to 
Department operations and the Real Time Information Center (RTIC).
The Department does not possess any superseded, withdrawn, archived, or prior versions of procedures 1100 
and 1102 beyond those provided. The attached documents represent the only operative versions maintained by 
the Department.
During our review of your request, we discovered that the Department's Transparency webpage contained two 
entries titled "Automated License Plate Reader Operating Procedure." One entry correctly linked to SOP 205, 
while the second entry was intended to link to SOP 1102 but it linked 205 (twice). To avoid confusion, SOP 
1102 has been retitled "RTIC – ALPR Systems," and the Transparency webpage has been updated accordingly 
here: Standard Operating Procedure Manual | San Mateo, CA - Official Website
