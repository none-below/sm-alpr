Dear Brian C,
 
The City received the below records request from you on July 25, 2026.
 
Under the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the following records:
1. The San Mateo Police Department's Flock Safety Automated License Plate Reader (ALPR) "network audit" 
— the log of searches performed against the Department's ALPR cameras and network by outside agencies 
with access to that network — for the period January 1, 2024 through June 30, 2024, limited to searches 
performed by, or attributed to the account or user credentials of, the Costa Mesa Police Department, and in 
particular the searching user recorded in the Department's Flock records as Robert Josett (which the Flock 
export abbreviates in the "Name" field to "R. Jos").
2. For each responsive entry, the complete audit record as maintained in Flock, including: the searching 
organization/agency name, the searching user's name, the date and time of the search, the search "time frame" 
or look-back window, the stated reason, any case number, the search type, and the counts of networks and 
devices searched.
As you are aware, Robert Josett was suspended by the Costa Mesa Police Department in December 2023, was 
not performing authorized law-enforcement duties during the period covered by this request, and was later 
terminated without reinstatement; searches performed under his credentials during that period are not records 
of any authorized law-enforcement investigation.
The Flock Safety platform generates and retains this network audit for each customer agency and exports it in 
spreadsheet form, so the responsive records are readily producible. Please provide them in their native 
electronic format (CSV or the equivalent Flock export) rather than on paper or as a scanned image.
If the Department withholds any responsive record or portion of a record, please identify each record withheld, 
state the specific statutory exemption relied upon, and produce all reasonably segregable non-exempt portions 
(Gov. Code §§ 7922.000, 7922.525). Please also provide the determination required within 10 days under Gov. 
Code § 7922.535, and, if any fees would apply to the direct cost of duplication, an itemized estimate before 
those costs are incurred.
Thank you for your assistance.
Brian
