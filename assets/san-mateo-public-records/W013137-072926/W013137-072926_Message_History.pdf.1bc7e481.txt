search "time frame" or look-back window.
Robert Josett was suspended by the Costa Mesa Police Department in December 2023 and was later 
terminated without reinstatement.
For context: the Department produces its own Flock search-audit exports on a rolling monthly basis under 
W012541-041426 — most recently the May 2024 export, produced on July 29, 2026 — with each row 
containing the search date and time, the network count, and a free-text justification. At least 65 California law 
enforcement agencies, including seven San Mateo County police departments (Belmont, East Palo Alto, Foster 
City, Menlo Park, Pacifica, San Bruno, and South San Francisco), publish search audit data from the same 
platform on their public transparency portals.
If the Department maintains that any requested field is exempt, please produce the entries with that field 
redacted, identify the specific exemption relied upon for each field withheld, and produce all reasonably 
segregable non-exempt portions (Gov. Code §§ 7922.525(b), 7922.540). Please provide responsive records in 
their native electronic format (CSV or the equivalent Flock export), and the determination required within 10 
days under Gov. Code § 7922.535.

As you were notified under request W013113-072526, records reflecting searches conducted by outside law 
enforcement agencies accessing the Department’s ALPR data through the Flock Safety network sharing 
platform are exempt from disclosure for the reasons set forth below:
The requested records, in their entirety, are exempt from disclosure pursuant to Government Code § 7923.600, 
Government Code § 7922.000, Evidence Code § 1040, and Civil Code § 1798.90.55.
Investigatory Records Exemption and Investigative Files Exemption (Government Code § 7923.600).
The requested audit records reflect search activity conducted by a law enforcement agency in connection with 
criminal investigations. Each log entry documents the existence, timing, and nature of an investigative act by 
another agency, including the category of criminal offense under investigation and the time frame of the search. 
Disclosure of these records would reveal confidential investigative activity. These records are therefore exempt 
from disclosure as investigatory records compiled by a law enforcement agency for law enforcement purposes 
pursuant to Government Code section 7923.600.
In addition, the audit log entries at issue reflect the investigative decisions and activities of other law 
enforcement agencies, not those of the San Mateo Police Department. The Department is not the originating 
agency for these investigative records and is not in a position to assess whether disclosure would compromise 
another agency’s ongoing or pending investigations.
Public Interest Balancing Test (Government Code § 7922.000).
Even to the extent any portion of the requested records might not fall squarely within a categorical exemption, 
the Department withholds these records under the public interest balancing test set forth in Government Code 
section 7922.000. The audit logs contain a combination of data fields that, taken together, would allow 
identification of specific criminal investigations being conducted by specific agencies during specific time 
periods. The public interest served by nondisclosure — protecting the integrity of active and pending criminal 
investigations across multiple jurisdictions — clearly outweighs the public interest served by disclosure.
Official Information Privilege (Evidence Code § 1040).
The requested records constitute official information acquired by law enforcement personnel in confidence. 
Disclosure of the investigative search activity of other agencies would be against the public interest because 
there is a necessity for preserving the confidentiality of this information that outweighs the necessity for 
disclosure in the interest of justice. Revealing which agencies are searching for which categories of criminal 
activity, and when, would compromise investigative strategies and techniques across multiple law enforcement 
jurisdictions.
Automated License Plate Reader (ALPR) Exemption (Civil Code § 1798.90.55).
In addition, your request has been denied pursuant to Government Code 7927.705, as records prohibited from
