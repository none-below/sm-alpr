Dear Brian C,
 
The City received the following records request from you on August 16, 2026.
 
Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 6250 et seq.), I request the following:
1. A list of any Flock Safety devices installed, activated, replaced, relocated, decommissioned, or taken offline 
since February 1, 2026, including for each device: the location (for relocations, both the prior and new 
location), device type or model/product designation, whether the device has ALPR capability, the date of the 
change, and any records documenting the reason for the change.
2. All invoices, purchase orders, order forms, quotes, contract amendments, or other purchase or payment 
records between Flock Safety and the City of San Mateo dated on or after February 1, 2026, including any 
installation fees or charges for devices or services beyond the Master Services Agreement and Amendment No. 
1.
3. A current list of all Flock Safety devices associated with the San Mateo Police Department, including for 
each device: location (address or intersection for fixed devices; designation as mobile/trailer-mounted if 
applicable), device type or model/product designation (e.g., Falcon, Condor, Picard, or other), whether the 
device has ALPR capability, ownership status (SMPD-owned, community camera, or other — please specify), 
and installation or activation date. Flock Camera Manager screenshots or a device-list export in the same form 
as the Department's February 26, 2026 production under W012201-022326 would be responsive.
4. As you are aware, the Department stated in February 2026 correspondence that two decommissioned 
devices would be deducted from the transparency site by Flock, and that cameras marked "in planning" were 
rejected locations that Flock was working to remove from the portal. I request all communications between the 
Department and Flock Safety since February 1, 2026 requesting additions, removals, or corrections to the 
device information displayed on the Department's transparency portal, and any records documenting when 
those changes were completed.
Please provide responsive records in electronic format where available.
 
Due to the large number of records which are associated with this request in item 4, the City will send you 
records on a rolling production basis. Please see the attachments for our response to the other items. 
 
We will further respond to your request with updates no later than 09/02/2026
If you have any questions, please contact me using the below contact information.
