To: Chief Barberini 
From.  Lieutenant Steve Casazza 
Date: August 4, 2026 
Re: Flock Audit July 2026 pursuant to Policy 463 
In August 2026, I conducted an audit of our internal and external ALPR usage for July 2026. The 
audit covered our internal and external usage for Flock Safety and internal usage of Vigilant.  
The review assessed access, search reasons, as well as for entries not in compliance with Lexipol 
Policy 463.  I verified that data sharing is limited to agencies within California, with no sharing 
occurring with federal law enforcement agencies or agencies outside the state.  I also confirmed 
that the “Revoke Out-of-State Sharing” setting is selected.  
 
On 07/14/2026, I temporarily disabled our network sharing in Flock while I drafted an ALPR 
Data Sharing Agreement. The agreement has now been finalized and is being sent to agencies 
that have requested access to our data. Once the signed agreements are received by me, I instruct 
the respective agency to request sharing access in Flock which I then approve.  As of this 
writing, we are only sharing with three agencies- Belmont PD, Foster City PD, and Palo Alto 
PD.  Additionally, our ALPR policy has been updated to show that a written request (Data 
Sharing Agreement) is now required before any agency is granted access to our shared ALPR 
data. 
 
Finally, I confirmed Lexipol Policy 463, SOP 205, Public Audit, and Network Sharing are 
displayed on our transparency portal.  A follow-up audit will be conducted in September to 
review ALPR usage for the month of August.
