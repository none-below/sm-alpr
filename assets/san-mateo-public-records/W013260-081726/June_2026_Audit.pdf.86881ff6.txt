Re: Flock Audit June 2026 pursuant to Policy 463 
In July 2026, I conducted an audit of our internal and external ALPR usage for June 2026. The 
audit covered our internal and external usage for Flock Safety and internal usage of Vigilant.  
The review assessed access, search reasons, as well as for entries not in compliance with Lexipol 
Policy 463.  I verified that data sharing is limited to agencies within California, with no sharing 
occurring with federal law enforcement agencies or agencies outside the state.  I also confirmed 
that the “Revoke Out-of-State Sharing” setting is selected.  
 
I confirmed Lexipol Policy 463, SOP 205, Public Audit, and Network Sharing are displayed on 
our transparency portal.  A follow-up audit will be conducted in August to review ALPR usage 
for the month of July.
