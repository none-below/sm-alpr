To: Chief Barberini

Date: June 1, 2026 
Re: Flock Audit May 2026 pursuant to Policy 463 
In June 2026, I conducted an audit of our internal and external ALPR usage for May 2026. The 
audit covered our internal and external usage for Flock Safety and internal usage of Vigilant.  
The review assessed access, search reasons, as well as for entries not in compliance with Lexipol 
Policy 463.  I verified that data sharing is limited to agencies within California, with no sharing 
occurring with federal law enforcement agencies or agencies outside the state.  I also confirmed 
that the “Revoke Out-of-State Sharing” setting is selected.  
 
On 05/18/2026, our Flock Public Audit went live and is displaying on our transparency portal.  
The public audit displays our internal searches across the Flock network.  I also confirmed 
Lexipol Policy 463 and SOP 205 are displaying on our portal.  On 05/21/2026, Sgt. Trujillo and I 
provided Flock training to the Patrol Leadership group.  We have also begun department wide 
Flock training.  The training will start with Patrol and move throughout the department until 
every user has received the refresher training.  A follow-up audit will be conducted in July to 
review ALPR usage for the month of June.
