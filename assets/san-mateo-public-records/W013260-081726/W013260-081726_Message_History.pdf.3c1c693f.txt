Record(s) Requested: Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I 
request the following records from the San Mateo Police Department. This request seeks existing records 
only (Gov. Code § 7922.530; Sierra Club v. Superior Court (2013) 57 Cal.4th 157).
Context
As you are aware, SMPD Policy 463, section 463.10 (ALPR Data Search Audits, effective December 22, 
2025, produced under W012666-050426) provides: "It is the responsibility of the ALPR Committee 
Captain or the authorized designee to ensure an audit is conducted of ALPR data searches at least 
monthly for internal ALPR users and quarterly for external CA public agency users. The audit shall be 
documented in the form of an internal department memorandum to the Chief of Police. The 
memorandum shall include any data errors found so any such errors can be corrected. After review by 
the Chief of Police, the memorandum and any associated documentation shall be filed and retained."
In response to W012461-040226 (closed April 8, 2026), which requested quarterly external audit 
memoranda produced under Policy 463.10, the Department produced four monthly audit memoranda

covering December 2025 through March 2026 and stated "We have no additional records." Since that 
response, more than two full quarterly periods have elapsed under any quarterly schedule — whether 
measured as calendar quarters or from the policy's December 22, 2025 effective date. Policy 463.10 does 
not define the quarterly schedule, so this request is framed to cover whichever schedule the Department 
applies.
Records requested — covering December 22, 2025 through the date of production:
1. Each memorandum to the Chief of Police documenting a quarterly audit of ALPR data searches by 
external CA public agency users under Policy 463.10, for each quarterly period that has ended since 
December 22, 2025 under the quarterly schedule the Department applies — whether calendar quarters, 
quarters measured from the policy's effective date, City fiscal-year quarters, or another schedule.
2. Any record establishing or describing the quarterly schedule or audit calendar applied under Policy 
463.10 — for example, an audit calendar, scheduling or tasking record, or assignment to the ALPR 
Committee Captain or an authorized designee.
3. For each audit in item 1, the record identifying the external CA public agencies whose searches were 
reviewed, including any list of the Department's network-sharing partners in effect during the audited 
period.
4. For each audit in item 1, records sufficient to identify the "associated documentation" filed and 
retained under Policy 463.10 — for example, the file names, export or report dates, covering date ranges, 
and source platform of the platform exports, network audit reports, or search logs the auditor reviewed. 
This item seeks identification of the retained documentation, not production of the underlying exports 
themselves.
5. Records reflecting the Chief of Police's review of each item 1 memorandum and its subsequent filing 
and retention, with dates. This item seeks transmittal, routing, review, or filing records associated with 
the memoranda themselves; it does not ask for review of any general email corpus.
If no records are located for any individual item, please respond to that item separately and continue 
processing the remaining items.
If the Department contends that any monthly audit memorandum constitutes a quarterly external audit 
under Policy 463.10, please identify which memorandum constitutes the quarterly external audit for each
closed quarterly period, and identify the portions of that memorandum reflecting the external scope of 
the audit — the external agencies reviewed and the searches examined.
If the Department contends that no quarterly period has yet closed under its schedule, please identify the 
schedule applied and the end date of the first quarterly period.
Each item is severable. If any single item requires additional processing time, please respond to the 
remaining items on the statutory timeline rather than extending the request as a whole.
If any portion is denied, please provide specific exemption citations per Gov. Code § 7922.540 and
