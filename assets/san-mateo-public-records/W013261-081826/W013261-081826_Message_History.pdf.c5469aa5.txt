The City received the following records request from you on August 18, 2026.
 
Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the following records 
from the San Mateo Police Department. This request seeks existing records only (Gov. Code § 7922.530; 
Sierra Club v. Superior Court (2013) 57 Cal.4th 157).
Context
As you are aware, SMPD Policy 463, section 463.8 (Releasing ALPR Data, effective December 22, 2025, 
produced under W012666-050426) provides that ALPR data "may be shared only with public agencies and 
only as otherwise permitted by law," and states: "The following procedures are guidelines for ALPR requests: 
(a) The agency makes a request for the ALPR data that includes: 1. The name of the agency. 2. The name of the 
person requesting. 3. The intended purpose of obtaining the information. (b) The request is reviewed by the 
ALPR Committee Captain or the authorized designee and approved before the request is fulfilled."
The Department's Flock Safety transparency portal listed approximately 259 agencies receiving San Mateo 
ALPR data in early July 2026; on or about July 16, 2026 the portal listed no recipient agencies; as of August 
13, 2026 it listed five agencies; and as of August 17, 2026 it lists six: Belmont Police Department, Brisbane 
Police Department, California Highway Patrol, Foster City Police Department, Palo Alto Police Department, 
and South San Francisco Police Department.
Records held by Flock Safety as the Department's contractor are Department records regardless of physical 
location (Gov. Code § 7920.530; City of San Jose v. Superior Court (2017) 2 Cal.5th 608), as reflected in the 
Department's production of a Flock platform event log export under W012570-041926.
Records requested — covering December 22, 2025 through the date of production:
1. Any written criteria, standards, or guidelines governing the approval, denial, periodic review, or 
termination of ALPR data-sharing relationships with California public agencies, beyond the text of Policy 
463.8 itself — including any criteria applied in the 2026 changes to the Department's sharing list.
2. Records of the decision reflected in the transparency portal on or about July 16, 2026, when the list of 
recipient agencies went from approximately 259 to none — including any memorandum, directive, instruction, 
or other record of the decision, its authorization, and the criterion applied.
3. For each agency listed as a recipient as of the date this request is processed — as of August 17, 2026: 
Belmont Police Department, Brisbane Police Department, California Highway Patrol, Foster City Police
