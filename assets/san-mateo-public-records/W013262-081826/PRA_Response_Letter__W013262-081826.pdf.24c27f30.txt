3. Records reflecting the routing of W013113-072526 and W013137-072926 for the legal

transmittal records themselves; it does not seek the content of any legal advice. 
 
Department Response: 
Your request for records relating to routing for legal review has been denied. California 
Government Code § 7927.705 exempts records protected by privileges under the Evidence Code. 
This includes attorney-client privileged communications pursuant to California Evidence Code 
section 954. 
Work Product Privilege: 
California Government Code section 7927.705 exempts records protected by privileges under the 
Evidence Code or any state other law. Therefore, the records requested are exempt from 
disclosure pursuant to Evidence Code section 954 and CCP section 2018.030.
