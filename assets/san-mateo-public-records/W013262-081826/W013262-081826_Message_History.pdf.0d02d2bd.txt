Thank you for your interest in public records of the City of San Mateo.  Your request was received by the City 
of San Mateo on 8/18/2026 and given the reference number W013262-081826 for tracking purposes.
Record(s) Requested: Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I 
request the following records from the San Mateo Police Department. This request seeks existing records 
only (Gov. Code § 7922.530; Sierra Club v. Superior Court (2013) 57 Cal.4th 157).
Context
As you are aware, the Department denied PRA W013113-072526 in full on July 29, 2026, denied PRA 
W013137-072926 in full on August 4, 2026, and denied item 3 of PRA W012665-050426 by letter. The 
August 4 response letter states that "[i]n accordance to Government Code Section § 7922.525 a legal 
review of your request was completed." Each of these three letters concludes without a signature block, a 
name, or a title.
Government Code section 7922.540(b) provides that "[t]he notification of denial shall set forth the names 
and titles or positions of each person responsible for the denial."

Records held by Flock Safety as the Department's contractor are Department records regardless of 
physical location (Gov. Code § 7920.530; City of San Jose v. Superior Court (2017) 2 Cal.5th 608), as 
reflected in the Department's production of a Flock platform event log export under W012570-041926.
Records requested:
1. The names and titles or positions of each person responsible for the denial of PRA W013113-072526, 
of PRA W013137-072926, and of item 3 of PRA W012665-050426, as set forth in Government Code 
section 7922.540(b), together with any record identifying the person or persons who made the 
determination to deny each request.
2. For the period July 20, 2026 through the date of production, the Flock platform event log for the 
Department's account, limited to report generation, audit export, network-audit, and search-audit query 
events, in the native export format in which Flock provides it. This is the record type the Department 
obtained and produced under W012570-041926; this item seeks the same record type for the stated 
period and event scope.
3. Records reflecting the routing of W013113-072526 and W013137-072926 for the legal review described 
in the August 4, 2026 response letter — transmittal, assignment, tasking, or routing records between 
Police Department staff and the City Attorney's Office, and the return of each review to the Department, 
with dates. This item seeks the routing and transmittal records themselves; it does not seek the content of 
any legal advice.
4. Any record created in the course of responding to W013113-072526 or W013137-072926 that identifies 
which fields, portions, or categories of the requested audit entries were considered for release or for 
redaction — for example, a review worksheet, redaction log, field-by-field notation, marked-up export, 
or exemption index. This item seeks the record itself, not a narrative description of any analysis.
5. If any record responsive to item 3 or item 4 is withheld on the basis of attorney-client privilege or 
attorney work product, an index identifying each withheld record by date, author, recipient, and record 
type, and the specific privilege asserted for each.
If no records are located for any individual item, please respond to that item separately and continue 
processing the remaining items.
Each item is severable. If any single item requires additional processing time, please respond to the 
remaining items on the statutory timeline rather than extending the request as a whole.
If any portion is denied, please provide specific exemption citations per Gov. Code § 7922.540 and 
identify the withheld record type and volume. Exact copies of existing records are requested per Gov. 
Code § 7922.530(a); this request does not ask the Department to create any record that does not exist — 
where none exists, a statement to that effect is requested.
Thank you.
