Dear Brian C,
 
The City received the following records request from you on August 18, 2026.
 
Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the following records 
from the San Mateo Police Department. This request seeks existing records only (Gov. Code § 7922.530).
As you are aware, the 2025-2026 San Mateo County Civil Grand Jury released its report "Is Our Flock of 
ALPR Cameras Properly Tended?" on August 3, 2026, with responses requested under Penal Code sections 
933 and 933.05. This request covers August 3, 2026 through the date of production — a period of a few weeks 
— and does not seek the Department's or the City's own internal draft responses.
To assist the search: responsive records may use terms such as "grand jury," "civil grand jury," "SMCCGJ," 
"Properly Tended," "ALPR report," "Flock report," "grand jury response," "933," or "933.05." This list is 
illustrative only; the request is not limited to records containing these terms. Given the short covered period, 
an effective alternative search is messages exchanged with other law enforcement agencies' or the association's
email domains during the period, reviewed for reference to the report.
Records requested:
1. Communications — including email, text messages, and written correspondence — between the Department 
and any other law enforcement agency, city, town, the County, or the San Mateo County Police Chiefs and 
Sheriffs Association, referencing the report or any agency's response to it.
2. Calendar entries, meeting invitations, or agendas for any meeting or call at which the report or responses to 
it is a listed topic, and any minutes or notes of such a meeting received or retained by the Department.
3. Any draft response, response template, or model language received by the Department from another agency 
or association in connection with responses to the report.
If no records are located for any individual item, please respond to that item separately. For any 
communication withheld under a claimed exemption, please produce the segregable date, sender, recipients, 
and subject per Gov. Code § 7922.525(b). If any portion is denied, please provide exemption citations per Gov. 
Code § 7922.540.
Thank you.
 
As you are aware, batches of responsive records have been released on a rolling basis to show a good faith and 
diligent effort to complete your request. A final batch of records has been released and made available on the
