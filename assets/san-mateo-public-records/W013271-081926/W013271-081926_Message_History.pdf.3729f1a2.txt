The City received the below records request from you on August 19, 2026.
 
Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the following records 
from the San Mateo Police Department. This request seeks existing records only (Gov. Code § 7922.530).
Context
As you are aware, the Department's July 4, 2026 correspondence states that "when the San Mateo Police 
Department becomes aware that an agency is not complying with applicable state law, the department assesses 
whether to discontinue data sharing with that agency," and that the City may act on such information 
"[r]egardless of how the City becomes aware." As you are also aware, at the June 4, 2026 meeting and in 
correspondence of June 22 and July 12, 2026, I brought to the Department's attention searches that Stockton 
Police Department's published audit records attribute to requests from the U.S. Marshals Service, and at least 
one search that Yuba City Police Department's records attribute to a DEA inquiry, and noted their potential 
reach into the regional sharing network that included San Mateo data.
This request covers June 4, 2026 through the date of production. Items 1 and 3 are scoped to named 
counterparties and this subject; no review of any general email corpus is requested. To assist the search: 
responsive records may use terms such as "Stockton," "USMS," "U.S. Marshals," "Yuba City," "DEA," 
"federal," or "on behalf of." This list is illustrative only; the request is not limited to records containing these 
terms.
Records requested:
1. Communications between the Department and Stockton Police Department or Yuba City Police Department 
referencing searches conducted on behalf of, or at the request of, federal agencies.
2. Records of any assessment conducted under the practice described in the Department's July 4, 2026 
correspondence with respect to Stockton Police Department or Yuba City Police Department — including 
records reflecting the assessment's initiation, the criteria applied, and its disposition.
3. Communications between the ALPR Committee Captain or authorized designee (per the Department's 
records, Lt. Steve Casazza) and Flock Safety referencing the searches described in the Context section or 
referencing federal-agency-related search activity by partner agencies.
4. Any record of review of Stockton Police Department's or Yuba City Police Department's status as a recipient 
of San Mateo ALPR data in connection with changes to the Department's sharing configuration during July

5. Any network audit report or equivalent record listing searches conducted by other agencies against San
