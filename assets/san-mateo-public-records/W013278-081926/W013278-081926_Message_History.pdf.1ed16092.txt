Thank you for your interest in public records of the City of San Mateo.  Your request was received by the City 
of San Mateo on 8/19/2026 and given the reference number W013278-081926 for tracking purposes.
Record(s) Requested: This request seeks one document and the records surrounding it: the data 
protection agreement that Flock Safety directs its customers to execute. It covers January 1, 2020 
through the date of production, and concerns the City's agreements with Flock Group Inc. d/b/a Flock 
Safety — the 2020 service agreement, the 2023 Master Services Agreement, and Amendments No. 1 and 
No. 2.
Flock's Privacy Policy, at https://www.flocksafety.com/legal/privacy-policy (version marked "Last 
Updated: August 1, 2025"), states: "Data protection and privacy laws in your jurisdiction may impose 
certain responsibilities on you and your use of our products. You are responsible for ensuring that you 
comply with all applicable laws when you use the Flock Services or products, including, where so 
required, to execute a mutually agreed upon data protection agreement."
As you are aware, the Department is both an ALPR operator under California Civil Code § 1798.90.51 
and an ALPR end-user under § 1798.90.53, and both sections require reasonable security procedures 
and practices — operational, administrative, technical, and physical — to protect ALPR information 
from unauthorized access, destruction, use, modification, or disclosure. Flock directs its customers to 
execute a data protection agreement where their jurisdiction imposes such responsibilities. This request 
seeks the discrete documents held in the City's contract, purchasing, and vendor files that would show 
whether the City did so as its vendor directs, and how that obligation is allocated between the two.
1. The executed "mutually agreed upon data protection agreement" described in the passage quoted 
above — the instrument Flock's Privacy Policy directs its customers to execute — whatever title it 
actually carries (data protection agreement, data processing agreement, data processing addendum, 
DPA, data security agreement, or similar), including all exhibits, schedules, and amendments.

2. Any template, form, model, or standard version of that instrument held in City or Department files, in 
any state — executed, unexecuted, draft, or redlined.
3. The index, inventory, document list, or file listing for the Flock vendor file and contract file, as 
maintained in the City's contract management, purchasing, or records system — the listing itself, 
sufficient to identify every document filed under the Flock agreements. Not the underlying documents.
4. Contract review checklists, contract routing or approval forms, contract summary sheets, and 
signature routing slips prepared for the 2020 agreement, the 2023 Master Services Agreement, and each 
amendment.
5. The City's standard contract provision, exhibit, or template addressing data security, data privacy, or 
vendor handling of personal information, together with any record documenting its inclusion in, waiver 
from, or omission from the Flock agreements.
6. Any Management Control Agreement, CJIS Security Addendum, or equivalent instrument executed 
with Flock or with any Flock subcontractor, and records sufficient to identify whether any hot list used 
by the Department within the Flock platform derives from CLETS, NCIC, or the Stolen Vehicle System.
7. Any copy of Flock's Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, or other published vendor terms maintained in 
the City's contract or vendor files, and any record identifying which version was reviewed at each 
execution.
8. Records sufficient to identify each subcontractor or third party Flock uses to store or process San 
Mateo ALPR data, and any City approval, review, or acknowledgment of them.
9. Any notification from Flock to the City of a data breach, security incident, or unauthorized access 
affecting San Mateo ALPR data.
Every item above seeks a discrete document maintained in a contract, purchasing, or vendor file. No 
item requests a search of any email account, and no item is satisfied by production from the email review 
pending under W012462-040226 or under any other request. Item 3 is a single listing and can be 
produced independently of every other item. This request is addressed to the City. Responsive records 
are likely held by the City Clerk, by Finance or Purchasing, and by the City Attorney's Office. To the 
extent items 6, 8, or 9 are held by the Police Department instead, I ask that those individual items be 
routed accordingly rather than treated as non-responsive.
Records held by Flock as the City's contractor are City records under Gov. Code § 7920.530 and City of 
San Jose v. Superior Court (2017) 2 Cal.5th 608, as the Department's production of Flock platform 
event-log data under W012570-041926 confirms.
A determination under Gov. Code § 7922.535 is requested within 10 days. If any item is unclear, Gov. 
Code § 7922.600 provides for the City's assistance in identifying responsive records, and I will narrow on 
request.
