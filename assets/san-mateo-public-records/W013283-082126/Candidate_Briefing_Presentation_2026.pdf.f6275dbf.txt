COMMUNITY SERVICE OFFICERS (CSO)

CSO's enhance police services and community
relations by supporting the department by handling
non-emergency calls for service and follow-up tasks,
including processing crime scenes, taking police
reports with no immediate suspect, and analyzing
cases to develop leads. They work closely with officers
and detectives to advance investigations and help
solve cases. CSOs also provide administrative support
and assist with community outreach.

CRIME REDUCTION UNIT (CRU)

CRU's primary mission is to combat street-level gang,
narcotics, and vice-related criminal activity, including
human trafficking. The unit also supports the
Criminal Investigations Bureau and Patrol Division
through targeted enforcement, surveillance, search
warrant execution, fugitive apprehension, and expert
court testimony. They are also responsible for Gang
Registrants.

DOWNTOWN UNIT (DTU)

DTU maintains the safety, accessibility, and quality of
life downtown and citywide. Officers address
disorderly conduct and nuisance issues, actively
problem-solve community concerns, and connect
individuals experiencing homelessness with
appropriate resources. They engage with merchants,
residents, and visitors, support city and community
events, and collaborate with local organizations and
service providers. Through professional,
compassionate enforcement and proactive
community engagement, the unit ensures our
downtown remains a safe hub to visit.

TRAFFIC UNIT

The San Mateo Police Department, in partnership with the City of San Mateo Department of
Public Works, works to keep our community safe by maintaining and monitoring roadways,
sidewalks, bike lanes, signage, and traffic signals, while enforcing traffic laws for all who travel
through San Mateo.

Through the Three E’s of Traffic Safety—Education, Enforcement, and Engineering—the Traffic
Unit takes a comprehensive approach to protect motorists, cyclists, and pedestrians, keeping
streets safe, efficient, and accessible.

* Education: The Traffic Unit engages the community through outreach, school programs, and
social media campaigns to promote safe driving, cycling, and pedestrian practices that
prevent collisions and save lives.

+ Enforcement: Motor Officers target high-collision, high-congestion, and high-complaint
areas, addressing speeding, distracted driving, and impaired driving. They also manage
traffic during collisions, special events, and civic functions.

« Engineering: In partnership with Public Works, the unit evaluates roadway safety concerns,
using data and traffic patterns to recommend improvements in road design, signage, signal
timing, and infrastructure.

Parking Enforcement

To better serve our community and address evolving parking needs, the City of San Mateo
contracts parking enforcement with LAZ Parking to address Downtown parking enforcement,
the Residential Permit Parking Program (RPPP), street sweeping enforcement, and metered and
commercial zone parking.

Police personnel continue to oversee our Abandoned Vehicle Abatement Unit and other
specialized enforcement requiring a community policing approach.

mobile trailers to enhance public safety. ALPR technology assists in locating stolen vehicles, missing persons, and wanted

| Real Time Information Center (RTIC)

The San Mateo Police Department launched its Real-Time Information Center (RTIC), marking a
significant advancement in the use of technology to enhance public safety. The RTIC is staffed by
trained personnel from Dispatch and Patrol, who provide real-time intelligence to support officers in
the field and improve decision-making during active incidents.

Initial efforts focus on integrating critical data into Fusus, the department's primary platform for live
video feeds and intelligence sharing. Through voluntary participation, businesses and residents may
purchase a Fusus device that allows officers to access camera footage in real time during incidents.
Information about the program is available on page 30 under San Mateo Connect.

The Center represents an important step toward establishing a modern, technology-driven hub that
strengthens operational effectiveness and community safety.
