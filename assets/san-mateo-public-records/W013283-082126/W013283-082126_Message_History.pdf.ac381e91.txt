The City received the following records request from you on August 21, 2026.
 
Subject: Public Records Act request — recording and materials, August 20, 2026 City Council Special Meeting 
(2026 Candidate Briefing)
Under the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request copies of the following 
records concerning the City Council Special Meeting held August 20, 2026 at 6:00 PM in the City Hall Council 
Chamber, agendized as "Candidate Briefing for the November 2026 Election" (PrimeGov meeting ID 2372).
1. All audio and video recordings of the meeting made by or at the direction of the City, including the Channel 
San Mateo broadcast recording, any recording made for later posting to the City's YouTube channel or Public 
Meeting Portal, and any Zoom cloud recording of webinar ID 899 4193 7699.
2. Any caption, transcript, or CART files generated for the meeting, in their native format (for example .vtt, .srt, 
or .txt).
3. All presentations, slide decks, handouts, and other materials displayed or distributed at the meeting, 
including any submitted to the City Clerk in advance under the agenda's presentation-submission procedure.
4. The candidate briefing packet or materials provided to attendees, including any department-specific 
materials.
5. Records identifying the City staff who presented at the meeting, by name and title, and any run-of-show, 
speaking order, or internal agenda prepared for the briefing.
6. The agenda for this meeting as originally posted and as amended, together with records showing the date 
and time each version was posted and what the amendment changed.
7. The minutes of the meeting, or the Clerk's notes if minutes are not yet approved.
8. Communications between the City Clerk's Office and any department head or their staff, from August 1, 
2026 through August 21, 2026, concerning the content or scope of the candidate briefing.
As you are aware, Government Code § 54953.5(b) provides that a recording of an open and public meeting 
made by or at the direction of a local agency is subject to inspection under the Public Records Act, and that 
such a recording may be erased or destroyed 30 days after the recording is made. That 30-day period for this 
meeting runs through approximately September 19, 2026. I am asking that the recordings described in item 1
