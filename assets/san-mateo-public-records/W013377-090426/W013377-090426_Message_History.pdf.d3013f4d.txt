6abce941fdc7 (03/05/2026, 10:15:07 PM UTC), the platform's offense category is "Homicide/Death 
Investigation", the words "Homicide/Death" being the category's identifying text and "Investigation" the 
remainder of its label, and the typed reason is "Ivne".
This request is for the platform's search-audit record of the query itself, not for any investigative file, case 
report, or other record that the entry may reference.
This request is distinct from request W013308-082426 and is not satisfied by the record produced under it. 
That request was answered on September 2, 2026 with a spreadsheet export rendered to PDF, in which the 
words "Homicide/Death" were redacted from the category and the word "Investigation" was left in place, the 
typed reason did not appear, no withheld field was marked as such, and no field-specific statement was 
provided. This request seeks different record products, the platform's Audit PDF report and its CSV export, 
with withheld portions marked in place and a determination specific to this record. Please process it as a new 
request.
1. The platform's Audit PDF report covering March 5, 2026, as generated by the platform, including the row 
for the search identified above with every field that report includes.
2. The record for the same search in the platform's CSV export, with every field the export includes.
For both items, where any portion of a field is withheld, please mark it in place with a marker that 
distinguishes a withheld value from a field the platform left empty.
If information is withheld from any field of this record, please provide, as part of the written determination 
under Government Code section 7922.540, a statement specific to this record that identifies each field from 
which information was withheld, the exemption relied upon for that field, and the basis on which no reasonably 
segregable portion of that field could be released under section 7922.525(b). If the offense category, or the 
words "Homicide/Death" within it, is withheld, please state the basis on which that value, which the 
Department publishes on its transparency portal for each of its searches, is exempt as to this record. If the
