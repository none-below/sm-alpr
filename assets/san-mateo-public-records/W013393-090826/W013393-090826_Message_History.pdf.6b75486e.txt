The Department has conducted a review of its redaction procedures in consideration of the information made 
publicly available through the Flock Safety Transparency Portal as well as the applicable Government Codes. 
As a result of that review, the Department has revised certain redaction practices applicable to the audit records 
being produced.
 
These revisions are intended to expand, rather than restrict, the information made available to the public. 
Accordingly, the Department will reissue previously produced audit records on a rolling basis on request 
#W012541-041426 beginning with the earliest production and proceeding chronologically. 
 
The records previously produced in response to this request contained redactions made pursuant to the 
investigative records exemption under Government Code § 7923.600. Under the Department’s prior redaction 
process, redacted portions of the records appeared as blank or white space rather than as visible black redaction 
blocks. This Government Code exemption was not cited with these individual releases, moving forward the 
Government Code exemptions will be cited along with the document. 
 
The Department has revised its redaction process so that redacted information will appear as black blocks. 
Accordingly, pursuant to Government Code § 7922.580(c), the Department will continue to provide these 
records in PDF format to preserve the security and integrity of the redactions while providing access to all 
reasonably segregable, nonexempt responsive information and your current request for the following native 
files is denied under Government Code § 7922.580(c), but will be provided again in PDF format under request 
#W012541-041426. For item 2 there are no responsive records as the Department continues to maintain these 
documents.
