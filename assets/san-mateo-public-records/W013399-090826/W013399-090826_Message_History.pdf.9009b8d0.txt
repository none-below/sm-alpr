Record(s) Requested: Public Records Act Request — ALPR reason-entry compliance and enforcement 
records
As you are aware, the Department produced on June 23, 2026 under W012320-031126 an interoffice 
memorandum dated July 22, 2022, titled "MANDATORY REASON FOR ALPR QUERIES," which 
states that "inadequate entry of the REASON for your ALPR query will result in the suspension of your 
access to the databases and receipt of a Record of Discussion (ROD)," and that "Remedial training is 
required for reinstated access." That memorandum requires, for every query, a "Case or Incident 
number associated with the query," or failing that "a specific crime code or name of the crime and the 
vehicle description." For queries made on behalf of outside agencies it requires agency name, requestor 
name, and case or incident number, and states that "If there is no case or incident number, the query 
will not be completed."
The same production included June 27, 2022 correspondence in which a Criminal Investigations Bureau 
sergeant wrote "I am conducting an ALPR audit per department policy" and, addressing a specific 
query, recorded the Department's position that a reason of "INVESTIGATION" "does not meet the 
requirement" — the same deficiency the July 22, 2022 memorandum states "will result in the suspension 
of your access to the databases and receipt of a Record of Discussion."
As you are also aware, the search audit records the Department has produced under W012541-041426 
and W012818-053026 contain 105,315 queries for the period January 1, 2023 through August 31, 2026. 
Of those, at least 64,891 — approximately 62 percent — state "investigation" as the entire reason for the 
query. The median such query carried a networkCount of 112.
I request the following records, from June 1, 2022 to the present:
