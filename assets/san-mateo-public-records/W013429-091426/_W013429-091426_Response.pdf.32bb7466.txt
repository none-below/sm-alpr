See attached documents.
Portions of your request are withheld pursuant to the following:
Government Code § 7923.600, as they constitute as law-enforcement investigative record.

Pursuant to the California Public Records Act, the City has redacted the personal home
addresses of members of the public contained within the requested records. This
information is protected under Article I, Section 1 of the California Constitution, which
establishes privacy as an inalienable right and the exemption of Government Code
Section 7922.000.

The City has determined that individuals retain a substantial and reasonable expectation of
privacy and safety in their home addresses. Conversely, there is little to no legitimate public
interest in disclosing the exact residential locations of private citizens, as such information
does not shed light on the conduct or operations of the City. Because the public interest
served by not disclosing this private information clearly outweighs the public interest
served by disclosure, the addresses have been appropriately segregated and redacted.
