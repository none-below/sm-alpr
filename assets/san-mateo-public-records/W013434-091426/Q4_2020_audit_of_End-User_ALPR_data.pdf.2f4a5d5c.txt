Introduction 
 
Per SMPD Standard Operating Procedure 205, our Department conducts quarterly audits of our ‘End-User’ 
data to ensure our employees are reviewing data in a manner consistent with policy and procedure. The data 
reviewed is generated and provided by the Northern California Regional Intelligence Center (NCRIC), as 
they are the keeper of this data. The results of these audits are summarized and presented to the Support 
Services Captain for review and later for retention by the Support Services Division. 
 
Quarter 4, 2020 (October, November, December) 
 
Our employees conducted 207 searches of ALPR historical data through the NCRIC platform, which is 
almost a 200% increase in quarterly usage. This increase is reasonable, as almost half of the searches were 
related to the homicide on October 30th. A handful of searches were conducted as tests to check that the 
system was functioning correctly. All searches were determined to list the required criteria as required by our 
policy and procedure. As is usual, the top two reasons for searches were “Stolen or Wanted” or “Criminal or 
Arrest.”
