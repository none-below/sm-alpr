The City received the following records request from you on September 14, 2026.
Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the following records 
from the San Mateo Police Department.
This request seeks existing records only (Gov. Code § 7922.530; Sierra Club v. Superior Court (2013) 57 
Cal.4th 157). Each item is limited to records created on or before the date of this request.
As you are aware, SMPD SOP 205 provides, in its section titled "NCRIC Astrometrics Platform," that "[t]he 
Support Services Captain, or their designee, shall conduct audits of end-users usage of the NCRIC platform on 
a quarterly basis," and that "[o]ur Department can obtain such data for the purposes of an audit directly from 
the NCRIC." That provision appears in identical terms in the February 10, 2021 revision of SOP 205 produced 
in W012198-022326 and in the November 10, 2025 revision produced in W012159-021226. In response to 
W012461-040226 and again in response to W012666-050426, the Department stated that there were no 
responsive records for end-user audit records concerning the NCRIC platform under SOP 205 § 205.5.1. This 
request therefore does not seek those audits. It seeks the underlying access data and the records of what has 
become of San Mateo Police Department ALPR data held on the NCRIC Astrometrics platform.
As you are further aware, the Department's own Flock ALPR training materials, dated February 8, 2021 and 
produced in W012672-050426, state that "[d]ata is held for 30 days, then automatically deleted and purged 
from Flock's system," and that "[d]ata from Flock are uploaded to NCRIC – Astrometrics and held for 365 
days." The infrastructure code since published by NCRIC's vendor confirms that mechanism: an automated 
process retrieved each participating agency's prior day of Flock license plate reads on a daily schedule and 
wrote those reads into a separate NCRIC-operated database, with associated plate images stored in a separate 
cloud storage bucket. That stored copy is distinct from the Flock platform, is retained far beyond Flock's 30-
day period, and access to it is not reflected in Flock's audit records. This request concerns that NCRIC-held 
copy of San Mateo Police Department data and is not limited to activity visible through the Flock platform.
As you are further aware, the Department's Flock Safety transparency portal 
(https://transparency.flocksafety.com/san-mateo-ca-pd) states, under "Access Policy," that "[a]ll system access 
requires a valid reason and is stored indefinitely." That statement describes the record of who accessed the 
system and why, distinct from the underlying plate-read data subject to the 30- and 365-day retention periods 
described above, and represents that access record as retained without a time limit.
As you are further aware, the vendor that has operated the NCRIC Astrometrics platform publicized its role 
providing that platform to NCRIC in a November 17, 2022 press release, in which NCRIC's Assistant Deputy 
Director for Information Technology & Cyber Security was quoted endorsing the platform. That release states

that "[c]lient administrators also have access to real-time data ingestion and usage logs," that the platform 
"maintains all usage records," and that it "alerts agency stakeholders of any data breach." The usage records 
and access data this request seeks were therefore represented, by the platform's operating vendor, as existing 
and as available to client administrators.
As you are further aware, SOP 205 treats records held by a third-party hosting provider as available to the 
Department. It states that a record of the operator's use of the Flock Safety platform "is also maintained by 
Amazon Web Services and available for review and audit by SMPD," and that a record of end-user access to 
the Vigilant Solutions platform "is also maintained by Verio, a subsidiary of NTT Communications, and 
available for review and audit by SMPD." This request applies that same principle to the hosting provider for 
the NCRIC Astrometrics platform.
Scope of records. SMPD's executed Memorandum of Understanding with NCRIC, dated August 2014 and 
produced in W012376-032026, provides at Section 6 that "Member Agencies remain the official custodian of 
all information contributed to the NCRIC-DSP," that Public Records Act requests "will be referred to the 
Member Agency that is the owner of the requested data," and that such Member Agency "will be responsible 
for responding to the request." Records held by NCRIC on SMPD's behalf as contributed-data custodian, and 
records SMPD is entitled to obtain under SOP 205, are writings "prepared, owned, used, or retained" by 
SMPD within the meaning of Gov. Code § 7920.530 and are within the scope of this request regardless of 
which server they reside on.
Records requested:
1. Any access, usage, or audit data concerning the NCRIC Astrometrics platform obtained by the Department 
from NCRIC or its vendor at any time and for any purpose, whether or not obtained in connection with an 
audit — including any export, report, log, or file reflecting end-user sign-on information, date and time of 
access, search terms or case numbers entered, or the connection between searched data and the stated 
justification.
2. Any request, correspondence, or service ticket by which the Department asked NCRIC, or NCRIC's vendor, 
for access data, audit data, or usage reports concerning the NCRIC Astrometrics platform.
3. Records concerning the disposition of San Mateo Police Department ALPR data upon the termination, wind-
down, or cessation of operations of the vendor that operated the NCRIC Astrometrics platform — including 
any certificate of destruction, deletion confirmation, data-return record, transition or migration record, or 
correspondence concerning what became of that data.
4. Any notice, advisory, correspondence, or communication the Department received from NCRIC, or from the 
vendor that operated the NCRIC Astrometrics platform, concerning (a) the wind-down, cessation of operations, 
or change in status of that vendor; (b) the migration, transfer, deletion, or continued retention of San Mateo 
Police Department ALPR data held on the platform; or (c) the public disclosure of the platform's infrastructure 
code, configuration, or audit records. This item includes any record showing that no such notice was received, 
to the extent one exists.
If no records are located for any individual item, please respond to that item separately and continue 
processing the remaining items.
