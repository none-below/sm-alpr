Introduction 
 
Per SMPD Standard Operating Procedure 205, our Department conducts quarterly audits of our 
‘End-User’ data to ensure our employees are reviewing data in a manner consistent with policy and 
procedure. The data reviewed is generated and provided by the Norther California Regional 
Intelligence Center (NCRIC), as they are the keeper of this data. The results of these audits are 
summarized and presented to the Support Services Captain for review and later for retention by the 
Support Services Division. 
 
Quarter 1, 2020 (January, February, and March) 
 
Our employees conducted 48 searches of ALPR historical data through the NCRIC platform. 100% 
of the searches were determined to list the required criteria as required by our policy and procedure. 
The high majority, 90%, of these searches were related to criminal investigations (refer to chart 
below).
