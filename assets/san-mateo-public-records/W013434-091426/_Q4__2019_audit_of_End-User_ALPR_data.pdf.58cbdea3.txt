Introduction 
 
Per SMPD Standard Operating Procedure 205, our Department conducts quarterly audits of our 
‘End-User’ data to ensure our employees are reviewing data in a manner consistent with policy and 
procedure. The data reviewed is generated and provided by the Northern California Regional 
Intelligence Center (NCRIC), as they are the keeper of this data. The results of these audits are 
summarized and presented to the Support Services Captain for review and later for retention by the 
Support Services Division. 
 
Quarter 4, 2019 (October, November, and December) 
 
Our employees conducted 66 searches of ALPR historical data through the NCRIC platform, which 
was 46% fewer than Quarter 3, 2019. All Quarter 4 searches were determined to list the required 
criteria of case number and justification for the search. The high majority, 86%, of these searches 
were related to a criminal or arrest-related reason (refer to chart below).
