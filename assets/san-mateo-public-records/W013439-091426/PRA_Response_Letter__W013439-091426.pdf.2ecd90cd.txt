(1) The CAD event chronology, unit history, or dispatch log for the call — i.e., the time-
stamped record of unit assignment, status changes, and dispatcher/radio-traffic notations. 
 
Department Response: 
Responsive records provided in the attachments portion of this request on GovQA. Pursuant to 
the California Public Records Act, the City has redacted the personal license plates of members 
of the public contained within the requested records. This information is protected under Article 
I, Section 1 of the California Constitution, which establishes privacy as an inalienable right and 
the exemption of Government Code Section 7922.000. 
  
The City has determined that individuals retain a substantial and reasonable expectation of 
privacy and safety in regard to their license plates. Conversely, there is little to no legitimate 
public interest in disclosing the license plates of private citizens, as such information does not 
shed light on the conduct or operations of the City. Because the public interest served by not 
disclosing this private information clearly outweighs the public interest served by disclosure, the 
license plates have been appropriately segregated and redacted.
