Anticipated exemption issues:
Gov. Code section 7923.600 — a blanket assertion that this exemption applies is inconsistent with the statute's 
requirement that it be applied on an individualized, not categorical, basis, particularly where Items 1 and 2 
establish that a given incident did not result in an investigation.
Item 1 is not equivalent to Item 2 — a CAD chronology or dispatch log, the automatically time-stamped record 
of unit assignment and status changes, is an operational dispatch record, not investigative narrative content; 
even where the Department declines to produce a narrative report under section 7923.600, that basis does not 
extend automatically to the CAD chronology of the same call.
Segregability (Gov. Code section 7922.525(b)) — any reasonably segregable, non-exempt portion of a 
withheld record must be produced.
