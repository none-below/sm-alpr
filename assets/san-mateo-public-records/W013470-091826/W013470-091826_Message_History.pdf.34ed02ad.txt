Record(s) Requested: Subject: Public Records Request — NCRIC Data Sharing Partnership audit log, 
June–November 2023
Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the following 
record from the San Mateo Police Department.
This request seeks existing records only (Gov. Code § 7922.530; Sierra Club v. Superior Court (2013) 57 
Cal.4th 157).
As you are aware, the Department's September 17, 2026 correspondence states that "[a]n audit log file 
spanning June – November 2023 contains six entries of queries by San Mateo Police Department, 
including the officer's email address, plate searched, and associated case number."
As you are further aware, SMPD's executed Memorandum of Understanding with the Northern 
California Regional Intelligence Center ("NCRIC"), dated August 2014 and produced in W012376-
032026, provides in its general provisions that "[a]n audit log will be maintained for a period of no less 
than three years to record user access to shared data, including the name and organization of the user 
accessing via the NCRIC-DSP and the date and time when the data was accessed." That same 
instrument provides, at § 6 (Information Ownership and Release), that "Member Agencies remain the 
official custodian of all information contributed to the NCRIC-DSP" and that Public Records Act 
requests for a Member Agency's data "will be referred to the Member Agency that is the owner of the 
requested data," which "will be responsible for responding to the request." This request is submitted to 
SMPD as the Member Agency and official custodian under § 6.

The audit log NCRIC's Memorandum of Understanding with SMPD requires be maintained for no less 
than three years to record user access to shared data via the NCRIC-DSP — including, without 
limitation, the audit log file described in the Department's September 17, 2026 correspondence as

status, location, or disposition of that log, to the extent the log itself is withheld or cannot be located.
Anticipated exemption issues:
Gov. Code § 7923.600 (investigation records). § 7923.600 exempts investigatory files compiled by "a state 
or local police agency." The requested audit log records access "via the NCRIC-DSP" generally, not 
solely by SMPD personnel, and on its own description above already includes at least one non-SMPD 
organization (NCRIC's own administration of the log). To the extent the log or any withheld portion of it 
reflects access by federal law enforcement personnel, by personnel of a private entity (including a 
university), or by an employee or contractor of NCRIC's vendor, those entries do not reflect an 
investigation conducted by a state or local police agency and are not covered by § 7923.600 on their face. 
Please identify any entries withheld on this basis by the organization associated with the accessing user, 
without disclosing the user's identity if the identity itself is separately exempt.
"PRA NCRIC directly" as a response basis. NCRIC MOU § 6, quoted above, requires NCRIC to refer 
Public Records Act requests for Member-Agency data to the Member Agency, which is responsible for 
responding. To the extent any portion of this request is declined on the ground that the responsive record
is held by NCRIC or should be directed to NCRIC, please identify the provision of the MOU, the statute, 
or the other authority on which that position relies.
Segregability (§ 7922.525(b)). To the extent any entries are properly withheld, please produce all 
remaining entries and fields, redacted only as necessary.
If any portion is denied, please:
1. provide specific exemption citations per Gov. Code § 7922.540;
2. identify the withheld record type and volume, including the number of entries withheld and the date 
range they cover;
3. indicate whether the denial rests on a claim that the record is held by NCRIC rather than SMPD, and 
if so, address the effect of NCRIC MOU § 6 and Gov. Code § 7920.530 on that position; and
4. if the log, or any portion of it, no longer exists or cannot be located, state when and why, and identify 
any record reflecting its disposition.
