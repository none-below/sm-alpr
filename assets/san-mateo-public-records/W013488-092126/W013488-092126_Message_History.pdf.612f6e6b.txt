Record(s) Requested: Public Records Request — determination that Flock Safety's possession and 
publication of ALPR search-audit information on the transparency portal constitutes "data hosting" 
under Civil Code § 1798.90.55(b)
Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the following 
records from the San Mateo Police Department.
This request seeks existing records only (Gov. Code § 7922.530; Sierra Club v. Superior Court (2013) 57 
Cal.4th 157).
Context
On September 21, 2026, in its determination on request W013438-091426, the Department stated: "The 
Flock Safety transparency portal is maintained by Flock Safety. The Department does not maintain a 
separate screenshot, printout, or export of the 'Prohibited Uses' section outside of the information 
available on the portal."
Civil Code § 1798.90.55(b) permits a public agency's ALPR information to reach a vendor only as a sale, 
sharing, or transfer to another public agency, or as "the provision of data hosting or towing services," 
which the statute does not consider a sale, sharing, or transfer. If the transparency portal is maintained 
by Flock Safety and not the Department, the lawful basis for Flock Safety's possession and publication of 
the Department's ALPR search-audit information there would need to be something other than hosting 
on the Department's behalf.
