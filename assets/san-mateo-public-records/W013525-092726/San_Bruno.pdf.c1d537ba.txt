ALPR Data Sharing Agreement 
 
 
_______________________________ [Agency Name] has requested access to data generated by the 
San Mateo Police Department’s Automated License Plate Recognition (ALPR) system.  
Per this agreement, ALPR data shared by the San Mateo Police Department will be accessed for 
legitimate law enforcement or public safety purposes only; and used and shared in a manner 
consistent with applicable law, including but not limited to:  
 
• 
California Civil Code § 1798.90.5 et seq., (Collection of License Plate Information)  
• 
Government Code § 7284 et seq. (California Values Act)  
 
 
___________________ [Agency Name] has a process for periodic system audits, designed to 
monitor the security of the ALPR information accessed or used, and its compliance with applicable 
law.
