Under the California Public Records Act, I request the records described below. As you are aware, the 
Department's revised Policy 463 (Automated License Plate Readers), bearing Lexipol date 2026/09/11, is

Periodic Review, Suspension or Termination, and Documentation provisions for in-state ALPR network-
sharing relationships. As you are also aware, under request W013261-081826 the Department produced

"Current relationship" below means each in-state ALPR network-sharing relationship in effect on the 
date of this request. Please respond to each item separately.
1. For each current relationship, the record of the Department's approval, including the record of the 
basis for the decision, as maintained under the Documentation provision of Policy 463.8.
2. For each current relationship, the record of the determinations made under the Approval Criteria 
provision of Policy 463.8, items (a) through (e).
3. For each current relationship, the agency's written request described in Policy 463.8(a) and the 
approved request retained on file under Policy 463.8(c).
4. The written criteria for evaluating requests for in-state ALPR network-sharing access that the ALPR 
Program Administrator is to establish and maintain under the Approval Criteria provision of Policy 
463.8.
