Dear Brian C,
 
The City received the following records request from you on September 27, 2026.
 
To the San Mateo Police Department:
Under the California Public Records Act, I request the records described below. As you are aware, the 
Department's revised Policy 463 (Automated License Plate Readers), bearing Lexipol date 2026/09/11, is now 
posted on the City's website, and its section 463.8 includes Approval Criteria, Denial Criteria, Periodic 
Review, Suspension or Termination, and Documentation provisions for in-state ALPR network-sharing 
relationships. As you are also aware, under request W013261-081826 the Department produced six executed 
ALPR Data Sharing Agreements (Belmont, Brisbane, California Highway Patrol, Foster City, Palo Alto, and 
South San Francisco).
"Current relationship" below means each in-state ALPR network-sharing relationship in effect on the date of 
this request. Please respond to each item separately.
1. For each current relationship, the record of the Department's approval, including the record of the basis for 
the decision, as maintained under the Documentation provision of Policy 463.8.
2. For each current relationship, the record of the determinations made under the Approval Criteria provision 
of Policy 463.8, items (a) through (e).
3. For each current relationship, the agency's written request described in Policy 463.8(a) and the approved 
request retained on file under Policy 463.8(c).
4. The written criteria for evaluating requests for in-state ALPR network-sharing access that the ALPR 
Program Administrator is to establish and maintain under the Approval Criteria provision of Policy 463.8.
5. Any record designating the person or position serving as the ALPR Program Administrator referenced in 
Policy 463.8.
6. Any record of a denial, periodic review, suspension, or termination of an in-state ALPR network-sharing 
relationship made on or after September 11, 2026, as maintained under the Documentation provision of Policy 
463.8.
7. Records reflecting the approval and effective date of the Policy 463 revision bearing Lexipol date 
2026/09/11, for example an approval by the Chief of Police, a Lexipol publication or acknowledgment record, 
or a department-wide transmittal.
