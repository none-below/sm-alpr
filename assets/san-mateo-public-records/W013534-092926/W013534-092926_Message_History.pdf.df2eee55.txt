Dear Brian C,
 
The City received the following records request from you on September 29, 2026.
 
Under the California Public Records Act, I request records concerning a single Flock Safety ALPR search 
conducted under the San Mateo Police Department's Flock account: search ID e3ff26d0-8f26-43d6-8e73-
7c70b723b665, conducted 04/09/2024 at 07:44:34 PM UTC, 428 networks searched.
As you are aware, each search under the Department's Flock account is recorded with a search ID, a time, a 
network count, and a reason entered by the user.
Please provide the following:
1. The Flock Audit PDF report record for this search, including every field of that record, among them the 
reason text as entered by the user.
2. The organization-audit spreadsheet/CSV export record for this search, in its native electronic format (.csv or 
.xlsx) (Gov. Code § 7922.570), including every field of that record, among them the reason text as entered by 
the user, the Case # field, the Time Frame, and the Filters.
3. Any CAD incident, RMS entry, incident report, or case report that the Department associates with this 
search, or that bears a case, incident, or file number entered for this search.
4. Any record reflecting a request for this search from any person or agency, or an approval of it, including 
any email, text message, chat or messaging-app message, dispatch or assist record, supervisor approval, or 
task-force record held by the City.
5. Any record reflecting the provision of this search's results (including plate reads, images, locations, or 
reports) to any person or entity outside the Department, including any email, text message, or chat or 
messaging-app message, and any Flock share, export, or download of the results (including the corresponding 
Flock event-log entries). This includes messages sent or received by the user recorded for this search in the 
Flock audit record, on City accounts or devices and on any personal account or device used to conduct City 
business (City of San Jose v. Superior Court (2017) 2 Cal.5th 608).
For items 4 and 5, I'd ask that the search of email and messaging accounts use as search terms the search ID 
above and each case, incident, or file number entered for this search in its Flock audit record (in the reason 
text or the Case # field), for the period April 1, 2024 through the date of this request.
If any portion of these records is withheld, I'd ask that it be marked in place, field by field, with the exemption
