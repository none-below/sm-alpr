Axon Outpost and 
Axon Lightpost Bundles
Axon Outpost and Axon Lightpost represent the next leap in fixed camera 
technology, offering ALPR and livestreaming in one device, plus seamless 
integration into Axon Fusus.
Hardware
Axon Outpost is an Axon-built fixed camera that combines ALPR, vehicle attribute recognition and livestreaming 
in a single unit. It offers flexible deployment and is able to be placed on new or existing poles, buildings, trailers, 
vehicles, or trees. The camera captures plates at speeds of up to 75 mph at up to 75 feet of distance.
Axon Lightpost, built in partnership with Ubicquia, also provides ALPR, vehicle attribute recognition and 
livestreaming. Its unique form factor enables it to be deployed on existing Ubicquia streetlights in under an 
hour. It captures plate reads at distances greater than 75 feet.
Bundles include field-ready hardware components for scalable ALPR deployments:
•	 Outpost or Lightpost camera units and mounting accessories, including poles when relevant
•	 Power Accessories:
•	 Outpost: Solar or 110V 
•	 Lightpost: NEMA 7-pin Photocell (up to 130V) via included UbiHub device

Software
Both product bundles provide access to Axon Fusus*, our real-time operations 
platform, and include a full LTE subscription for real-time livestreaming, ALPR data 
upload, and video recall upload. The vehicle search capabilities in Fusus provide:
•	 Unified search across any ALPR-enabled camera by plate, partials, make, model, 
color, or unique attributes
•	 Visualization of vehicle movement history to support interception or investigation
•	 Near-instant alerts from any ALPR camera — fixed, mobile, or partner
•	 Cross-agency sharing when and where you choose
Axon Fusus also enables you to immediately get vehicle intelligence from any 
third-party cameras you already own that are certified Works With Axon partners 
as well as pull vehicle attribute information from other integrated CCTV cameras.
Deployment
In order to ensure success, Axon bundles include assessment and installation 
services. We will:
•	 Evaluate and recommend installation sites by assessing power/network 
availability and traffic flow
•	 Obtain Attachment Agreements for Axon Lightpost
•	 Organize permitting for Axon Outpost, including:
•	 Identifying required permits and supplying drawings and specs
•	 Coordinating with local/state agencies
•	 Tracking and supporting permit progress
•	 Install and activate each unit with full documentation
