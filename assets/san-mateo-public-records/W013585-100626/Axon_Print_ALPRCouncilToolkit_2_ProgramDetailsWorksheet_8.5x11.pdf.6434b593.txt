Use your completed Section 2 to fill in every highlighted field below. Regular text is written to be used as is; your

These templates are written in first person for verbal testimony. For written materials, substitute the agency’s 
name for “we” and “our.”
What does the system collect?
Our program captures vehicle detections at specific camera locations, including a license plate image, 
a vehicle context image, basic vehicle attributes such as color, make and vehicle type, and the time and 
location of each detection. The context image provides visual context for review. Because it is an image 
from the scene, it may incidentally include surroundings, signage or people in view. It is not analyzed for 
facial recognition or behavioral profiling. The system does not perform live registered-owner lookups or 
build a profile of a registered owner. Our deployment includes [Fleet 3 / Axon Outpost / Axon Lightpost / 
Works With Axon partner cameras] and covers [public-safe description of deployment scope or  
coverage area].
Can this be used to track people over time?
The system does not monitor people or maintain a live location feed. Authorized users can review prior 
detections of a specific plate within [agency name]’s retention period of [number of hours or days] when 
there is an authorized investigative purpose. That search requires [case number / stated reason / agency-
approved justification], is logged with the user, action and time and, where configured, stated reason, and is 
reviewed by our [audit owner name and title] on a [monthly / quarterly / other] cadence.

How long is data kept?
ALPR detection data is retained for [number of hours or days] under [agency name]’s configured retention 
policy and removed automatically when that period expires. Detections preserved as evidence follow our 
evidence retention rules, which require [brief description of your evidence retention standard]. Retention 
periods are documented in our written use policy and configured in the system.
Who can access the data?
Access is limited to [authorized roles: for example, patrol officers, investigators, supervisors] who have been 
designated in our access configuration. [Agency name] defines and manages those roles. Every search is 
logged with the user, the action and the time, and our [audit owner name and title] reviews access activity on a 
[monthly / quarterly / other] cadence.

What happens if a federal agency requests access to the data?
Through Axon, there is no automatic, standing or built-in federal access to our ALPR data. Because we own 
and control the data, a lawful request goes to us and our counsel to handle. If Axon receives such a request, 
Axon directs it to us as the data owner and discloses data only where legally required to do so. If a request 
goes directly to our agency, we respond under [state law citation / local ordinance / our written policy], 
which [brief description of how your agency handles such requests]. Our process for lawful requests is 
documented in [policy document name].
What record shows the program is operating within policy?
Our audit owner, [name and title], reviews access activity and ALPR searches on a [monthly / quarterly / 
other] cadence. That review is documented and retained. We can produce a summary of our audit review 
process, our review cadence and, where legally appropriate, a redacted summary of findings. The system 
logs every ALPR search and relevant system action with user, action, time and, where configured, 
stated reason.
