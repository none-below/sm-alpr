Council Readiness 
Checklist
This checklist helps you confirm your governance decisions are made, your preparation is complete and the 
documents behind them are gathered. It is a final readiness check, not a maturity rating, and it does not cover 
every governance choice.
Broader policy choices belong in the agency’s written policy and legal review process. If any item is unresolved, 
settle it before a council hearing, briefing or community discussion.
Done
Step
□
Applicable state and local law reviewed, including any state automated license plate recognition 
(ALPR) statute, and compliance position confirmed
□
Surveillance use policy or local technology approval process completed, if required
□
Authorized uses defined: what the system may and may not be used for
□
Offense category configuration completed, reflecting authorized uses
□
ALPR detection data retention periods configured for system enforcement, with evidence retention 
rules confirmed for preserved detections
□
Sharing configuration decided (off or specific approved relationships) and who can enable 
or change it
Governance Decisions
