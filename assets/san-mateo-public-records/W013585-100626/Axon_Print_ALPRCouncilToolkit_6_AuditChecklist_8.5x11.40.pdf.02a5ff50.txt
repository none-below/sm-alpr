Audit & 
Accountability 
Checklist
Approval is the start of accountability, not the end. This checklist helps you keep your program operating the 
way you told your council it would, and gives you a record to show it. The readiness checklist helps you assign the 
decisions. This one helps you review whether practice matches those decisions over time.
Use your audit logs and audit reporting tools to review access, searches, sharing activity where enabled and 
configuration changes. Suggested frequencies are included with each item as a starting point. Adjust them to your 
agency’s policy requirements, applicable law and operational capacity. Document what you review so the record 
exists when you need it. A review trigger is a pattern worth examining, not evidence of misuse. Many will have a 
legitimate, documented purpose. The point is to review it, document the outcome and escalate when needed.

Escalate review when any of the following appear in the audit record.
•	
A search has no documented case or purpose and the user cannot account for it
•	
Repeated access to a plate or record falls outside any active investigation
•	
Sharing or access is found that was never authorized
•	
Permissions do not match a user’s current role
•	
A hotlist entry came from an unapproved source or skipped required approval
•	
An export or disclosure occurred outside policy
•	
Support access cannot be tied to an agency-initiated request
•	
Public-facing information is inaccurate, outdated or includes sensitive information
•	
Community feedback identifies possible misuse, a mistaken alert or an access issue
A U D I T  &  A C C O U N TA B I L I T Y  C H E C K L I S T  –  C O N T I N U E D
Do not publish raw ALPR reads, plate numbers, plate images, personal or driver information, officer 
names, user-level audit logs, case numbers, hotlist contents, exact camera locations or active 
investigation details. Redact before any external sharing.
What you can share publicly
•	
A link to your written use policy
•	
Your retention policy and retention periods
•	
Aggregate ALPR usage information, such as retained read and hit counts or active device 
counts where available
•	
A summary of your audit review process
•	
A corrective-action summary where appropriate and legally permissible
•	
Redacted audit exports where legally appropriate and reviewed under agency policy and 
applicable law
