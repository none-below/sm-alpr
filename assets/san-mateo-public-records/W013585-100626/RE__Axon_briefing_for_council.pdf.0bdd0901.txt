Hi Martin,
 
The Police Department is exploring additional technology solutions available through Axon that could support our public safety
operations, and Axon has offered to provide Council members with an informational briefing and demonstration of those
capabilities.
 
The primary focus will be on Axon’s ALPR technology, including its Outpost fixed license plate reader and video solution. Axon
also intends to provide an overview of some of its other public safety technology, including Dedrone airspace security, as well as
information regarding data security and privacy, ethical oversight, and potential contract scope and financial considerations.
 
At this point, the purpose is educational and intended to give Council members an opportunity to better understand the
technology, ask questions directly of Axon representatives, and learn about the operational, privacy, policy, and financial
considerations associated with these systems. This is not intended to represent a commitment by the City to purchase or
implement any particular product.
 
Axon has proposed several identical small-group sessions to accommodate Council members’ schedules and allow for a more
conversational presentation and Q&A.
 
Anthony can coordinate with you on potential dates and times once we have a sense of Council availability.
