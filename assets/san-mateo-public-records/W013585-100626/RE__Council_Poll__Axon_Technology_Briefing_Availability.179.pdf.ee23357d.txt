Subject: Council Poll: Axon Technology Briefing Availability
Importance: High
 
Good afternoon, City Council (Bcc’d),
The Police Department is exploring additional technology solutions available through Axon that could support the City’s public
safety operations. As part of that exploration, Axon has offered to provide Council members with an IN PERSON informational
briefing and demonstration of its technology.
The briefing will focus primarily on Axon’s Automated License Plate Recognition (ALPR) technology, including its Outpost
fixed license plate reader and video solution. Axon will also provide an overview of other public safety technology, including
Dedrone airspace security, as well as information regarding data security and privacy, ethical oversight, potential contract scope,
and financial considerations.
The purpose of this briefing is informational only. It is intended to provide Council members with an opportunity to learn
more about the technology and ask questions directly of Axon representatives. Participation does not represent a commitment by
the City to purchase or implement any particular product.
To accommodate Council schedules and allow for a more conversational presentation and Q&A, Axon has proposed several
identical one-hour small-group sessions (maximum of two Council Members per session).
Please reply to me at your earliest opportunity and indicate ALL of the time slots below that you would be available to
attend. We will use the responses to coordinate the sessions.

2:00–3:00 p.m.

4:00–5:00 p.m.
