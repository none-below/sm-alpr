Data Privacy and Security Considerations
Recently, there has been public discussion and concern regarding the potential sharing or accessibility of ALPR information
across jurisdictions. The San Mateo Police Department is aware of information contained in an audit that references six
searches associated with Department personnel. Based on the information currently available to us, the audit does not

agencies, or the public disclosure of San Mateo ALPR data.
 
The Department continues to review the available information to ensure the circumstances are accurately understood. SMPD
will continue to manage access to and use of ALPR information in accordance with applicable law, Department policy, and
established data-sharing restrictions.
