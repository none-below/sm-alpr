I am helping facilitate getting the Axon ALPR purchase in front of the San Mateo City Council for consideration on October
5th.
I've attached a proposed draft of Amendment No. 1 to our Master Services and Purchasing Agreement.
This has been reviewed and approved by our City Attorney.
 
This amendment seeks to:
1.               Incorporate the new quote for purchase of Axon ALPRs into the MSPA
2.               Clarify that the termination language in the MSPA does not apply to ALPRs (as those will have separate
terms that are stated in the quote)
3.               Add language as recommended in the recent San Mateo County Grand Jury Report to require compliance
with applicable California law prohibiting vendors from disclosing ALPR data to unauthorized third parties; and to
set forth requirements for disclosure in the event of a security breach. 
4.               Add language indicating Contractor shall maintain compliance with applicable Criminal Justice Information
Services (CJIS) Security Policy requirements. 
 
Can you please review this with your legal team and confirm whether Axon will be amendable to signing as presented?
Please let me know if there are any questions or concerns.

date? The current quote expires 9/29/26.
