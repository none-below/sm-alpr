HOW WE'VE STRUCTURED THE DAY
Three identical two-hour educational sessions on the same day — morning, midday, and afternoon. Smaller
groups create better conversations, and splitting Council members across sessions makes it easier for
everyone to attend given competing schedules. 
Under California's Brown Act, a "meeting" is triggered when a quorum — a majority — of the body gathers to
discuss city business. Richmond's seven-member Council (Mayor + six Councilmembers) has a quorum of four,
so keeping each session at two to three members stays safely below that threshold. 
Each session covers the same five topics in the same order. The only difference is the audience. Council
members attend one of the three Council sessions. Command Staff gets a separate session.
