1, PURPOSE.

This Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is intended to set forth the procedures,
responsibilities, and obligations of the California National Guard (NGCA) Counterdrug Task
Force (CDTF) and the San Mateo Police Department whereby the CDTF may provide requested
support to San Mateo Police Department counterdrug (CD) activities.

3. AUTHORITY.

a. 32 U.S.C. § 112 authorizes the Secretary of Defense (SecDef) to approve and provide
funds for the Governor of Caiifornia to utilize NGCA personnel not in federal service in support
of federal, state, tribal, and local law enforcement agencies (LEA) and community-based
organizations (CBO) in accordance with (IAW) CNGBM 3100.01 and the annual State Drug
Interdiction and Counterdrug Activities Support Plan (State CD Support Plan). The Governor of
California is the Commander-in-Chief of the NGCA, and may order it to execute the laws of the
State IAW Cal. Const. art. V, §§ 1, 7, and Cal. Mil. & Vet. Code § 140.

b. 32 U.S.C. § 112 requires Department of Defense (DoD) consultation with the Office of
National Drug Control Policy (ONDCP), prior to State CD Support Plan approval, for
coordination of drug-control activities and related funding [AW the President's efforts to reduce
illicit drug-related crime and violence as outlined in the annual National Drug Control Strategy.

c. The San Mateo Police Department is responsible for the investigation and suppression of
illegal drug activities in San Mateo County. The San Mateo Police Department targets violent
crime associated with gang activity, high risk crimes, and the use of illegal drugs through
collaborative involvement of the region’s police departments.

4, PLANNED DEPLOYMENT OF NATIONAL GUARD PERSONNEL AND/OR EQUIPMENT.

a. The role of the NGCA CDTF in CD operations is to support LEAs. All NGCA CDTF
operational support to LEAs and CBOs conducting drug interdiction and CD activities will be
initiated upon approval of a written request from the San Mateo Police Department and will be
performed in IAW the SecDef, Governor, and Attorney General approved mission categories
included in the State CD Support Plan. In order to ensure adequate mission planning and
execution, San Mateo Police Department will provide specific objectives for each requested
mission and designate a San Mateo Police Department point of contact.

b. San Mateo Police Department will be responsible for the physical security and safeguarding
of all CDTF arms and ammunition stored in their armory. San Mateo Police Department offers
storage and constant surveillance in a manned and secured armory for arms, ammunition, and
equipment. The manned San Mateo Police Department armory provides security-enhancing
systems and physical safeguards required by AR 190-11. CDTF Service Members will sign-in with
on-duty San Mateo Police Department personnel at the San Mateo Police Department upon
entrance to armory. The Watch Commander/Supervisor or designated personnel will escort the
authorized Service Members to draw necessary equipment.

5. COMMAND AND CONTROL.

a. The Governor of California has appointed The Adjutant General (TAG), per Military &
Veterans Code § 160, to exercise command and control of all NGCA personnel. TAG has
designated the Counterdrug Coordinator (CDC) to provide direction and oversight of NGCA
personnel conducting drug interdiction and CD activities. NGCA CDTF personnel will remain
under military command and control at all times. TAG and CDC retain the right to recall any and
all CDTF personnel or equipment from CD support to the San Mateo Police Department at any
time.

b. CDTF Pilots In Command (PICs) and Aircraft Commanders (A/Cs) are responsible for all
decisions affecting military aircraft, aircrew and passenger safety, and have authority to override
an order for any personnel to chamber rounds or fire a weapon while on board NBCA aircraft.

6. SCOPE OF APPROVED OPERATIONS.

a. Technical Support. (Linguist; Investigative Case Analyst; Paralegal; Communications;
Engineer; Subsurface/Subterrain; Counter Threat Finance Analyst; and Imagery/Map Support)

b. General Support. (Domestic illegal Drug Suppression Operations; Transportation)

c. Counterdrug Training. (Military-Specific Technical and Tactical Training)

d. Ground Reconnaissance/Observation. (Land and Maritime Observation and Sensor
Support)

e. Aerial Reconnaissance/Observation. (Rotary Wing, Fixed Wing, and Unmanned Aircraft
Systems)

f. Drug Demand Reduction Operations and Coalition Development. (Anti-Drug Coalition
Support)

a. The Governor of California authorized the NGCA CDTF to conduct armed operations in
support of LEAs for force protection. TAG has delegated the authority to arm CDTF personnel to
the CDC. NGCA CDTF Rules for Use of Force (RUF), certified [AW state law by the Attorney
General of California, will be outlined in detail with the San Mateo Police Department during the
planning phase of each mission per applicable policy, regulation, and law. CDTF personnel will
not, except for in exigent circumstances, directly participate in the arrest or search of U.S.
persons or become involved in the chain of custody for any evidence seized by LEAs. Exigent
circumstances inciude situations in which immediate action is necessary to: protect LEA
officers, CDTF personnel, or other civilian bystanders from death or great bodily injury; prevent
the destruction of evidence; or to prevent the escape of a suspect in LEA custody.

b. The San Mateo Police Department shall be responsible for ensuring work site security and
compliance with all applicable federal health, safety, and hazardous material regulations. CDOTF
personnel will wear the authorized military uniform or civilian attire at, and when traveling
to/from their assigned duty location as a risk mitigation measure. Further, CDTF personnel will
avoid unnecessary confrontation with civilians and foreign nationals encountered in the area of
operations.

c. LEAs reserve the right to perform additional criminal records checks, and/or security
screening of applicants in LEA offices or in positions with access to LEA information. Additional
screening includes LEA conducted criminal history queries covering SM driving record, criminal
record, and security clearance. CDTF personnel are not authorized to participate in polygraph or
lie detector-type tests without the CDC’s approval. LEAs may request additional screening for
CDTF personnel. Additional screening requests must be submitted in writing by the law
enforcement head of agency to the Counterdrug Commander (CDC), or designee, for approval.
Applicants will be informed that such inquiries are likely to be completed after entry on duty and
that rejection by LEAs may result in their involuntary early release from support of the CDTF.
San Mateo Police Department may require CDTF personnel to agree to additional San Mateo
Police Department physical and information security requirements.

8. PUBLIC AFFAIRS.

The San Mateo Police Department will be the lead agency for all public affairs (PA) and media
relations. PA and media concerns for CDTF support will be coordinated with the CDC and CD
PAO for interagency risk analysis prior releasing any information to the media. The CDTF will
not disclose information related to San Mateo Police Department without explicit guidance or
direction of the San Mateo Police Department (or their designated representative) and the San
Mateo Police Department of Public Affairs and will ensure that PA efforts do not jeopardize the
identity, safety, or security of CDTF or San Mateo Police Department personne! and equipment.

9. SPECIAL INSTRUCTIONS, REQUIREMENTS, AND/OR RESTRICTIONS.

a. CDTF personnel shail remain in a support role to the San Mateo Police Department. The
San Mateo Police Department will be solely responsible for determining the legal basis for
searches and observational support. This includes, but is not limited to, determining the legal
requirements for the use of thermal imaging or sense-enhancing systems and obtaining
necessary search warrants. CDTF personnel will not conduct cargo/mail inspection.

b. All CDTF criminal analyst/linguist activities authorized by applicable law, regulation, and

policy will be strictly limited to support of LEA drug interdiction and CD activities. CDTF
personnel shall not independently collect, retain, or disseminate information on United States

persons. The criminal information under analysis is the property of San Mateo Police
Department and will not be retained in NGCA facilities or databases, nor disseminated. CDTF

Mateo Police Department rules and regulations for information and operational security. CDTF

c. CDTF personnel will neither be deputized nor cross-designated with expanded law

d. The CDTF is required to provide timely reporting of drug interdiction and CD support
activities/statistics per applicable regulation, policy, and law. When necessary, and to the extent
practicable, San Mateo Police Department will provide the CDTF with illicit drug detection,
deterrence, disruption, and curtailment statistics (i.e. seizures, arrests, convictions) directly
attributed to CDTF support. Law enforcement and case sensitive information will not be included
in these reports.

e. The CDTF will investigate workplace/mission accidents or injuries invoiving CDTF
personnel and/or equipment. The San Mateo Police Department will provide a liaison to the
military investigation team, as required. CDTF personnel will not participate in San Mateo Police
Department internal investigations without the CDC’s express consent.

10. LIABILITY

a. It is agreed that the officers, agents, employees and/or volunteers of the San Mateo Police
Department shall not be deemed employees of the NGCA CDTF for workers’ compensation,
other insurance coverage, or for any other purpose. Likewise, the officers, agents, and
employees of the NGCA CDTF shall not be deemed employees of the San Mateo Police
Department for worker's compensation, other insurance coverage or for any purpose. Each
party will be solely responsible for processing any claims arising out of property damage or
injuries to, or death of its own employees and agents that occur as a result of the officer, agent
or employee's action(s) or omission(s) while that person is in the performance of his/her duty in
accordance with applicable state and federai law.

b. CDTF personnel in a 32 U.S.C. § 502(f) duty status under the command and control of the
NGCA and acting within the scope of their approved duties are protected from civil liability in
their individual capacity by the Federal Tort Claims Act (FTCA) under 28 U.S.C. §§ 2671-2680;
and are protected from state civil and criminal liability under Cal. Mil. & Vet. Code § 392.

11. ASSET FORFEITURE AND EQUITABLE SHARING.

The CDTF is considered a law enforcement agency solely for purposes of asset forfeiture
equitable sharing. It is the intent of all parties to the agreement that assets seized by San Mateo
Police Department as a result of operations or investigations supported by the CDTF will be
equitably shared if forfeited administratively or judicially pursuant to State and Federal law. It is
understood decisions made with respect to the equitable sharing of property administratively
forfeited by the San Mateo Police Department is at the discretion of the Riverside County
Sheriff's Office. The disposition of assets forfeited judicially is within the discretionary authority

12. RENEGOTIATION AND TERMINATION OF AGREEMENT.

Any and ail modifications to this MOU must be mutually agreed upon by all parties in writing.
This MOU will remain in effect while changes or amendments are being coordinated. This MOU
is effective immediately upon signature of all parties and will remain in effect until terminated by
either party in writing. This MOU will be reviewed annually by the CDTF to ensure
appropriateness and adequacy for support provided.

13. NO PRIVATE RIGHT CREATED.

This MOU does not create any right or benefit, substantial or procedural, enforceable at law or
equity, by a party against the United States, its departments, agencies, or other entities, its
employees, its officers, or any other person.
