Record(s) Requested: Subject: Public Records Act request — ALPR platform user accounts, account 
history, and training records (Flock Safety and Axon)
Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the following 
records from the San Mateo Police Department. This request seeks existing records only (Gov. Code § 
7922.530; Sierra Club v. Superior Court (2013) 57 Cal.4th 157).
As you are aware, Policy 463 (Automated License Plate Readers, Lexipol 2026/09/11), SOP 205 
(Automated License Plate Readers Operating Procedure, reissued September 21, 2026) and Policy 821 
(CJIS Access, Maintenance, and Security) govern who may hold an account on the Department's ALPR 
platforms, the training required before access, and how accounts are approved, reviewed and disabled. 
SOP 205.1 identifies those platforms as Flock Safety and Axon Fleet 3.
As you are also aware, in response to W012672-050426 the Department produced a LEFTA "Training 
Records Sign-In Sheet" for "Flock Training (Initial)" listing 151 names, and a POST course roster 
(POST 2-111) for a February 10–11, 2020 Flock training course. The LEFTA sheet is undated, and the 
completion checkbox beside every name is blank. On the February 2020 roster, the "Successfully 
Completed Course" column, the hours-attended column, the coordinator signature and the date are 
blank for every trainee; only a separate April 9, 2021 roster page marks three trainees as having 
completed the course. Those records show who was listed for training, not who completed it. They need 
not be produced again.
Scope. "ALPR platform" means Flock Safety; Axon, including Fleet 3 and any Axon application used to 
search, view or administer ALPR data; and any other ALPR platform on which the Department or its 
personnel have held user accounts since October 25, 2019, including Vigilant Solutions and the NCRIC

AstroMetrics platform. "Account" means any user account on a Department organization or tenant on 
an ALPR platform, and any account through which a person accessed ALPR data under the 
Department's authority, whether or not the account holder is a Department employee. The period is 
October 25, 2019 through the date of this request. Please respond to each item separately.
Several items below ask for records that Department policy or state law says shall exist, and each of 
those items quotes the requirement. For those items, a response of "no responsive records" would mean 
that the required record does not exist, and may indicate that the Department is not complying with that 
policy or law.
This request is in two parts. Part A (items 1–6) concerns the accounts on the Department's ALPR 
platforms and how they are managed; Part B (items 7–10) concerns per-person records for those 
account holders. Part B may require more compilation, so I ask that it not delay the determination or 
production for Part A. If the Department would prefer to log Part B as a separate request, that is fine.
Part A — Accounts on the Department's ALPR platforms and how they are managed
1. For each ALPR platform, the list of accounts as maintained by the platform or the Department (for 
example, the administrator user-list export from the Department's Flock Safety organization and the 
Axon equivalent). For each account it should show the holder's name, login or user identifier, agency or 
employer, role or permission level, creation date, and deactivation date if any. This includes accounts 
that have since been deactivated or deleted, to the extent a record of them exists, and accounts created 
before October 25, 2019 that remained active after that date. Requirement: Policy 463.6(a) provides that 
ALPR data "shall be accessible only through a login/password-protected system capable of documenting 
all access of information by name, date and time (Civil Code § 1798.90.52)." Civil Code § 
1798.90.52(a)(3) provides that the record of each access shall include "[t]he username of the person who 
accesses the information, and, as applicable, the organization or entity with whom the person is 
affiliated." A "no responsive records" response to this item would mean the Department cannot identify 
who holds accounts on its ALPR platforms.
2. Platform event-log or administrative-log entries recording account creation or invitation, changes to 
an account's role, permissions, name or profile, and account deactivation or deletion, for every account 
in item 1. For each event, the record showing its date and time, the account that performed it, the 
account affected, and the permissions before and after. The Flock "EVENTLOG" produced under 
W012570-041926 is an example of the record format for Flock; for Axon, the equivalent audit-trail 
records are sought. Because the Department has already produced this type of record from its Flock 
organization, a "no responsive records" response to this item would conflict with that earlier production.
3. Any record designating, authorizing or approving an "authorized designee," or any other person who 
is not a Department employee, to operate an ALPR system or access ALPR data under the Department's 
authority. This includes task-force agreements or memoranda of understanding, contractor or vendor 
agreements, and approval communications. Requirement: Policy 463.4(a) and 463.4(d) permit ALPR 
access only by a "member" or "authorized designee," and Policy 463.2 provides that "ALPR data 
retention and access, shall be managed by the ALPR Committee Captain or their designee." If every 
account holder has been a Department employee, please say so. Otherwise, a "no responsive records" 
response to this item would mean that people outside the Department were given access with no record

authorizing it.
4. The description of the job title or other designation of the employees and independent contractors 
authorized to use or access each ALPR platform, and any approved-roles list, eligibility memorandum or 
other criteria document used to decide who may hold an ALPR platform account. Requirement: Civil 
Code § 1798.90.51(b)(2)(B) provides that the usage and privacy policy shall include "[a] description of 
the job title or other designation of the employees and independent contractors who are authorized to 
use or access the ALPR system, or to collect ALPR information," and that "[t]he policy shall identify the 
training requirements necessary for those authorized employees and independent contractors." Civil 
Code § 1798.90.53(b)(2)(B) says the same for end-users, and the text of both sections appears in the 
Department's own training materials produced under W012672-050426. Policy 463 as posted describes 
authorized users as "Department member(s) or authorized designee whose job duties necessitate the use 
of ALPR" (463.4(a)) and lists no job titles or designations. If the Department regards that language as its 
description, please say so; if a description exists elsewhere, please produce it. A "no responsive records" 
response to this item would mean the Department's ALPR policy does not contain a description that 
state law says it shall include.
5. Any record showing whether accounts on each ALPR platform are created, reviewed, modified or 
disabled under Policy 821 (CJIS Access, Maintenance, and Security), including the annual account 
reviews for those accounts and any record of an account disabled under Policy 821.6. Requirement: 
Policy 821.3.1(b)(3) assigns the CJIS coordinator "[r]eviewing member accounts for compliance with 
legal and policy requirements at least annually." A "no responsive records" response to this item would 
mean either that ALPR platform accounts are not managed under Policy 821 or that the annual reviews 
it requires were not done.
6. Records showing how users authenticate to each ALPR platform (for example, single sign-on 
configuration or vendor setup documentation linking the platform to the City's user directory), sufficient 
to show whether disabling a member's City account also disables that member's platform access.
Part B — Per-person records for those account holders
7. For each account holder in item 1, the record showing that the holder completed the department-
approved ALPR training before the account was issued (for example, a completed and signed course 
roster, a certificate of completion, a Training Division or POST/METR completion entry, or an ALPR 
Administrator record). This item is not limited to people absent from the records produced under 
W012672-050426, because those records do not show completion. Requirement: Policy 463.4(d) provides 
that "[n]o member of this department or authorized designee shall operate ALPR equipment or access 
ALPR data without first completing department-approved training." SOP 205.4 provides that "[p]rior 
to accessing the ALPR platforms to review data as end-users, any person doing so shall complete 
Department approved training," and SOP 205.3 provides that "[r]ecords of this training shall be 
maintained by our Training Division." For any account holder for whom no completion record is 
produced, a "no responsive records" response would mean the Department has no record that the 
person completed the training Department policy requires before accessing ALPR data.
8. The records by which access to each ALPR platform was assigned or approved for each account 
holder, including any record of the determination that the holder's job duties necessitate ALPR use.

Requirement: Policy 463.4(a) limits ALPR use to a member or authorized designee "whose job duties 
necessitate the use of ALPR"; Policy 463.2 places ALPR data access under the ALPR Committee 
Captain or their designee; and Policy 821.6 provides that "Department accounts used to access CJI shall 
only be created upon approval of the Chief of Police or the authorized designee." A "no responsive 
records" response to this item would mean the Department has no record of who approved each person's 
access or on what basis.
9. For each account holder in item 1, records showing the start and end dates of the holder's employment 
with or assignment to the Department. Existing records from which the dates can be read (for example, 
assignment-history, roster or payroll-period records) are sufficient.
10. Any record identifying or addressing an account that remained active or was used after the holder's 
employment or assignment ended, or by a holder who had not completed the required training. This 
includes any audit memorandum or corrective communication. Requirement: Policy 821.6 provides that 
member accounts "shall be disabled within one week" when "[t]he account is no longer associated with a 
member" or "[t]he account has been inactive for 90 calendar days." A "no responsive records" response 
to this item would mean the Department has no record of finding or addressing any such account, which 
can be checked against items 1, 2 and 9.
If the Department withholds the name of any account holder, I ask that the account's remaining fields 
and its records under items 2, 7, 8 and 9 be produced with the name redacted, and that the exemption 
applied be identified (Gov. Code § 7922.000).
Where a responsive record contains exempt content, please produce the reasonably segregable non-
exempt portions (Gov. Code § 7922.525(b)). The records sought are access-control, training and 
employment-date records; the California Supreme Court has held that peace officers' names, employing 
agencies and hire and termination dates are not confidential personnel records (Commission on Peace 
Officer Standards & Training v. Superior Court (2007) 42 Cal.4th 278). If the Department withholds any 
of them under Penal Code § 832.7, Gov. Code § 7923.600 or Civil Code § 1798.90.55(b), please identify 
which records and the basis for each. Records held by Flock Safety or Axon on the Department's behalf 
are sought to the extent they are Department records (Gov. Code § 7920.530; City of San Jose v. 
Superior Court (2017) 2 Cal.5th 608). Where a record exists in electronic form, please provide it in its 
native format.
If any item yields no records, please say so for that item and continue with the rest. If the volume is 
large, I'm glad to discuss narrowing the scope.
