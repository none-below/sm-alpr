This letter responds to your California Public Records Act (“CPRA”) request dated October 9, 
2026, submitted under Reference #W013609-100926, in which you requested the following 
records: 
 
1. The Flock audit record of every search conducted under the Department's Flock account 
in which R3-23-0055 appears in the reason or Case # field, in any capitalization or spacing, 
including every field of each record: search ID, date and time, user, networks searched, 
time frame, license plate, reason, Case #, filters, and search type. I'd ask for these in the 
electronic format in which the Department holds them, such as the Flock organization-
audit .csv or .xlsx export (Gov. Code § 7922.570). Civil Code § 1798.90.52(a) provides that 
an ALPR operator that accesses ALPR information shall "Maintain a record of that access. 
At a minimum, the record shall include all of the following: (1) The date and time the 
information is accessed. ... (4) The purpose for accessing the information." The Department 
produced one such record under W013534-092926, so a "no responsive records" response 
to this item would conflict with that production. 
 
Department Response: 
The platform does not support searches of the data using the specific categories identified in your 
request-case number or reason field. To the extent that responsive queries exist, they will be 
included in the ongoing rolling production of audit logs provided to you under request 
#W012541-041426. 
 
 
2. All other Department records that contain or refer to case number R3-23-0055, 
including: any CAD incident, RMS entry, incident report, case report, or case-management
