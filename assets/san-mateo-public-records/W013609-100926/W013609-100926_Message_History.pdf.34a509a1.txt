Please provide the following, for January 1, 2023 through the date of this request:
1. The Flock audit record of every search conducted under the Department's Flock account in which R3-
23-0055 appears in the reason or Case # field, in any capitalization or spacing, including every field of 
each record: search ID, date and time, user, networks searched, time frame, license plate, reason, Case #,

1798.90.52(a) provides that an ALPR operator that accesses ALPR information shall "Maintain a record 
of that access. At a minimum, the record shall include all of the following: (1) The date and time the 
information is accessed. ... (4) The purpose for accessing the information." The Department produced 
one such record under W013534-092926, so a "no responsive records" response to this item would 
conflict with that production.
2. All other Department records that contain or refer to case number R3-23-0055, including: any CAD 
incident, RMS entry, incident report, case report, or case-management record; any record of a request 
for, approval of, or assignment of a search or other work under that number, from any person or 
agency; any record of ALPR results (plate reads, images, locations, or reports) provided to any person or
