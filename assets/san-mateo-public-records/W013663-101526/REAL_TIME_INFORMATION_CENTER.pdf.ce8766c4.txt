ELEMENTS OF AN RTIC
Property crime is one of the most prevalent
issues impacting communities within the bay
area. Stolen vehicles often go hand in hand
with package theft, retail theft, and robberies. 
Integrating Department drones and public
cameras allows the real time information center to
get eyes on an active scene within seconds of the
call for service - preserving resources and preparing
officers for unfolding incidents. 
The real time operators are well versed in various
intelligence databases and tactics. Timely
intelligence allows for officers to identify safety
risks and respond more prepared.

How Does a Flock Search Work?
1. Offense Type
Select the offense type that best matches
the investigation.
2. Case Number
Enter the case number to connect the search to the
related investigation. 
3. Reason for Search
Briefly explain the legitimate law enforcement
purpose for accessing ALPR data. 
Why do these fields matter?
Required fields create a clear, reviewable audit trail.
The audit trail records who conducted the search,
what was searched, and the documented purpose.
This supports accountability, transparency, and
compliance with department policy.

Flock cameras automatically compare detected

enforcement investigations

Flock can receive Hotlist information originating
from the National Crime Information Center (NCIC).

Protecting Privacy
Protecting community privacy is part of how we operate the
DFR program.​
During a flight, our pilots keep the camera pointed toward the
horizon to reduce incidental recording of people and private
property along the flight path. Upon arrival, the camera
focuses on the incident and the information needed to assist
first responders. ​The process is repeated for return flights.
We record every DFR flight from start to finish. That recording
preserves a record of the operation that can be reviewed to
evaluate how the drone and camera were used.​
The data is automatically uploaded to our CJIS compliant
evidence software​.
These safeguards reduce unnecessary observation and help
protect community privacy.​
