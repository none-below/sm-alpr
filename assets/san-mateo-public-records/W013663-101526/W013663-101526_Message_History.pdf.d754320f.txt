Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request copies of the following 
records.
As you are aware, the San Mateo Police Department gave a presentation at the City Council's October 5, 2026 
special meeting (Item 1, File ID 26-12644, "San Mateo Police Department Real Time Information Center — 
Presentation"), and the presentation can be seen in the City's video of that meeting. The meeting's PrimeGov 
page lists only the one-page agenda report, which states "Attachments: None." No copy of the presentation has 
been posted there.
1. The presentation displayed during that item, in the electronic format in which the City holds it (for example, 
the PowerPoint .pptx file), including all embedded images, video and audio.
2. Each video or audio file played during the presentation, including any drone (unmanned aircraft system) 
footage, in the electronic format in which the City holds it, whether the file was embedded in the presentation

because a PDF does not preserve embedded video.
If any portion of these records is withheld or redacted, please identify the specific legal authority for each 
withholding and release all reasonably segregable portions.
Electronic delivery through the portal is preferred.
