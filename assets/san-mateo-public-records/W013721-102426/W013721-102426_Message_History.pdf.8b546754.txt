Record(s) Requested: Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I 
request copies of the following records.
As you are aware, the Police Department's Real Time Information Center presentation to the City 
Council on October 5, 2026 (produced under W013663-101526) includes a slide titled "Audit 
Assistance." The slide shows the Flock Safety "Audit Assistance" screen with the tab "Audit Assistance 
(149)," a "Needs Review (149)" filter, the heading "149 Activities," and three listed activities, each with 
the status "Needs Review": "Searches Beyond 30 Day Timeframe" showing "587 total, 31 new" with 
latest activity 4/27/2026, 6:52:17 PM; "Searches Beyond 30 Day Timeframe" showing "13 total, 1 new" 
with latest activity 4/28/2026, 9:01:49 AM; and "Single-User Plate Searches." The screen's own tooltip 
describes the first category as "An identified user is running searches for a plate outside of a 30 day 
window."
Unless an item says otherwise, the period is April 1, 2026 through the date of production, and "the 
Department's Flock organization" means the San Mateo Police Department's account on the Flock 
Safety platform. Flock Safety has stated publicly (August 13, 2026) that Audit Assistance "detects 
abnormal activity and flags it for Administrator review," that it is adding "proactive lockout" under 
which "Flock will automatically suspend access pending administrator review," and that where an 
agency bypasses the case code requirement in an emergency, "every exigent search will be flagged for 
administrator review."
1. The Audit Assistance activity records for the Department's Flock organization: every activity listed 
under the "Needs Review," "Archived," and "All" views, with the fields the system displays (Activity, 
Related Activity, User, Plate, Total Searches, Latest Activity, Status) and any reviewer note, disposition, 
or archive date attached to an activity, including the counts shown on the "Needs Review" and

"Archived" views as they stand on the date of production. If the system exports this list (for example 
CSV or XLS), I request the export; otherwise, a printout or screen capture of each view.
2. For the two activities visible on the slide (the "587 total, 31 new" activity with latest activity 4/27/2026, 
6:52:17 PM, and the "13 total, 1 new" activity with latest activity 4/28/2026, 9:01:49 AM): (a) the activity 
detail record, including the list of searches that make up the activity with each search's date and time, 
offense type, search reason, case number, and the networks or cameras searched; and (b) any record of

3. The record reflecting the date on which Audit Assistance was enabled for the Department's Flock 
organization and the account that enabled it, for example the Flock event log entries (Timestamp, User, 
Event Type, Entity Type, Entity Details) recording that action, the Flock notice to the Department that 
the feature was enabled, or the settings page showing the enablement date; and any record designating 
the personnel responsible for reviewing Audit Assistance activities.
4. Any record documenting review of an Audit Assistance activity, whether as part of the Policy 463.10 
monthly audits or otherwise, including "Audit History" entries, archive actions, notes, and any audit 
memorandum or attachment that refers to an Audit Assistance activity, user, or plate. Policy 463.10 
states: "It is the responsibility of the ALPR Committee Captain or the authorized designee to ensure an 
audit is conducted of ALPR data searches at least monthly for internal ALPR users and quarterly for 
external CA public agency users. The audit shall be documented in the form of an internal department 
memorandum to the Chief of Police. ... After review by the Chief of Police, the memorandum and any 
associated documentation shall be filed and retained." A "no responsive records" response to this item 
would mean the Department has no record of reviewing any of the activities the Flock system flagged for 
review, including the activity showing 587 searches.
5. Any report, flag, alert, or review record generated by the Axon Evidence or Axon Fusus platforms 
identifying atypical or anomalous ALPR search activity (Fleet 3 or any other Axon ALPR data), and any 
record of administrator review of such a flag.
6. Any record of a suspension or lockout of a user's access to the Department's Flock organization 
triggered by the platform's abnormal-activity criteria ("proactive lockout"), including the notice of 
suspension, the administrator review, and the outcome; and any record reflecting whether and when 
proactive lockout was enabled for the Department's Flock organization.
7. Any flag, alert, or review record for a search run against the Department's Flock organization as an 
emergency or exigent search that bypassed the case code, case number, or offense type requirement, and 
any record of administrator review of that flag.
Where a record exists in an electronic format, I request it in that format (Gov. Code § 7922.570). If the 
Department withholds any portion of a record, please mark the withheld portion in place, per field, and 
identify the legal authority for each withholding (Gov. Code § 7922.540).
