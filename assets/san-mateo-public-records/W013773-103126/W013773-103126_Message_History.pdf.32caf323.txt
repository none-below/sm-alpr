Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the following

7922.530; Sierra Club v. Superior Court (2013) 57 Cal.4th 157).
As you are aware, on October 30, 2026 the City closed request W013585-100626 with a production of 30 
files and the statement that "Portions of your request are being withheld pursuant to California 
Government Code section 7927.705 ... This includes attorney-client privileged communications pursuant 
to California Evidence Code section 954." The determination does not identify the records withheld, the 
item of the request they fall under, or their number. Items 1 and 2 of that request asked for 
communications "with attachments."
Please respond to each item separately.
1. The following files, each named as an attachment in an email the City produced under W013585-
100626 and not included in the production, in the form transmitted: (a) "AxonOutpost-Lightpost 
Bundles.pdf", attached to Patrick Newton's February 10, 2026 email "Axon Fixed ALPR-San Mateo 
PD"; (b) "Draft_Agenda_Report_Axon_Outpost_18_Month_Trial.docx", attached to Lt. Riccardi's July

email "Axon AR"; (d) "San Mateo PD Axon council session.pdf", attached to Lt. Riccardi's August 19, 
2026 email "Axon briefing for council". Gov. Code § 7927.500 reaches "preliminary drafts, notes, or 
interagency or intraagency memoranda that are not retained by a public agency in the ordinary course

of business, if the public interest in withholding those records clearly outweighs the public interest in 
disclosure." Each of these files was transmitted by City email and retained there, which is where the City 
located and produced the transmitting message. If the City withholds any of them, please identify the

2. For each record withheld in whole or in part under the October 30, 2026 determination: the record 
identifying it (date, sender, recipients, subject line or title, and the item of W013585 it responds to) and 
the exemption applied to it, including any withholding list, privilege log or review notes prepared in 
making the determination, and the record identifying the persons responsible for it. Requirement: Gov. 
Code § 7922.540(b) provides that "[t]he notification of denial shall set forth the names and titles or 
positions of each person responsible for the denial," and § 7922.540(c) provides that "[a]n agency shall 
justify withholding any record by complying with Section 7922.000." A "no responsive records" 
response to this item would mean the City has no record of which records it withheld or who decided to 
withhold them. If no such record exists, I ask that the City state the date, sender, recipients and subject 
of each withheld record, so that the scope of the withholding can be understood.
3. Any withheld record that was sent to or received from an axon.com address, or that was otherwise 
shared with Axon Enterprise, Inc. or any person outside the City. Evidence Code § 954 protects "a 
confidential communication between client and lawyer," and under Evidence Code § 912(a) the privilege 
"is waived with respect to a communication protected by the privilege if any holder of the privilege, 
without coercion, has disclosed a significant part of the communication or has consented to disclosure 
made by anyone." If the City withholds under § 954 a communication that was sent to or received from 
Axon, please identify the basis on which the privilege applies to that communication.
Where a responsive record contains exempt content, please produce the reasonably segregable non-
exempt portions (Gov. Code § 7922.525(b)). Where a record exists in electronic form, please provide it in 
its native format; for the two .docx files, the native file is requested. If any item yields no records, please
