Good afternoon, City Council (Bcc’d),
The Police Department is exploring additional technology solutions available through Axon
that could support the City’s public safety operations. As part of that exploration, Axon has
offered to provide Council members with an IN PERSON informational briefing and
demonstration of its technology.
The briefing will focus primarily on Axon’s Automated License Plate Recognition (ALPR)
technology, including its Outpost fixed license plate reader and video solution. Axon will also
provide an overview of other public safety technology, including Dedrone airspace security, as
well as information regarding data security and privacy, ethical oversight, potential contract
scope, and financial considerations.
The purpose of this briefing is informational only. It is intended to provide Council members
with an opportunity to learn more about the technology and ask questions directly of Axon
representatives. Participation does not represent a commitment by the City to purchase or
implement any particular product.
To accommodate Council schedules and allow for a more conversational presentation and
Q&A, Axon has proposed several identical one-hour small-group sessions (maximum of two
Council Members per session).
