Subject: Public Records Act request — scheduling, attendance and materials for the Axon presentation to 
Council members (August 1 – October 5, 2026)
Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the following records 
from the City of San Mateo. This request seeks existing records only (Gov. Code § 7922.530; Sierra Club v. 
Superior Court (2013) 57 Cal.4th 157).
As you are aware, the production under W013585-100626 includes Lt. Riccardi's August 19, 2026 email "Axon 
briefing for council" to Chief Barberini, City Manager Khojikian, Laurie Strange and Martin McTaggart. It 
states that Axon "would like to come out to San Mateo to do an in-person executive presentation on the Axon 
technology and ALPR's," attaches "a sample of how we can structure the day with up to two council members 
at a time," and asks for "help with times and dates that work best with council." No reply, scheduling record or 
attachment is in the production.
For August 1, 2026 through October 5, 2026, and for the mailboxes, calendars and text messages of the Chief 
of Police, the City Manager, Laurie Strange, Martin McTaggart, Lt. Riccardi and each member of the City 
Council, including any personal account or device used for City business (City of San Jose v. Superior Court 
(2017) 2 Cal.5th 608):
1. Communications, with attachments, concerning the scheduling, format, attendance, agenda or materials of 
an Axon presentation, briefing or meeting for Council members, including communications with any axon.com 
address. The search may be limited to messages containing "Axon."
2. Calendar entries for any meeting, call or presentation with Axon personnel, showing date, time, location, 
subject and attendees.
3. Any agenda, sign-in sheet, visitor record, slide deck, handout or other material presented to or left with any 
Council member, the City Manager or the Chief by Axon at such a session, including material Axon sent before 
or after it.
4. Communications between Police Department command staff or the City Manager's office and any Council 
member concerning the Axon Outpost proposal (File 26-11633) before its October 5, 2026 hearing, with 
attachments. The search may be limited to messages containing "Axon" or "Outpost."
Where a responsive record contains exempt content, please produce the reasonably segregable non-exempt
