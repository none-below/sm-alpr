Thank you for your interest in public records of the City of San Mateo.  Your request was received by the City 
of San Mateo on 11/9/2026 and given the reference number W013836-110926 for tracking purposes.
Record(s) Requested: As you are aware, on October 5, 2026 the City Council approved Amendment No. 1 
to the City's Master Services and Purchasing Agreement with Axon Enterprise, Inc. (File 26-11633), 
which adds Axon Quote Q-865809-009 as Exhibit D for Axon Outpost license plate reader cameras and 
poles. Section 4.2 of the Outpost Statement of Work in Exhibit D states: "All permit licenses will be in 
the name of the customer."
For January 1, 2026 through the date of the search, I request the following Public Works Department 
records:
1. Each application for an encroachment permit, or any other permit under San Mateo Municipal Code 
Chapter 17.08, for installing Axon Outpost cameras, poles, solar panels, batteries or related equipment, 
whether submitted by the City, the Police Department, Axon Enterprise, Inc., or a contractor working 
for any of them, in any status (including pending, withdrawn or denied), with its attachments, site plans, 
location lists and traffic control plans, and each permit issued. Section 17.08.070 states: "No person shall 
place or use, or cause to be placed or used, any material, machinery or apparatus for building, paving, 
construction, excavation, demolition or other purposes, on any street, sidewalk or public place, without a 
permit having been first obtained from the Superintendent of Streets." A "no responsive records" 
response to this item would mean that, as of the search date, no City permit has been applied for or 
issued for installing any Outpost camera in a City street, sidewalk or public place.
2. Any application for a Caltrans encroachment permit for the same work that the City submitted or 
holds a copy of, with its attachments, and any permit Caltrans issued.
These are discrete permit records, and this request does not ask for an email search. Electronic copies in 
the form held are fine.
