Thank you for the quick reply. I think there may be a misunderstanding about what the request seeks. I am not requesting the grand
jury's report. I have the report, and I agree the Court's website is where it lives. The request is for the responses that
responding agencies filed to that report.
Penal Code section 933(c) provides: "A copy of all responses to grand jury reports shall be placed on file with the clerk of the
public agency and the office of the county clerk, or the mayor when applicable, and shall remain on file in those offices." That
sentence names the office of the County Clerk, and it applies to the responses rather than to the report.
So the record is clear, this message clarifies -- and does not replace or withdraw -- my request of August 18, 2026, which
remains:
1. All responses filed pursuant to Penal Code section 933 to the 2016-2017 San Mateo County Civil Grand Jury report "A Delicate
Balance: Privacy vs. Protection" (filed July 12, 2017) -- the response letters or resolutions of each responding agency, including
the City of San Mateo.
