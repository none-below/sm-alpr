Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the following records from the San Mateo
County Sheriff's Office. This request seeks existing records only (Gov. Code § 7922.530).
As you are aware, the 2025-2026 San Mateo County Civil Grand Jury released its report "Is Our Flock of ALPR Cameras Properly
Tended?" on August 3, 2026, with a response requested from the Sheriff under Penal Code sections 933 and 933.05. This request
covers August 3, 2026 through the date of production — a period of a few weeks — and does not seek the Sheriff's Office's own
internal draft response.
To assist the search: responsive records may use terms such as "grand jury," "civil grand jury," "SMCCGJ," "Properly Tended,"
"ALPR report," "Flock report," "grand jury response," "933," or "933.05." This list is illustrative only; the request is not
limited to records containing these terms. Given the short covered period, an effective alternative search is messages exchanged
with other law enforcement agencies' or the association's email domains during the period, reviewed for reference to the report.
Records requested:
1. Communications — including email, text messages, and written correspondence — between the Sheriff's Office and any other law
enforcement agency, city, town, county department, or the San Mateo County Police Chiefs and Sheriffs Association, referencing the
report or any agency's response to it.
2. Calendar entries, meeting invitations, or agendas for any meeting or call at which the report or responses to it is a listed
topic, and any minutes or notes of such a meeting received or retained by the Sheriff's Office.
3. Any draft response, response template, or model language received by the Sheriff's Office from another agency or association in
connection with responses to the report.
If no records are located for any individual item, please respond to that item separately. For any communication withheld under a
claimed exemption, please produce the segregable date, sender, recipients, and subject per Gov. Code § 7922.525(b). If any portion
is denied, please provide exemption citations per Gov. Code § 7922.540.
Please acknowledge receipt of this request so the record reflects the date received.
