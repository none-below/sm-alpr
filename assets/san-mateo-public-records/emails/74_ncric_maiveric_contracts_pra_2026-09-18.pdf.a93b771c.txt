To the Northern California Regional Intelligence Center:
Pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code § 7920.000 et seq.), I request the following records from the Northern California
Regional Intelligence Center ("NCRIC").
This request seeks existing records only (Gov. Code § 7922.530; Sierra Club v. Superior Court (2013) 57 Cal.4th 157). It is directed to
NCRIC's own contracting and procurement records, and does not seek information contributed to the NCRIC Data Sharing Partnership by any
Member Agency.
Records requested:
1. Maiveric, Inc. All contracts and agreements between NCRIC and Maiveric, Inc., together with all purchase orders, task orders, statements
of work, amendments, renewals, extensions, addenda, exhibits, appendices, schedules, and any terms and conditions incorporated by
reference, for all periods. This item includes any separately executed agreement between NCRIC and Maiveric, Inc., or between NCRIC and an
employee or contractor of Maiveric, Inc., relating to the same work — for example, a nondisclosure, confidentiality, security, or
assignment agreement.
2. OpenLattice, Inc. All contracts and agreements between NCRIC and OpenLattice, Inc., or any successor in interest, relating to the
AstroMetrics application, to automated license plate recognition search and retrieval, or to the associated data-integration work —
together with all purchase orders, task orders, statements of work, amendments, renewals, extensions, addenda, exhibits, appendices,
schedules, and any terms and conditions incorporated by reference, for all periods. This item likewise includes any separately executed
nondisclosure, confidentiality, security, or assignment agreement with that vendor or its personnel relating to the same work.
3. Transition between the two vendors. Any assignment, novation, assumption, consent-to-assignment, or transfer instrument by which work,
obligations, or rights under an agreement identified in item 2 passed to Maiveric, Inc., or to any other entity.
Anticipated exemption and routing issues:
NCRIC MOU § 6 (referral to the Member Agency). The NCRIC Memorandum of Understanding provides at § 6 that "Member Agencies remain the
official custodian of all information contributed to the NCRIC-DSP" and that Public Records Act requests "will be referred to the Member
Agency that is the owner of the requested data." The records requested here are NCRIC's own contracting and procurement records; they are
not information contributed to the NCRIC-DSP by a Member Agency. If any portion of this request is referred to a Member Agency on the basis
of § 6, please identify the Member Agency and the responsive record to which the referral applies.
NCRIC's status under the Act. If NCRIC's position is that it is not a state or local agency subject to the California Public Records Act, I
ask that the written response state that position and identify the legal authority for it, so that this request may be directed
appropriately.
Proprietary or trade secret designations. If any contract term, pricing term, or attachment is withheld as confidential, proprietary, or
trade secret, please identify the authority relied upon, state whether the designation originates with NCRIC or with the vendor, and state
whether the vendor was notified of this request and responded.
Segregability (§ 7922.525(b)). To the extent any portion of a responsive record is properly withheld, please produce the remaining
portions, redacted only as necessary.
If any portion of this request is denied, please: 1. provide a written determination within 10 days as provided by Gov. Code § 7922.535; 2.
provide specific exemption citations as required by Gov. Code § 7922.540; 3. identify the record type and volume withheld, including the
number of pages and the date of each withheld record; and 4. state, item by item, if no responsive records exist for that item, and if a
record once existed but no longer does, state when and why it was destroyed or transferred and identify any record reflecting its
disposition.
I request electronic copies. Thank you.
