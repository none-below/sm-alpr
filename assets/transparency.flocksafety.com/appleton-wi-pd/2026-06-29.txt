OVERVIEW

Appleton WI PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Appleton WI PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Appleton WI PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

POLICIES

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Appleton WI PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.
Flock does use a small percentage of images to train its' machine learning systems. To ensure privacy Flock's terms dictate that those images are stripped of all metadata, location data, and personal identifying information before being used for product improvement.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason and is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.
