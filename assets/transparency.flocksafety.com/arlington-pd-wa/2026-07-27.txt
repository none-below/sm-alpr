Overview

The Arlington Police Department (APD) uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Arlington Police Department utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Arlington Police Department utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to locate missing endangered children/adults, and to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

Policy & Trust

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data used by the Arlington Police Department is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Traffic enforcement, immigration enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion) or personal use.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.
