OVERVIEW

Belton MO PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Belton MO PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Belton MO PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

POLICIES

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Belton MO PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason and is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.

Recent Success Story

Aggravated Assault and Unlawful Discharge of a Firearm

In February of 2023, officers were dispatched to a business where shots had been fired, and the suspects fled the scene after a physical disturbance. The investigation included the use of Flock cameras which identified a vehicle that fled from the scene of the incident. This Flock identification assisted with finding the vehicle and identifying a suspect that was involved in the shooting.



Aggravated Assault and Unlawful Use of a Weapon

In December of 2024, officers were dispatched to a residence on a drive-by shooting. The caller was only able to provide a description of a black minivan missing the right-rear hubcap. Dispatchers entered this description into the Flock system and identified a suspect vehicle leaving the area. An alert went out to officers and surrounding agencies with a full vehicle description and a license plate. The vehicle was located within minutes in a neighboring city and the suspect was safely taken into custody.



Leaving the Scene of an Accident and Driving While Intoxicated

In March of 2026, officers were dispatched to a motor vehicle accident in which a driver left the scene. The victim was able to give a description of the vehicle and driver. Officers, using Flock cameras, were able to locate the suspect vehicle and obtain a license plate. The vehicle was located and a driver identified. During the subsequent investigation, the driver of the suspect vehicle was arrested for driving while intoxicated and found to be armed with a pistol.



Recovered Stolen Auto

In January of 2025, officers received a Flock alert of a stolen motor vehicle out of Independence, MO. Officers responded to check the area and located the stolen vehicle in the parking lot of Ulta Beauty Supply. Officers arrested 3 people in the stolen car, one of whom had a felony warrant out of Kansas and was in possession of stolen credit cards.



Sexual Assault

In December of 2025, officers were dispatched to a business at which a suspect had entered the store and assaulted a female employee by kissing her and groping her sexually. Responding officers were able to get a suspect and vehicle description, to include the license plate. A search of the license plate in Flock indicated a location for the vehicle and officers were able to locate the vehicle and arrest the suspect.



Felony Stealing and Drug Possession

In March of 2026, officers received a Flock notification indicating that a vehicle that was suspected of being involved in a felony stealing had passed a Belton Flock camera. The vehicle had been involved in two felony stealing's from Harbor Freight. Officers located the suspect vehicle and were able to arrest the driver and passenger. The driver of the vehicle was in possession of illegal narcotics. The investigation revealed that the suspects were involved in the two felony stealing's in Belton and had also been suspected of stealing at another Harbor Freight store in another jurisdiction.



Stolen Vehicle, Stealing and Shots Fired

In February of 2026, officers received a Flock notification of a stolen vehicle driving through the city. One officer located the vehicle and attempted to stop it but it failed to yield. At the same time, a call for shots fired was dispatched with a suspect vehicle description given matching the stolen vehicle that had failed to yield. The suspects in the vehicle had been rifling through cars in an attempt to steal items. Officers were able to broadcast information, and another law enforcement agency was able to locate the stolen vehicle and pursued it into Kansas City, Kansas, where they were taken into custody. One of the firearms used in this incident was recovered in the stolen vehicle.



Stolen Vehicle, Shoplifting

In December of 2025, Officers were alerted via Flock that a stolen vehicle had entered the City of Belton on I-49 Highway. Officers located the stolen vehicle in the parking lot of Academy Sports and attempted to stop it. The vehicle fled from the scene, but the investigation revealed that two occupants had been left at Academy Sports. The two occupants were located, and they fled after being contacted by the police officers. Officers pursued the suspects on foot and were able to apprehend a juvenile and an adult that had stolen merchandise from Academy sports. The adult suspect was armed with a pistol. Both occupants were arrested and had charges filed against them.
