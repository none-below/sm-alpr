OVERVIEW

Blue Springs MO PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Blue Springs MO PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Blue Springs MO PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

POLICIES

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Blue Springs MO PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason and is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.

Domestic Assault - Success Story

On 03/01/2026, officers were dispatched to a residence regarding a reported violent domestic assault that had just occurred. The victim advised that the suspect fled the scene in a vehicle and provided the suspects name. Officers utilized the departments computer system to locate a possible license plate number. The license plate was entered into the Flock Safety camera system, which indicated the vehicle traveling northbound on a nearby highway.

Based on investigative information, the suspect was believed to be traveling toward another residence. An officer positioned themselves in a nearby business and waited for the vehicle to pass. Upon locating the vehicle the officer activated emergency lights, and conducted a traffic stop.

The suspect was successfully apprehended without incident. The suspect was held pending the following charges:
" Domestic Assault 3rd Degree
" Domestic Assault 4th Degree
" Endangering the Welfare of a Child
" Harassment 1st Degree
" Property Damage 2nd Degree

Had it not been for the information provided by the Flock Safety System the suspect likely would not have been located as quickly as they were. The successful apprehension provided immediate safety to the juvenile and adult victim.

Ulta Beauty Burglary - Success story

In this case, a suspect burglarized the Ulta Beauty store located in the Adams Dairy Landing shopping center, resulting in a loss exceeding $11,000. Through the use of Flock Safety License Plate Reader (LPR) cameras, officers and detectives were able to identify the suspect vehicle and obtain a license plate number.

As detectives and our crime analyst coordinated with Ulta Beauty Loss Prevention and partner agencies, the investigation ultimately linked the same suspect to 15 additional Ulta Beauty burglaries across six states. The combined total loss from these incidents was approximately $300,000. The investigative lead generated by the Flock LPR cameras was instrumental in identifying the suspect, leading to an arrest and the filing of criminal charges.

Credit Card Skimming Ring - Success story

In January 2024, a citizen reported that a credit card reader at a Hy-Vee gas station pump was hanging by wires. Officers determined that the device was not the original manufacturer-installed equipment, but rather a skimming device placed with the intent to unlawfully capture customers credit card information.

Patrol officers conducted checks at additional gas stations in the area and located several more skimming devices. While reviewing surveillance footage from one of the locations, officers identified a suspect vehicle; however, the license plate was not readable. By utilizing Flock Safety LPR cameras in the area, officers were able to identify the vehicle. This information led to the location of the vehicle and the execution of a search warrant at a related residence, where additional skimming equipment and computers were seized. The suspect was ultimately linked to additional crimes, formally charged, and the case was turned over to federal authorities for prosecution.

The Flock Safety LPR cameras deployed in Blue Springs have also assisted in numerous other investigations, including homicides, missing persons cases, thefts, and hit-and-run incidents. The examples outlined above represent just two instances demonstrating how the Blue Springs Police Department has effectively leveraged Flock Safety LPR technology to identify suspects, support investigations, and enhance public safety within our community.
