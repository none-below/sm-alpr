Overview

Cathedral City CA PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Cathedral City CA PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Cathedral City CA PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

Success Stories

On February 15, 2023, Jaime Tran, used the internet to research locations with a kosher market, planning to shoot someone near a kosher market because he believed there would be Jewish people in the area. Tran drove to Pico-Robertson and shot a Jewish victim wearing a yarmulke as he was leaving religious services at a synagogue. Tran, believing the victim was Jewish, shot him at close range centimeters from his spine, intending to kill him. Tran then fled the scene in his car. The next morning, Feb. 16, 2023, Tran returned to the Pico-Robertson area, intending to shoot another Jewish person. Tran shot a second Jewish victim, also wearing a yarmulke and leaving a synagogue after attending religious services. Tran shot the victim at close range, intending to kill him, as the victim crossed the street. Tran again fled the scene.

Cathedral City Police were alerted the vehicle Tran was driving, was captured on a FLOCK camera entering the city. As a result, officers began searching the city for Tran. While searching, officers were dispatched to a shooting call on East Palm Canyon drive. A subject was seen shooting a rifle in the air. Officers responded and located Tran and were safely able to take him into custody. He was subsequently prosecuted federally and sentenced to 35 years in prison.

CASE# 2602C-2240: On February 17, 2026, Officers responded to a shooting in 30900 block of San Gabriel Circle. Upon arrival, they located a male subject suffering from gunshot wounds. Fire Department personnel arrived on-scene and determined he was deceased. Through the investigation, detectives obtained a description of the vehicle. Utilizing FLOCK cameras, detectives were able to identify the vehicle and identify the suspects. Three suspects were arrested and charged with murder.

CASE# 2508C-1880: On August 13, 2025, Officers responded to a shooting at Whispering Palms Trail & Mission Indian Trail. Upon arrival, they located a 13YO, boy suffering from gunshot wounds. Fire Department personnel arrived and began attending to the victim. However, due to the injuries sustained he was pronounced deceased on-scene. Detectives began investigating, and were provided with a general vehicle description. Utilizing FLOCK cameras, a suspect vehicle was identified. After further investigation, two suspects were arrested and charged with murder.

FLOCK Safety cameras have led to the recovery of numerous stolen vehicles, valued at over $250,000. The system was able to identify the stolen vehicles and helped lead to its recovery and return to the vehicle owner.

Policy & Trust

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Cathedral City CA PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harrassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason and is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.
