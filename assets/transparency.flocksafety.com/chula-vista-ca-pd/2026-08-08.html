Overview

Chula Vista CA PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Chula Vista CA PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Chula Vista CA PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

Community Impact

Featured Success Stories

Recent Success Story

On December 28th, 2025 a 31-year-old victim was shot near 656 E Street in Chula Vista. Officers administered life-saving measures until paramedics transported the victim to a local hospital. Unfortunately, the victim succumbed to his injuries and passed away on January 1st, 2026. With the help of witnesses and available live video footage in the area, investigators developed suspect information and a potential license plate. Using the Flock Safety automated license plate reader system, investigators located the vehicle and confirmed the suspect’s identity. An arrest warrant has been issued but it is believed the suspect has fled to Mexico. https://www.chulavistaca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/30312/639033124624630000

Recent Success Story

On January 11, 2026 Chula Vista PD officers responded to a hit-and-run collision near the intersection of North Greensview Dr. and Park Meadows Rd., where an elderly female pedestrian was found critically injured in the roadway. The driver fled the scene before authorities arrived, and the victim later died at a hospital from her injuries. With very limited suspect information, investigators canvassed the area, interviewed residents, and searched for video evidence. With the limited vehicle description the detectives developed, they turned to Flock where they successfully found the vehicle and license plate. On January 13, 2026, the technology led them to a residence in the city, where they identified and arrested a female suspect believed to be the driver. The suspect is being charged with felony hit-and-run and misdemeanor vehicular manslaughter. https://www.chulavistaca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/30366/639039738315370000

Policy & Trust

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Chula Vista CA PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harrassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason and is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.
