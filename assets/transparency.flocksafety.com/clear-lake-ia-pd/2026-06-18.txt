OVERVIEW

The Clear Lake Police Department leverages Flock Safety technology to enhance the capabilities of the Department in bolstering public safety, locating endangered community members, crime prevention, solving crime, increasing our productivity and the effectiveness of our resources, while recognizing the established privacy rights of the community. In doing so we have made this information available to the public.

POLICIES

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Clear Lake IA PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Traffic enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason and is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.
