Overview

The Des Moines Police Department uses Flock Safety automated license plate reader technology to support criminal investigations and enhance public safety while maintaining appropriate privacy protections. Authorized personnel may conduct searches following a crime and receive real time alerts involving stolen vehicles, missing or endangered persons, qualifying felony or gross misdemeanor warrants, and vehicles connected to felony or gross misdemeanor investigations.

The department’s use of this technology is governed by policy, training, access controls, audits, and data retention requirements. It complies with Washington’s Driver Privacy Act, ESSB 6002, and applicable requirements and guidance from the Washington State Attorney General’s Office.

As part of our commitment to transparency and responsible use, the department has made its policies and usage statistics available below

Prohibited Uses

DMPD Policy 432.7 Restrictions on Collection and Use of ALPR Data

The Department shall not use an automated license plate reader system or ALPR data for:

-Immigration investigations or enforcement, as provided in RCW 10.93.160.
-Investigations involving protected health care services under Chapter 7.115 RCW.
-Tracking or otherwise monitoring activity protected by the state and federal constitutions

The Department shall not collect ALPR data on, immediately surrounding, or providing access to or from:
-Facilities providing protected health care services.
-Facilities conducting immigration matters, as defined in RCW 19.154.020.
-Elementary or secondary schools.
-Places of worship.
-Courts.
-Food banks.

Access Policy

To support the mission of the Des Moines Police Department, approved DMPD personnel may access:

a. Data in any of the following databases maintained by either a federal or Washington State agency:
-The WA State department of licensing
-The state criminal justice information system
-The federal bureau of investigations kidnappings and missing persons list
-The national center for missing and exploited children (NCMEC) list
-The Washington missing persons list

OR

b. License plate numbers or vehicle characteristics that have been entered into a state or local automated license plate reader system database or entered in order to perform a search, upon an officer's determination that the license plate numbers or vehicle characteristics are relevant and material to an investigation of a vehicle that is:
-Stolen
-Associated with a missing or endangered person
-Registered to an individual for whom there is an outstanding felony or gross misdemeanor warrant
-Related to or involved in a felony or gross misdemeanor criminal investigation
