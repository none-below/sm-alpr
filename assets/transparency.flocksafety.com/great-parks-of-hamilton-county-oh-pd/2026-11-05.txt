Overview

Great Parks of Hamilton County, Ohio, uses Flock Safety License Plate Readers (LPR's) to capture objective evidence without compromising individual privacy. Great Parks of Hamilton County, Ohio, utilizes retroactive searches to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Great Parks of Hamilton County, Ohio, uses real-time vehicle alerts through system notifications for active police investigations, wanted persons, missing persons, and other safety alerts. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the policies and usage statistics below available to the public.

Policy & Trust

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Great Parks of Hamilton County OH PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harrassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason and is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist Policy

System notifications involving active police investigations, missing persons, or safety alerts are required to be human-verified prior to action.
