Overview

Huron OH PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Huron OH PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Huron OH PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.
