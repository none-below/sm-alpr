Overview

The Johnson City TN PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. The Johnson City TN PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, the Johnson City TN PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

Recent Success Stories

On September 16, 2026, investigators utilized Flock LPR technology to identify a suspect traveling into Johnson City with illegal drugs. A traffic stop was conducted on the car and fentanyl was recovered. The traffic stop also led to the location of a subject wanted on a felony violation of parole warrant.

On September 11, 2026, Flock LPR technology was used to locate a vehicle driven by a suspect with felony drug warrants. After using LPRs to locate the vehicle, a traffic stop was conducted and the suspect was taken into custody.

On September 9, 2026, investigators utilized Flock LPR technology to identify a vehicle driven by a drug trafficker, responsible for importing illegal drugs to Johnson City. Investigators made contact with the suspect and recovered $6,500 that was the proceeds of drug trafficking. Also recovered was over $1,000 in clothing that was stolen from area retailers and traded for illegal drugs. Two suspects in the vehicle were arrested.

Policy & Trust

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Johnson City TN PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason and is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.
