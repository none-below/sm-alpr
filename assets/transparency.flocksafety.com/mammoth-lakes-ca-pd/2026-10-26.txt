Overview

Mammoth Lakes CA PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Mammoth Lakes CA PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Mammoth Lakes CA PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

What's Detected

What's Not Detected

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Mammoth Lakes CA PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason and is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.
