Overview

Monterey Park CA PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Monterey Park CA PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Monterey Park CA PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

Community Impact
Featured Success Stories

Success Story - Monterey Park Mass Shooting Suspect (2023)

Following the Monterey Park mass shooting, ALPR technology provided investigators with critical investigative leads by helping identify and track the suspect vehicle. The information assisted law enforcement in locating the vehicle in a neighboring jurisdiction and supported the rapid coordination of the regional homicide investigation, demonstrating the value of ALPR technology in solving major violent crimes.


Success Story - Monterey Park Off-Duty Officer Homicide (2022)

An off duty Monterey Park Police officer was fatally shot during an attempted carjacking by three suspects. Within hours of the homicide, investigators used Automated License Plate Reader (ALPR) data to locate the suspect vehicle in a neighboring jurisdiction. The ALPR information provided critical investigative leads that helped detectives identify, locate, and arrest the suspects. This case demonstrates how ALPR technology can rapidly generate investigative leads and assist law enforcement in solving violent crimes while working in conjunction with traditional investigative methods.


Success Story – Burglary/Stalking Investigation (2026)

A disgruntled ex-boyfriend began to stalk and harass his ex-girlfriend. The suspect’s erratic behavior escalated, which eventually led him to burglarize the victim’s home. The victim and her children no longer felt safe and temporarily left their home. Using ALPR, detectives were able to build a case to place the suspect in the crime scene during the commission of the crime. The invaluable investigative lead that the ALPR provided eventually led to the apprehension of the suspect.


Success Story – Robbery Investigation (2026)

A 90-year-old woman was walking home when 3 suspects approached her and began stealing items from her person. The suspects used force to take items from the victim, and the victim was in fear for her life. Video footage depicted the suspect vehicle having a distinct white sticker on the rear window of the vehicle. Using ALPR, detectives located the vehicle with the identical sticker. Using this lead, detectives were later able to identify the suspect vehicle license plate and eventually the suspects themselves. The suspects were later arrested for robbery.


Success Story - Critical Missing Person Investigation (2026)

An elderly male with dementia was reported missing by his wife. The elderly male left his residence in his vehicle during early morning hours to get fast food and never returned home. The missing person's license plate was entered into ALPR, and the missing person’s vehicle was detected by ALPR cameras in Riverside County. With the use of ALPR, we were able to continue to monitor the missing person’s vehicle whereabouts. Officers eventually located the elderly missing person in the Long Beach area. The elderly male said he had gotten lost and forgot how to get back home. The use of ALPR cameras allowed law enforcement to locate the missing person and reunite him with his family.


Success Story – Auto Burglary Investigation (2026)

Over the span of one month, at least 7 different vehicles were broken into, by the same suspects, at 3 different locations in Monterey Park. Using ALPR, detectives discovered the suspect vehicle’s pattern of travel, as well as the dates and times of the crimes. ALPR helped establish a path of travel on Thursdays, from Northern California to Monterey Park. Utilizing ALPR, it was discovered that the suspect vehicle was once again traveling southbound from Northern California, and entering Monterey Park at nighttime hours. Suspects were once again captured on surveillance cameras breaking into vehicles. ALPR assisted officers in locating the suspect vehicle that night, and 3 suspects were taken into custody and a total of 16 Felony counts were filed between the 3 suspects.


Success Story - Assault with a Deadly Weapon Investigation (2025)

MPPD officers received multiple calls for service regarding a male shooting at passing vehicles. Further investigation revealed that the suspect and his girlfriend were engaged in a verbal argument. As the suspect and his girlfriend argued, the suspect retrieved a firearm and shot at a passing vehicle, striking an uninvolved victim’s driver's side door. The suspect then entered the vehicle with his girlfriend and fled the area. With the assistance of ALPR, the vehicle was identified, which provided an investigative lead to identify the suspect who committed the shooting. The suspect was later arrested, convicted, and sentenced to 18 years in prison.

Success Story - Theft Investigation (2026)

A male suspect opened the door of an unlocked vehicle and stole items including money, IDs, and bank cards. The suspect is later captured on camera using the victim’s cards at a nearby gas station. Video surveillance captured a white pickup truck parked next to the victim's vehicle at the time of the incident. Using ALPR, detectives identified the suspect vehicle's license plate, leading to the suspect's arrest. Multiple Felony counts were filed against the suspect, and stolen items were returned to the victim.

Success Story - Auto Burglary Investigation (2026)

A suspect shattered the window of a locked vehicle and stole over 1,000 dollars in tools. The suspect’s pickup truck and a description of the suspect were captured on camera. The suspect's pickup truck had a bed cover and unique airbrushed graphics on the sides. Using ALPR, detectives identified the suspect vehicle with an identical airbrushed paint job. Based on this, detectives were able to generate a lead, which later revealed the suspect's identity. The vehicle was later located with the male suspect driving it, leading to his arrest.

Success Story - Criminal Threats Investigation (2026)

A juvenile was walking to school and was threatened by a convicted felon. The juvenile was able to identify the vehicle used and provided additional identifying information. By reviewing ALPR data, detectives were able to identify the suspect’s vehicle and track its movements across the city. The camera hits allowed investigators to establish a timeline, pinpoint the suspect’s likely travel patterns, and determine his current location. Armed with this information, officers coordinated a safe approach and ultimately apprehended the individual without incident. Thanks to the timely use of automated license plate cameras, investigators were able to quickly locate the suspect, prevent further harm, and bring a dangerous individual into custody.

Success Story - Lewd Acts with a Minor Investigation (2026)

An adult male was accused of committing lewd acts with a child, then threatened to take his own life and fled the scene with firearms. Immediately after the suspect fled, officers entered the vehicle’s license plate information into the ALPR system. Within minutes, the cameras detected the suspect’s vehicle traveling across multiple jurisdictions. By monitoring the vehicle’s movement in real time, detectives were able to track the suspect’s direction of travel and identify a predictable pattern. Working collaboratively with patrol units in the corresponding areas, detectives coordinated a safe and strategic containment. The suspect was taken into custody without incident. This case highlights how ALPR technology significantly enhances investigative capabilities, accelerates suspect location efforts, and improves public safety outcomes.

Success Story - Shooting at an Inhabited Dwelling Investigation (2026)

Officers responded to a report of shots fired at an occupied residence. Surveillance footage captured a vehicle driving past the location at the time the shots were fired; however, the license plate was not visible. During the follow-up investigation, detectives used ALPR data to identify vehicles matching the description in the area at the time of the shooting. Through additional analysis, detectives located the vehicle, identified the responsible party, and later arrested the suspect.

Success Story - Auto Burglary and Identify Theft Investigation (2025)

Patrol officers contacted a suspicious vehicle and discovered burglary tools, stolen personal identifying information, and evidence of identity theft. The suspect was arrested for those offenses. During the follow-up investigation, detectives used ALPR data to link the vehicle to a separate recent vehicle burglary in Monterey Park. Additional property stolen during that burglary was later located inside the suspect vehicle.

Success Story - Chain Snatch Robbery Investigation (2026)

An elderly male was targeted in the grocery store by 2 male suspects and 1 male suspect getaway driver. The suspects approached the victim and forcefully removed his gold chain worth approximately $5,000. The suspects entered a distinct vehicle, and using ALPR data, a license plate for the suspect vehicle was obtained. After further investigation, it was revealed that these suspects were involved in approximately 10 other robberies in Los Angeles County, using multiple vehicles. The use of ALPR assisted in identifying all the suspect vehicles involved in this string of robberies, which later resulted in 4 arrests and multiple felony counts filed.

Success Story - Commercial Burglary Investigation (2025)

The suspect entered Home Depot and stole merchandise worth over $950. Detectives used ALPR data to determine that the suspect vehicle was at the Marketplace and observed the stolen property inside. Further investigation revealed the suspect was involved in more than 15 Home Depot theft cases. The suspect was identified and arrested, and the case was filed with the DA’s Office's Organized Crime Division for commercial burglary.

Success Story - Grand Theft Investigation (2025)

A grand theft occurred at the Guitar Center—the theft of a guitar worth more than $950. The employee photographed the suspect vehicle's license plate and stated the same suspect had shoplifted in 2024. Using ALPR data, detectives were able to confirm that the suspect vehicle entered/exited the Marketplace and his place of residence based on his route of travel. With the help of the suspect’s parents, the suspect turned himself in to MPPD and confessed to all crimes. Multiple counts of felony theft were filed.

Success Story - Shoplifting Investigation (2025)

The suspect entered Costco and stole merchandise worth under $950. An employee photographed the suspect vehicle's license plate, and detectives were able to confirm the suspect vehicle entered/exited the Marketplace. Further investigation revealed the suspect was on probation for theft in Orange County and was ultimately arrested at her probation appointment. The DA filed the case, and the suspect was sentenced to 16 months in state prison based on her numerous theft priors.

Policy & Trust

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Monterey Park CA PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harrassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason and is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.
