OVERVIEW

The Real Time Crime Center (RTCC) at The Murfreesboro Police Department (MPD) utilizes
a variety of advanced technologies, including Automated License Plate Readers
(ALPRs), as a means of providing both real-time assistance to officers during active
incidents and investigative support to detectives during criminal investigations. The MPD
ALPR network also serves as an additional layer of protection for both Murfreesboro
residents and visitors by assisting in identifying vehicles connected to crimes and
increasing solvability.

ALPRs capture images of vehicles, which are stored securely for only 30 days, after
which they are permanently deleted. Importantly, ALPRs are not used as an automated
traffic enforcement tool, nor does the LPR system access personal information. ALPR
technology does not identify drivers or passengers, nor does it directly access any
license plate databases, ensuring that no personal data can be retrieved from ALPR
systems. Instead, it alerts RTCC analysts, MPD officers, and dispatchers when vehicles
associated with criminal activity appear on a hotlist, such as those generated by the
National Crime Information Center (NCIC). These hotlists include wanted persons,
missing people and children, stolen plates, stolen vehicles, and other vehicles directly
connected to criminal investigations. ALPRs are also utilized by the MPD RTCC to
safeguard the community by addressing various crimes including homicide, kidnapping,
robbery, aggravated assault, burglary, and motor vehicle theft.

These systems are also designed to foster collaboration with the community. Local
neighborhoods and businesses can partner with MPD and the RTCC, integrating
their systems with MPD to contribute to a safer community. To be transparent and
ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, MPD has made the following policies
and usage information available to the public.

POLICIES

What's Detected by ALPR

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected by ALPR

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Murfreesboro TN PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses of ALPR

Immigration enforcement, automated traffic enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid search reason and is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.
