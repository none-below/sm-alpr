Overview

The North Kingstown Police Department (NKPD) uses Flock Safety's Automated License Plate Reader (ALPR) technology as an investigative tool to assist in solving crimes and locating wanted or missing persons while respecting individual privacy. The system is used primarily to conduct retroactive searches after crimes have occurred and to receive real-time alerts when a vehicle associated with a criminal investigation or other authorized law enforcement purpose is detected.

To promote transparency and accountability, the NKPD has made its policies governing the use of this technology, along with system usage statistics, available to the public. These safeguards are intended to ensure the technology is used appropriately, consistently, and in accordance with applicable laws and departmental policy.

Policy & Trust

What's Detected

The system captures license plates and observable vehicle characteristics, such as make, model, color, body style, and other distinguishing features. These characteristics are determined from images of the vehicle - not from information associated with the vehicle's registered owner or occupants.

What's Not Detected

Faces or facial recognition, the identity of drivers or passengers, registered owner of the vehicle, gender, race, or other personal characteristics.

Acceptable Use Policy

The data may only be used for legitimate law enforcement purposes in accordance with department policy. NKPD owns its data and does not sell it to third parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, routine traffic enforcement, town ordinance violations, personal use, harassment or intimidation, or discriminatory enforcement based on a protected characteristic (such as race, gender, religion, or ethnicity).

All searches are automatically logged and subject to supervisory review. Unauthorized access or misuse of the system may result in disciplinary action and criminal penalties where applicable.

Access Policy

Every search requires a documented offense type or law enforcement reason, which is logged for auditing and accountability.

Hotlist Policy

Every hotlist alert is reviewed and verified by an officer before any enforcement action is taken.
