Overview

The Pacifica Police Department uses Flock Safety's Operating System to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Pacifica PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Pacifica PD utilizes real-time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to be open with the public to demonstrate our proper usage and communicate what guardrails are in place, we have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

Policy & Trust

What's Detected

License Plates and vehicle characteristics (make, color, body type), with the date, time and camera location

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, video, audio, speed, registered owner information, gender, race. The cameras are not designed to capture or record people.

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for official law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by the City of Pacifica, is never sold, and is shared only with approved public law enforcement agencies in the nine Bay Area counties.

Prohibited Uses

General surveillance, immigration enforcement, enforcement related to lawful reproductive health care, traffic enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), personal use, searches on behalf of unauthorized parties.

Access Policy

Every search requires a case number and one of 42 listed offense types. All searches are logged, audited each quarter, and the audits are published.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist alerts are investigative leads only. Officers confirm the plate image and the hotlist record before taking enforcement action.
