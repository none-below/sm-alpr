OVERVIEW

Pflugerville TX PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Pflugerville TX PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Pflugerville TX PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

POLICIES

In Texas, license plates are not subject to Open Records Requests.

ALPR Policy

https://public.powerdms.com/CityofPflugerville/documents/2167841

What's Detected

License Plates, Image of the Rear of Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Pflugerville TX PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, speed enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), personal use.

Access Policy

All system access is controlled by administrators and requires a valid reason which is stored indefinitely, for audit purposes.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be officer verified prior to action.
*Hotlist refers to alerts related to stolen vehicles, warrants or Amber Alerts.
