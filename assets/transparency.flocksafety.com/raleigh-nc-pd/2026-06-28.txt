OVERVIEW

Raleigh NC PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Raleigh NC PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Raleigh NC PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

POLICIES

What's Detected

License Plates, Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Raleigh NC PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason and this access record is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.
