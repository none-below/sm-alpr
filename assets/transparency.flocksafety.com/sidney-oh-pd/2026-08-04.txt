Overview

Sidney OH PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Sidney OH PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Sidney OH PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

What's Not Detected

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Sidney OH PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason and is stored indefinitely.
