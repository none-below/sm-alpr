Overview

Sparks Police Department uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Sparks Police Department utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Sparks Police Department utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

Policy & Trust

What's Detected

License Plates, Stolen License Plates, Stolen Vehicles, Warrant Related Vehicles

What's Not Detected

Facial recognition, People, Gender, Race

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Sparks NV PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason and is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action being taken by officers.
