Overview

Tracy CA PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Tracy CA PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Tracy CA PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

San Jose Sideshow Vehicle Recovered

Tracy PD Teamwork Leads to Recovery of Vehicle

On September 15, 2026, a victim called to report that his truck and trailer had been stolen while he was shopping at Home Depot. The victim observed the vehicle being driven away from the store and contacted Tracy PD. The suspects were arrested and booked into county jail for the theft of the vehicle and multiple warrants out of Manteca.

Road Rage Homicide

August 4, 2023, Tracy PD officers responded to multiple calls of shots being fired at the Am Pm located and N. Tracy Blvd and Clover Rd. Multiple witnesses advised that a large white work van had been the responsible vehicle for the shooting. A witness was able to provide photos of the actual suspect vehicle as he had witnessed the road rage incident leading up to the shooting. Utilizing Flock ALPR cameras, officers were able to quickly confirm and identify the suspect vehicle. Officers were able to locate the suspect vehicle attempting to flee Eastbound on Highway 205 within 15 minutes of the initial call for service. Two individuals were arrested without incident and have since been convicted.

Felony Vehicle Alert leads to Arrest

On February 12, 2023, a Flock alert on a Felony vehicle out of Lathrop PD was broadcasted to Tracy PD officers. The alert indicated that driver was wanted for false imprisonment, brandishing a knife and making criminal threats. The suspect was believed to be armed with a knife and had threatened suicide by cop. Tracy PD officers located the vehicle and took the suspect into custody without incident.

Pursuit Leads to Location and Safe Return of Kidnapping Victims

On January 31, 2023, Tracy PD officers were alerted via Flock to a felony armed and dangerous vehicle involved in a shooting and carjacking in Santa Clara. Officers located the vehicle and were lead on a pursuit throughout the city. The pursuit ended after a successful pit maneuver. Two juveniles were located in the vehicle unharmed. The suspect was taken into custody on several felony charges to include; Attempted Murder, Assault with a Deadly Weapon, Kidnapping, Carjacking and Shooting at an Inhabited Dwelling.

What's Not Detected

Acceptable Use Policy

Data is used for law enforcement purposes only. Data is owned by Tracy CA PD and is never sold to 3rd parties.

Prohibited Uses

Immigration enforcement, traffic enforcement, harrassment or intimidation, usage based solely on a protected class (i.e. race, sex, religion), Personal use.

Access Policy

All system access requires a valid reason and is stored indefinitely.

Hotlist Policy

Hotlist hits are required to be human verified prior to action.

Myth vs. Fact
Myth

Flock has been hacked.

Fact

Flock's cloud environment has never been breached. Flock invests in a strong security posture, including adhering to NDAA, SOC2 (Type II), ISO 27001, ISO 27018 FedRAMP 20x, and Higher Education Community Vendor Assessment Tool (HECVAT) compliance. Additional security precautions include Role-Based Access Controls, data encrypted at-rest and in transit, required Multi-Factor Authentication, and regular penetration testing.

Myth

Flock sells and/or shares customer data.

Fact

Your community owns 100% of your data and it will never be sold. Only your agency decides who to share your data with, not Flock. Flock Safety does not publicly release agency search data. Flock does not operate or support any public-facing searchable database of law enforcement lookups, and we do not provide license plate search histories to websites or third parties without the agency's knowledge and authorization. Other agencies who you share footage with have been responding to FOIA requests and sharing this search data.

Myth

My data is fed into a national database.

Fact

Each agency controls access to its own data. Agencies may choose to share information through a broader network, but nothing is shared automatically. Participation is opt-in, governed by defined agreements, and limited to lawful investigative purposes.

Myth

Flock cameras use facial recognition.

Fact

Flock cameras do not use facial recognition technology. Searches are based on vehicle characteristics, and not identity. Flock cannot recognize, identify, or track individuals. It is purpose built for specific public safety investigations, not to monitor people.
