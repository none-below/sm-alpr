Overview

Webster Groves MO PD uses Flock Safety technology to capture objective evidence without compromising on individual privacy. Webster Groves MO PD utilizes retroactive search to solve crimes after they've occurred. Additionally, Webster Groves MO PD utilizes real time alerting of hotlist vehicles to capture wanted criminals. In an effort to ensure proper usage and guardrails are in place, they have made the below policies and usage statistics available to the public.

In 2025, during a homicide investigation, Webster Groves detectives utilized Flock camera technology to track the suspects movements. The information obtained through Flock was instrumental in supporting the filing of charges against the suspect.

Recent Success Story

In 2025, while investigating a series of auto burglaries, Webster Groves detectives used Flock camera technology to track the suspect vehicles movements. The information obtained through Flock was critical in clearing these cases and led to the suspects being linked to numerous additional crimes across the region, including weapons offenses, armed robbery, and homicide. This investigation also demonstrated the value of Flock in enabling seamless collaboration among multiple jurisdictions on cases that cross municipal boundaries.
