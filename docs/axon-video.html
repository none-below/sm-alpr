10:44 Why it’s a bad deal, and who voted for it

Rushed: approved before any town hall, while still bound to Flock until Aug 2028; Axon’s deadline had already moved twice. Locked in: 98 months, to 2035; the penalty-free exit closes 10 months in. Not free: charged from Nov 15, 2026, installed or not; the first bill comes in June 2028. Not a better price: Axon’s usual discount is 20% off list (Englewood’s presenter above: “20 off every year or … your first year free”); most agencies got at least that; San Mateo got 17% or less.

Oct 5, 2026 roll call: Yes — Deputy Mayor Nicole Fernandez, Council Members Danielle Cwirko-Godycki, Lisa Diaz Nash and Robert Newsom Jr. No — Mayor Adam Loraine, who had moved to wait a month for a town hall.
